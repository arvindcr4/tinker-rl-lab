## Strongest defensible original claim

The strongest current original claim is **decision insufficiency of terminal homogeneity**, in its deliberately restricted form:

> For the two actions {stop, commit to `m` prepaid batch rollouts}, the same finite all-failure history is compatible with `p=0` and `p=q>0`; when `Delta=B[1-(1-q)^m]-cm>0`, the optimal action reverses and every outcome-only randomized rule has positive two-point minimax regret `cm*Delta/(cm+Delta)`.

This is fully proved in F-MAIN Proposition 3 and needs no missing empirical cell. Its allowed implication is that ZVF or an all-equal terminal reward vector cannot, by itself, prescribe retry/drop/resample. It does **not** establish a useful controller, trace value, sample-efficiency gain, or learning effect.

## Best-supported manuscript spine

Use **F-MAIN, _Same Terminal Signal, Different Action: Fail-Closed Conformance and Decision Insufficiency in Group-Relative RL_**, as the single canonical spine:

1. Problem: homogeneous terminal rewards certify absent within-group contrast but do not identify cause or action.
2. Theory: first-contrast accounting, the all-`G` rare-outcome rollout lower bound, and the restricted action-reversal/minimax-regret witness.
3. Method: fail-closed conformance from objective equation to gradient relation, controller output, and immutable run receipt.
4. Evidence: S1 as implementation-conformance evidence; r4-2 as a source-bound registered failure/postmortem with descriptive receipt-field near-collinearity, a `69/100 < 95/100` negative-control gate failure, and an infeasible positive-control construction.
5. Non-claims: no causal training effect, framework ranking, controller benefit, RLM result, equivalence, or end-to-end mitigation.

Do not merge the spectral auxiliary proposal, E1, or next-submission preflights into this paper. E1's aggregate, generated table, manuscript, and compiled PDF now consistently report all four comparisons as `INCONCLUSIVE`; it remains a separate same-stack reproducibility paper and still needs the stale 39/40 campaign-state contradiction reconciled plus reviewer-visible anonymized evidence.
