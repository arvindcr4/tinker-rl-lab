Signal to noise
The noise values allows us to interpret reported results. 
To get a sense of the quality of the benchmark, we need 
signal to noise ratio.
Signal: the gain from doubling the size of model in each 
series
Noise: implied standard deviation by statistical testing 
(same as bootstrapping)
sig/noise = |A-B| / SE(A-B)
sig/noise < 2: cannot reliably measure the improvement 
from doubling model size. True on most code 
benchmarks! examples of inversion
Simple multiple choice benchmarks have much better 
signal to noise ratio than complex generation 
benchmarks
