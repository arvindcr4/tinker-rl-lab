39
Common Failure Modes
•
Long horizon reasoning and planning:
-
Models oscillate between two webpages, or get stuck in a loop
-
Correctly performing tasks but undoing them
-
Agents tend to stop exploration / execution too early
•
Failures in visual processing
-
Clicking the wrong item
-
Identifying specific items in complex webpages
-
Spatial reasoning (“what are the prices of products in the first row?”)

40
What is Missing?
•
We need to do a lot more to close the gap:
-
Reasoning and Planning over long horizons
-
Allow agent to Search, execute and coordinate multiple instances in parallel and 
ask for clarifications/confirmations
-
Strong vision-language-code models
-
Identifying the appropriate level of abstraction for agents (HTML/screenshots/
APIs)
-
Multimodal models: Many real-world tasks require visual grounding to 
effectively solve (e.g., every task involving PowerPoint, Excel, Photoshop). To 
develop strong general agents, we will need to train and build strong vision-
language models.
