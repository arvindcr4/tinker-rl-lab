## Cross-paper coupling

1. **Iter-7 row-13 N10 zvf→acc** — iter 7 reported steady-state zvf
   predicts held-out accuracy r=0.607 [0.408, 0.779] across seeds.
   Iter 133 confirms at per-channel level: on N10 zvf is seed-dominated
   (η²_seed=0.10), not step-band-dominated.
2. **Iter-89 row-106 N2 algorithm-axis η²** — iter 89 measured same η²
   helper on N2 algorithm axis. Iter 133 uses identical paired-seed
   cluster-bootstrap.
3. **Iter-93 row-109 mega-98 stack-axis η²** — iter 93 measured stack-axis
   η² on mega-98 cells. Iter 133 seeds+runs the same script template on
   the n10 corpus.
4. **Iter-101 row-116 zvf130 risk-index η²** — iter 101 measured η²
   (algo-axis, zvf_risk). Iter 133 measures η²(seed/band, zvf) on the
   lighter-weight step-log level.
5. **Iter-119 row-132 / iter-125 row-138 / iter-131 row-146 controllers** —
   iter 133 H4 provides an explicit per-corpus eligibility check
   (η²_step_band_zvf ≥ 0.10) that the controllers implicitly assume.
   N10 panel fails this gate ⇒ iter-131/iter-119 results do not transfer
   without re-validation.
6. **Iter-125 row-138 chained-R** — iter-125 R≥4 on zvf is
   corpus-shape-dependent (mega-98 multi-stack); N10 single-stack reverts
   zvf R<1. The two corpora are TWO REGIMES, not one. Reward and mean_len
   carry the cross-corpus invariant (both regimes show band-curriculum
   dominance); zvf/stack is corpus-shaping-dependent.
7. **FRONTIER-INSIGHTS Round 1 (Critic-Degeneracy Hypothesis)** — the
   iter-133 seed-domination on zvf is consistent with the FRONTIER
   synthesis observation that zvf is observed-signal-availability
   (driven by seed-level sampling noise) rather than latent-difficulty.

## Operational recommendation

(a) **Cross-corpus P5 claims must specify which axes are swept**
in each corpus. Reporting $R$ without naming the axes is misleading.

(b) **Report BOTH** absolute η² per axis per channel (corpus-comparable)
AND relative $R$ (corpus-shape-relative). The two convey different
invariants.

(c) **P7 controllers should adopt η²(step_band, zvf) ≥ 0.10** as a
per-corpus eligibility gate before applying step-level zvf-based
firing rules. The iter-131 / iter-119 controllers implicitly assumed
this gate; iter 133 makes it explicit.
