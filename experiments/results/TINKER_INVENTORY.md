## What the API exposes

Per training run: `training_run_id`, `base_model`, `model_owner`, `is_lora`, `lora_rank`, `corrupted`, `last_request_time`, plus the most recent checkpoint (`last_checkpoint` / `last_sampler_checkpoint`).

Per checkpoint: `checkpoint_id`, `checkpoint_type`, `time`, `tinker_path`, `size_bytes`, `public`, `expires_at`.

**Important:** the Tinker REST API/SDK does **not** expose training configuration metadata such as algorithm, dataset, group size, learning rate, or total steps. `user_metadata` is always `null` for historical runs. Config-space coverage therefore cannot be reconstructed from Tinker alone; merge with W&B for that.
