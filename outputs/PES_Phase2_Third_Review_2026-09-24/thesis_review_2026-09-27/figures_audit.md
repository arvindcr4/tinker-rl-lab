# Figure audit — Thesis_Report_ArvindCR.pdf (209 pp)

Date: 2026-09-27. Scope: all 24 numbered figures (`\fig{...}` in thesis_master.tex; sources in `thesis/figures/*.tex`, built by compile_figures.py). Method: pymupdf render of every figure page at 110 dpi plus 260 dpi crops; text-span font sizes read from the thesis PDF; numbers checked against thesis_master.tex and figure sources; greyscale render of the colour-dependent figures; WCAG contrast/L* worked out for the shared palette. No existing files were modified.

Severity: **Critical** = a wrong number or a claim the figure contradicts. **Major** = misleads, or an examiner will likely flag it. **Minor** = polish.
