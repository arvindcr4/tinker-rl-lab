## What was verified as correct

- **Table 9.A (ch09:94–109).** Recomputing every Wilson interval from `result.json` numerators and denominators reproduces the published values exactly for all 13 count-based rows.
- **Table 9.B (ch09:113–131).** Recomputing every McNemar exact p-value and every b/c pair from `paired.json` per-item data reproduces all 14 binary rows. The E10 harm-score and benign-score bootstraps also reproduce: [−0.053, 0.075] and [−0.049, 0.012].
- **Error and timeout denominators in the small-scale runs are handled correctly.** E4 (1 error), E5 (1 error) and E7 (1 error, 5 of 6 scored) all keep the failed item in the denominator. E9 counts invalid submissions as no medal. E1-original keeps its 14 generation failures and 4 lost artifacts in the 731 (713+14+4=731).
- **Campaign component sums.** E8's per-category correct counts sum to 450 and its denominators to 1,967. E11's per-framing log verdicts, recounted from `e11_full_receipt.json`, give 67/156 + 62/156 = 129/312.
- **Power numbers.** The MDE values are d=2.024 for 5 vs 5 seeds and d=1.530 for 5 vs 15 (ch04:24).
- **Correlations.** r=−0.769 (n=15) gives p=0.0008; r=0.40 (n=19) gives p=0.09; r=0.13 (n=7) gives p=0.78 (ch04:61).
- **Eight-seed audit (ch01:29).** Paired-t p-values recompute from `zvf-program/audit/results/full/*.json`: DAPO 0.756, GSPO 0.210, Dr. GRPO 0.673, AERO 0.858. After BH correction all are ≥0.84.
- **Pooled McNemar counts.** The Dr. GRPO GSM8K-CoT pooled McNemar reproduces: 63/26 gives p=1.10e−4 and 53/23 gives p=7.6e−4 (but see H9 for the unit of analysis).
- **P5, P7 and P8 arithmetic.** Coverage ratios, the 1,723/1,867 = 92.3% escalation count, the mean G* of 6.376 (cost ratio 0.797), the 35× cost ratio and the fraud F1 all check out.
