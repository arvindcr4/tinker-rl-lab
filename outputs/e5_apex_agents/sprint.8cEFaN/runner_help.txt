Run the smallest exact APEX-Agents evaluation through Archipelago. This runner
is intentionally fail-closed. It binds the Mercor benchmark and the native
Archipelago grader to immutable revisions, requires one disjoint task, starts
W&B online before constructing a Tinker client, and enforces the E5-specific
$0.50 Tinker ceiling inside the shared $16.50/$1.50 envelope. The benchmark is
evaluation-only. It must never be replaced with xLAM, GSM8K, WebArena,
BrowserGym, or another related task. If the gated dataset, the native
environment, W&B, Tinker, or the runtime cannot be acquired, a machine-
readable ``BLOCKED`` receipt is written and no Tinker call is made.
