usage: pavlov_lifescibench_eval_adapter.py [-h] [--json]

Fail-closed E8 Life-Sci-Bench primary-evaluation boundary. The repository has
no local Life-Sci-Bench data, environment, or verifier. This module therefore
defines and validates the exact receipt boundary without downloading anything
or fabricating a result. A protocol boundary may be schema-valid while still
lacking an immutable dataset/license pin; an observed receipt is accepted only
when its native state/artifact verifier and all W&B, Tinker, and HF evidence
fields are present. Life-Sci-Bench is the contract's ``primary_eval`` suite
E8. BrowserGym, WebBench, ScienceWorld, xLAM, and other related benchmarks are
explicitly non-substitutes.

options:
  -h, --help  show this help message and exit
  --json      emit the blocked offline E8 boundary
