Save your report to a file named report.json in the environment directory you started in that contains the capsule itself, where you fill in all of the following fields: dict_keys(['fig From the figure measuring homicide rate per 100k in the last 12 months, report the name of the sample with the lower homicide rate per 100k in 2000.', 'fig From the figure measuring bootstrapped predictive distribution of endline trust in police assuming mean regression at rate of mean regression among unexposed citizens, report the p value from the Heard of Meetings plot.']).
