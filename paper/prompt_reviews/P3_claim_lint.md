## 3) Rewrite suggestions for risky claims (semantic-preserving)

1. **A7 (abstract, worst offender — appears 3×):** "…is an implicit all-pairs preference contrast, ~~validated against our measured gradients~~ **consistent with our measured training diagnostics (per-step advantage variance, ZVF, and gradient norms), with the decisive gradient-level test specified but not yet run**, with zero-variance groups projected out." Apply the same correction at p3_results_intro.tex:22–24 ("We validate this identity against measured gradients" → "We show this identity is an exact algebraic rewriting and check its observable signatures against measured training diagnostics") and p3_conclusion.tex:8–9 ("validated on measured gradients for the runs where we could log them" → "checked against logged per-step diagnostics (gradient norms, advantage variance) for the runs where we could log them; a direct gradient-vector validation remains open").
2. **A4/R1/C2:** "near-complete reward retention (≈100.3%…)" → "near-complete **held-out accuracy** retention (≈100.3%…)"; conclusion: "reward is retained almost completely across a wide range of G **on the measured near-ceiling task; at canonical GSM8K scale our illustrative reanalysis instead shows retention falling to ≈73%**."
3. **A3:** "A G=4 versus G=32 sweep shows a large held-out swing" → "A G=4 versus G=32 **token-budget reanalysis (illustrative, reconstructed from ablation logs)** shows a large held-out swing".
4. **A1:** "70+ RL runs" → "95 runs attempted, 61 completed, across two seven-library rosters (two LLM-RL launchers producing completed runs)" — or keep "70+" only if the count includes partial/in-progress runs, and say so.
5. **M13:** Replace the half-sat column with the artifact values 0.59/0.71/0.86/1.21/1.75 M (or regenerate the TSV) — whichever side is stale.
6. **M20:** "Spearman ρ=−1.000 (n=4, p<10⁻²⁴)" → "Spearman ρ=−1.000 (n=4; exact permutation p=0.083 — reported descriptively given only four budget points)".
7. **M15:** Either flip the artifact flag or soften the headline: "the Wu claim holds natively on G=2 vs G=16 **by our retention/TOST criterion (retention 1.0035, CI [0.99, 1.02]); note the artifact's stricter flag disagrees and is being reconciled**".
8. **M16:** "the log-log ZVF fit … slope −0.230/decade" → "the **ZVF-vs-log₁₀G linear fit** has slope −0.230 per decade of G (the log-log slope is −0.137, vs the −0.5 iid ideal)".
9. **R2:** "the gains concentrate where within-group contrast is scarce" → "the gains concentrate in bins where small-G groups frequently lack contrast **but larger G can restore it (frontier/mid difficulty); at full saturation no G recovers contrast and the gains vanish**".
10. **C5:** "we release the sweeps, gradients, and scripts" → "we release the sweeps, per-step gradient-norm logs, and scripts".
