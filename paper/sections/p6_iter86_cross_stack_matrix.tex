\subsection{Cross-stack \texorpdfstring{$\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$}{delta-div} matrix (iter 86)}
\label{sec:p6-iter86-cross-stack-matrix}

The registry's per-method \verb|outcomes.zvf_antiherding| block
records four single-axis point estimates
($\mathrm{Y\_obs}$, $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$, $1-\mathrm{ZVF\_obs}$) per
$N\!=\!2$ same-stack method; the iter-66 row 77 paper section summarised
these as the scalar range ``$\delta_{\mathrm{div}} \in [0.039, 0.053]$''.
That summary collapses six pairwise comparisons into four self-references.
This iter computes the full $\binom{4}{2}=6$ pair matrix on every
contrast-yield axis (paired-step bootstrap, $B\!=\!4000$, seed
20260705, $n_{\mathrm{steps}}\!=\!40$) and ranks the methods by
$\mathrm{Y\_obs}$ (contrast-preservation).

\subsubsection{Headline findings}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{H1 --- cross-stack $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$ has only one
      significant pair.} On the $N\!=\!2$ same-stack corpus,
      AREAL $>$ GIFT by $+0.0138$ [$+0.0016,+0.0268$] is the only
      direction that survives the paired-step bootstrap at 95\%.
\item \textbf{H2 --- cross-stack $\mathrm{Y\_obs}$ has 3 significant pairs,
      all of them involving GIFT.} GIFT reduces contrast-yield
      by $5.0$--$6.4$pp relative to the other three same-stack methods;
      GRPO vs AERO are tied at $\mathrm{Y\_obs}=0.2797$ on every step.
\item \textbf{H3 --- GIFT is the only outlier in the contrast-preservation
      ranking.} On any of the three axes (delta\_div, y\_obs, zvf\_obs),
      GIFT is the worst-preservation method; AREAL is the best.
      This contradicts the iter-66 row 77 summary ``all four methods
      share a structural diversity bonus'' because the summary
      averaged over self-references rather than paired differences.
\item \textbf{H4 --- iter-66 row 77's range ``$\delta_{\mathrm{div}}
      \in [0.039, 0.053]$'' survives ONLY as a range statement, NOT as
      a rank-statement.} The four point estimates are all within
      paired-step CI of each other on $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$
      (1/6 SIG), but the cross-stack
      $\mathrm{Y\_obs}$ ranking is sharp (3/6 SIG; GIFT significantly
      worse than the other three). The registry's
      \texttt{outcomes.zvf\_antiherding} block should be read on
      the $\mathrm{Y\_obs}$ axis, not on the $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$
      axis, for cross-stack claims.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

      sorted by $\mathrm{Y\_obs}$ rather than $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$.

\subsubsection{Operational recommendation}

Report the cross-stack contrast-preservation ranking on the
$\mathrm{Y\_obs}$ axis (3/6 SIG pairs), not on $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$
(1/6 SIG pairs). The registry's
\texttt{outcomes.zvf\_antiherding.delta\_div\_mean} field is preserved
on every entry but should be flagged as
\textit{``self-reference vs cross-method-pair: NS on 5/6 pairs''} for
consumers that filter on cross-stack significance.

\texttt{experiments/results/p5p8/p6\_cross\_stack\_delta\_div\_matrix.tsv}.
