\subsection{Joint-controller cost-ratio extension of the iter-70 prediction block}\label{sec:p6-joint-controller-extension}

The iter-70 row 82 block lifts per-(trigger $\times$ threshold) controller
savings into the registry. The iter-72 row 85 joint controller
extends the family to a 2-branch composite (Dualformer-Auto rollout
saves + $\delta_\text{div}$-triage zvf-saved) and reports net-saves
and cost-ratio for $4 \text{ methods} \times 5\, \tau$ values. This
section closes the loop on the iter-72 mint: lift the iter-72 row 85
outputs into the registry as a new additive-optional sub-block.

\subsubsection{Schema extension}

Two new additive-optional sub-blocks (all fields nullable, no
required additions) are added to the registry schema, with
\texttt{additionalProperties: false} preserved:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{controller\_predicted\_savings\_per\_rollout.joint\_controller}
          in the variant-delta record --- a panel name, an array of
          per-method joint predictions, and a CI-method provenance.
    \item \texttt{outcomes.joint\_controller\_predictions} in the
          stack record --- the mirror block, with $G$, $n_\text{steps}$,
          and the prediction list.
\end{itemize}
Six nullable integer fields are added to each prediction item
(\texttt{net\_saves}, \texttt{n\_contrast\_prompts},
\texttt{n\_fired\_steps}, \texttt{n\_zvf\_saved},
\texttt{n\_rollout\_saves}, \texttt{g\_total}) plus one nullable
string (\texttt{source\_iter}).

\subsubsection{Schema validation}

The registry is re-validated after the patch; \textbf{34/34 entries
PASS} ($\texttt{ok}{=}34$, $\texttt{fail}{=}0$, schema-conformance
gate preserved). The patch is purely additive: 7 entries (4 stack +
3 delta) carry the new sub-block; the remaining 27 entries are
unchanged.
