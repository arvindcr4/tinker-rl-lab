\section{Measured-vs-Claimed Variant-Delta Reconciliation}
\label{sec:p6-measured-claimed}

The previous section established that the registry's same-stack reward
deltas are all $\leq 2$pp in magnitude (with the AERO and AREAL
decreases statistically significant). That finding isolates the
reward axis; this section asks the broader question: \emph{for each
named variant delta, does the registry's qualitative claim survive
quantitative scrutiny on every measurable proxy available?}

\subsection{Method}
We enumerate every \texttt{delta\_*.json} record (11 in total) and, for
each, build three evidence bases:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{N2 same-stack four-method tensors} (aero, gift, areal,
    with grpo as baseline) --- paired bootstrap on per-step scalars at
    $n_{\text{boot}}{=}2000$, last-$10$ steps. Five proxies:
    \texttt{zvf}, \texttt{loss}, \texttt{mean\_len}, \texttt{cv\_len},
    \texttt{reward\_mean}.
  \item \textbf{zvf130 5-seed risk index} --- mean ZVF across 5 seeds
    on the repository's canonical prompt distribution, for 9 of the 11
    variants. The variants not covered by this panel are
    \texttt{dapo}, \texttt{drgrpo}, \texttt{gspo}.
  \item \textbf{Registry-side claimant stacks} --- the number and status
    mix of \texttt{stack} records that explicitly claim the delta via
    \texttt{variant\_delta\_applied[*].delta\_id}.
\end{enumerate}
For each \texttt{(delta, proxy)} pair we then assign a verdict by
comparing the measured $\Delta$ to a predicted sign derived from the
delta's \texttt{claim\_summary}:
\textbf{SUPPORT} (sign matches, CI excludes 0), \textbf{WEAK} (sign
matches, CI contains 0), \textbf{OPPOSE} (sign contradicts), or
\textbf{NO\_DATA}.

\subsection{Three falsifiable findings}

\paragraph{Finding A: $8/8$ measured variants have \emph{lower} mean ZVF
than grpo.}
The zvf130 risk index is the only proxy that fires on every measured
variant, and it fires in the same direction on all 8: every variant
drops mean ZVF relative to grpo by between $0.16$ (scafgrpo) and $0.37$
(es / gift). The 5-seed reproducibility of this monotonic drop is the
strongest claim-direction result the registry now licenses: \emph{no
measured variant has lifted the within-group contrast that the variant
claims to lift}. The \texttt{mcgrpo} diversity-bonus and \texttt{aero}
off-policy rollouts are \emph{claimed} to raise within-group contrast;
the canonical prompt distribution says the opposite.

\paragraph{Finding B: $3/11$ variants are claim-only.}
\texttt{dapo}, \texttt{drgrpo}, \texttt{gspo} have no N2 same-stack
measurement and no zvf130 measurement. They are also the variants with
the largest claimant populations ($10$, $4$, $2$ respectively) and,
for DAPO specifically, the widest status mix (4 implemented / 1
surrogate / 3 absent / 2 unknown, per the iter-14 audit). The
adoption-vs-evidence gap is sharpest where the registry is most cited:
the variant the field claims to use most is the one whose measured
effect is least characterised.

\paragraph{Finding C: $1/11$ variants shows an OPPOSE verdict.}
The \texttt{aero} delta's predicted sign for ZVF was $-1$ (inflating
the effective group size should lower zero-variance probability).
Measured N2 reports $\Delta_{\text{ZVF}}{=}+0.025$ (single-seed CI
contains 0); zvf130 reports $\Delta_{\text{ZVF}}{=}-0.261$ (5 seeds).
The N2 single-step measurement points weakly opposite; the
multi-seed measurement points weakly in the predicted direction. This
is the only OPPOSE verdict the reconciliation produces, and the
direction is itself ambiguous.

\subsection{What the reconciliation rules out}
The reconciliation table is \emph{not} a quality judgement on the
variants. It is a machine-readable record of the agreement between the
qualitative claim each delta makes and the measured proxies available.
Three things it rules out:

\begin{itemize}
  \item The reward-axis claim (\emph{``variant $X$ improves reward''})
    is not supported on the N2 same-stack data for any of the 4
    measured variants --- the largest effect is a $2$pp reward
    \emph{decrease} (AERO $-0.014$, AREAL $-0.020$, both CIs exclude 0),
    so no variant improves reward and two significantly lower it.
  \item The within-group-contrast claim (\emph{``variant $X$ raises
    within-group contrast''}) is contradicted by the zvf130 panel for
    every variant that makes such a claim.
  \item The ``registry adoption implies evidence'' inference is
    falsified for DAPO: 10 claimants, 0 measured proxies. The registry
    correctly \emph{catalogs} the adoption; it does not yet
    \emph{justify} it.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implications for downstream papers}
The reconciliation table is consumable by Pillar-3 (P7, controller
design) and Pillar-4 (P8, fraud scorer) only insofar as it sharpens
the ``the algorithm label is under-identified'' thesis (already
established in Pillar 1 / P5). Its direct contribution to Pillar 2
(P6) is that the registry now carries a top-level \emph{claim vs
measurement} check, not just a reporting-coverage check (the iter-14
audit). The two are complementary: coverage answers ``was the claim
even made?''; reconciliation answers ``does the measured behaviour
match it?''
