\subsection{Iter-54: Closing the Registry's Missing-Delta Gap}
\label{sec:p6-missing-deltas}

The iter-50 audit (Section~\ref{sec:p6-registry-health-iter50})
catalogued the registry as \textbf{31/31 entries PASS, 60.9\%
coverage grid}, and explicitly flagged four veins for future iters.
Three were closed by the same iter (CI-style validator, coverage grid,
verdict-signature clustering); the fourth --- ``add new entries for
the missing framework $\times$ method cells'' --- was deferred because
``the entry would require measured data on a new cell that is not
currently in the worktree'' (Section~\ref{sec:p6-registry-health-iter50}).
This subsection
closes the fourth vein: three new $\texttt{delta\_*.json}$ ship this
iter, two of them as honest provenance-only placeholders and the
third as a measured same-stack delta on the worktree's own
$\texttt{qp7\_adaptive.tsv}$ panel.

\paragraph{New entries.}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\texttt{delta\_adaptiveg}$ --- adaptive group-size ladder
(4$\to$6$\to$8 on ZVF$>$0.5, de-escalate on ZVF$<$0.2). The base
stack $\texttt{grpo}$ is the same model, task, sampler, and
RLHF-pipeline as the fixed-G arm; the \emph{only} difference is the
group-size schedule. Provenance is
$\texttt{experiments/results/quick\_20260704/qp7\_adaptive.tsv}$
(arm B adaptive 4$\to$6$\to$8 vs.\ arm A fixed $G=4$, 16 paired
steps). The measured block carries paired bootstrap CIs
($B=2{,}000$, $\text{seed}=20260704$) on two metrics:
$\texttt{reward\_mean}$ and $\texttt{zvf}$.
\item $\texttt{delta\_reinforce}$ --- REINFORCE without baseline.
Provenance-only ($\texttt{measured}=[]$): REINFORCE is mentioned in
$\texttt{experiments/results/EXPERIMENT\_LEDGER.md}$ (gsm8k-reinforce,
four wandb runs on Qwen3-8B / Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) but no
$\texttt{delta\_*.json}$ existed. The entry closes that gap and
provides a stable id for a future same-stack REINFORCE arm to land
in without re-minting the entry.
\item $\texttt{delta\_liteppo}$ --- LitePPO (no value head, symmetric
clip). Provenance-only ($\texttt{measured}=[]$): mentioned as
$\texttt{ppo\_lite}$ in the ledger but had no entry. Citation is a
transparent placeholder ($\texttt{arxiv}=\text{``''}$) because no
peer-reviewed LitePPO paper is verified; this is flagged on the
entry itself rather than hidden.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Cross-reference audit ($\texttt{missing\_delta\_audit.tsv}$).}
For every $\texttt{(stack, variant\_deltas\_applied[*].delta\_id)}$
pair, check whether $\texttt{registry/entries/\textless delta\_id \textgreater.json}$
exists. Before this iter, the audit could not run; after this iter it
audits \textbf{26 (stack, delta) claims} and finds \textbf{0
CLAIMED\_BUT\_MISSING} --- every delta claimed by a stack now
resolves to a registered entry. The new
$\texttt{delta\_adaptiveg}$ is also cross-linked into
$\texttt{colab-open\_grpo-adaptiveg\_e3}$, whose
$\texttt{variant\_deltas\_applied}$ was previously $[]$ even though
the entry IS the live implementation of the adaptive-G delta.

\paragraph{Sharpest actionable finding.}
The two new provenance-only entries ($\texttt{delta\_reinforce}$,
$\texttt{delta\_liteppo}$) are the first entries in the registry with
$\texttt{measured}=[]$ declared \emph{by design} rather than as a
side-effect of unmeasured data. Their $\texttt{notes}$ field states
the explicit criterion for adding a measured row
(``same model + task + sampler + RLHF pipeline with only the baseline
removed''). This is the iter-50 sharpest-finding's promised
``backlog-with-provenance'' pattern: structural placeholders that
make the missing-data surface machine-readable, not hidden.

\paragraph{Cross-paper coupling.}
The $\Delta_{\text{zvf}}$ SUPPORTS verdict is the \emph{third}
empirical confirmation (after iter-31 and iter-47) that
ZVF-driven adaptive group-size reduces ZVF on a real worktree panel;
the chain predicted-effect $\to$ measured-effect on the same panel
is now a closed loop for the adaptive-G delta. The
$\Delta_{\text{reward\_mean}}$ NEUTRAL on 16 obs is informative on
its own: at this scale, the iter-31 prediction that adaptive-G
improves last-step reward is not falsified, just under-powered. A
larger $n$ (or a real GSM8K cell) would push it into either
SUPPORTS or CONTRADICTS --- the iter-31 prediction remains
unfalsified.
