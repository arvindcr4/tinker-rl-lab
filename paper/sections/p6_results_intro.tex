\section{The Resource: Overview}
\label{sec:p6-overview}
Unlike the empirical pillar papers, the primary deliverable here is an
artifact. It ships in the benchmark repository under \texttt{registry/}:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.15em]
  \item \texttt{registry/schema.json} --- a JSON Schema (draft 2020-12)
        defining the two record types (\texttt{stack} and
        \texttt{variant_delta});
  \item \texttt{registry/entries/*.json} --- 20 seed stack records and 11
        variant-delta records, every one derived from a concrete artifact of
        this benchmark (\secref{sec:setup}) or from a source paper we verified;
  \item \texttt{registry/query.py} --- a dependency-free reference CLI
        implementing \texttt{list}, \texttt{query}, \texttt{badge}, and
        \texttt{stackdiff}.
\end{itemize}

All numbers quoted from the registry in this paper (badge scores, query
results, flip-risk verdicts) are outputs of that CLI run on the shipped
entries, so the paper describes something the reader can re-execute in
seconds. The sections that follow present the schema
(Section~\ref{sec:p6-schema}), the population process and what it revealed
about our own reporting gaps (Section~\ref{sec:p6-population}), the query and
audit tooling (Section~\ref{sec:p6-query}), worked examples
(Section~\ref{sec:p6-worked}), the relation to existing community resources
(Section~\ref{sec:p6-related-resources}), and the maintenance model
(Section~\ref{sec:p6-governance}).

A scoping note: the benchmark infrastructure of \secref{sec:benchmark} supplies
the provenance for the seed entries, but this paper makes no new empirical
claims beyond the program measurements it cites; where a cited measurement
comes from toy-scale Colab audits we mark it as directional.
