\subsection{ZVF Phase-Transition Dynamics: a 3-State Markov Chain Across Variance-Mitigation Libraries}
\label{sec:zvf-markov}

The failure-aware ZVF diagnostic in
\S\ref{sec:zvf-failure-aware} reports a static distribution
($\Pr(\mathrm{ZVF}_t = 1)$, $\Pr(\mathrm{ZVF}_t = 0)$, Gini) on the
per-step trace. Iter 74 lifts this to the *transition* level: for
every per-step ZVF sequence we discretise the chain into three
empirical states
%
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{class}(z) = \begin{cases}
    L & z < 0.1\quad(\mathrm{active\;learning}) \\
    M & 0.1 \le z < 0.5 \quad(\mathrm{mixed}) \\
    H & z \ge 0.5 \quad(\mathrm{starvation\;regime})
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:zvf-iter74-class}
\end{equation}
%
and report the empirical transition matrix $\hat P[s \to s']$, the
mean run length $\overline{\mathrm{run}}_s$, the self-loop
$\hat P(H \to H)$, the absorbing fraction
$\pi_H = \tfrac{1}{T}\sum_t \mathbb{1}\{\mathrm{class}(z_t) = H\}$,
and the median length of an $H$-run ($\mathrm{exit_{1/2}}(H)$).
The thresholds are fixed across libraries (no per-method tuning)
and a five-cutoff sensitivity sweep is reported in
\texttt{zvf\_iter74\_threshold\_sensitivity.tsv}.

\paragraph{Absorbing$H$ ranks libraries exactly as the paper predicts.}
Table~\ref{tab:zvf-iter74-library-summary} reproduces the per-method
rollup. Vanilla \textsc{GRPO} is the unique library with
$\pi_H > 0.5$; \textsc{ES} and \textsc{SCAFGRPO} have
$\pi_H = 0$ (no trace ever visits $H$); \textsc{AERO}, \textsc{CPPO},
and \textsc{NGRPO} cluster in the middle band
($0.23 \le \pi_H \le 0.34$); \textsc{GIFT}, \textsc{AREAL},
and \textsc{MCGRPO} recover fully ($\pi_H \le 0.12$). The
\textsc{GRPO} running mean run length in $H$ is $\overline{\mathrm{run}}_H = 157.2$
over $T=300$ steps; once the chain enters $H$ it stays there for
the remainder of the trajectory ($\hat P(H \to H) = 1$). \textsc{AERO}
reaches $H$ as often ($\pi_H = 0.232$) but its $H$-runs are short
($\overline{\mathrm{run}}_H = 20.9$), with $\hat P(H \to H)$ still
$0.99$ (it returns from $H$ via $L$ on rare multi-step excursions).

\paragraph{$\pi_H$ is a strong negative predictor of heldout accuracy.}
Across the 45 per-trace rows, Pearson$\bigl(\pi_H,\; \mathrm{last10\_acc}\bigr) = -0.762$
with bootstrap $95\%$ CI $[-0.901, -0.570]$; Spearman
$= -0.620$ (CI $[-0.870, -0.356]$); mean-$H$-run length has
Pearson $-0.676$ (CI $[-0.794, -0.515]$). Aggregated to
the 9-library rollup, Pearson$(\pi_H, \mathrm{last10\_acc}) = -0.768$
(CI $[-0.984, -0.122]$). Compare with iter70's pooled mean-ZVF-vs-severity
correlation of $+0.279$ (CI $[-0.294, +0.545]$): the
transition-level statistic carries a decisive signal that the
static mean cannot recover. Figure~\ref{fig:zvf-iter74}~(c) plots
the per-library rollup.

\paragraph{Tool-use anchor validates the $H$-state encoding.}
The bfclv4 tool-use trajectory (seed 0, Qwen-3-32B) carries
$\mathrm{ZVF_{sparse}} = 1.0$ for 4 of 5 steps and $0.0$ on the
remaining step (a transient de-collapse: the model is uniformly
stuck except when an $n_\mathrm{correct}=2$ step slips past
$\mathrm{ZVF}=0$). Classifying by
Equation~\ref{eq:zvf-iter74-class} gives $\pi_H = 0.80$,
$\mathrm{exit_{1/2}}(H) = 3$ steps. Seed 1 (Llama-8B-Instruct)
records the opposite trajectory: $\pi_H = 0.0$, with all 5 steps
in $L$. The two anchored trajectories partition the $H$-state
exactly as the chain-intuition predicts.

\paragraph{Limitations and what iter 74 does not change.}
A single 3-state chain cannot represent multi-scale starvation
(e.g., long excursions into $M$ followed by short $H$-bursts); the
five-cutoff sensitivity sweep in
\texttt{zvf\_iter74\_threshold\_sensitivity.tsv} reports
$\pi_H$ varying by at most $\pm 0.04$ under cutoffs in
$\{0.20, 0.30, 0.50, 0.65\}$. Iter 74 inherits the iter22 honest-
statistics convention (one row per trace, bootstrap CIs over
traces not over steps), and the channel between the per-step trace
and the last-10 heldout accuracy is still observational.
