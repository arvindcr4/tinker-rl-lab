## Evidence limits and bibliography interpretation

The critique's sentence says twelve uncited suites but actually enumerates fifteen. At audit start the current refs.bib already had CORE-Bench, WebArena, MLE-bench, AgentHarm and BALROG citations. Its blanket claim that only five suites are cited therefore does not describe the current source. Six missing scholarly benchmark references and project/site citations are supplied separately; never fabricate a paper for a project without an authenticated one. Current URLs authenticate identity, not exact past versions or future permanence. Historic checkpoint availability is currently corroborated by metadata, but missing model-output artifacts and sampler equivalence are not repaired by citation checks.
