# #118 P5 — Stack-conditioning η² on the 9-method 5-seed zvf130 risk-index panel (iter 101)

**Pillar:** P5 (Pillar 1 — Report the Stack, Not the Label / MIN-REPORT)
**Target classes:** T1 (statistical rigor with paired-seed LOMO + permutation) + T2 (fresh-data evidence on the 9-method zvf130 panel) + T3 (cross-paper coupling to P6 iter 90 / FRONTIER_INSIGHTS Round 1)

## Brief vein picked

Brief vein (b) was: *"quantify stack-conditioning with the N2 four-method same-stack tensors and the berkeley unpacking_dpo_ppo factorization (algorithm-axis eta^2 vs stack axes)"*. Iter 89 row 106 closed this vein on the 4-method N2 per-step zvf trace (η² = 0.045, "decisive"). Iter 101 EXTENDS the same machinery to the 9-method 5-seed zvf130 risk-index panel (45 rows) and asks the y-axis-conditional question: does "algorithm equivalence" generalise to a different y-axis (the zvf130 risk-index summary)?

## Method (re-uses Berkeley row 22 machinery)

Reference: `platform_modal/scripts/berkeley/unpacking_dpo_ppo_factorization.py::axis_variance_fraction`. Iter 101 re-applies the same η² = SS_axis / SS_total decomposition to the 9-method × 5-seed zvf130 risk-index panel (`platform_hybrid/experiments/results/zvf_iter130_risk_index.tsv`, 45 measured rows after excluding 7 scaling-law / tool-use reference rows that have only 1 seed).

For each panel (full 9, n2_subset 4, grpo_family 7, grpo+es 8, grpo+scafgrpo 8), iter 101 computes:
1. **Point estimate** of η²(algo, metric) and η²(seed, metric) on every one of 7 risk-index channels (zvf_risk, mean_zvf, lag1_zvf_rolling_w15, slope, risk_mag, risk_csd, risk_drift).
2. **LOMO range** = [min_η²(LOMO), max_η²(LOMO)] across 9 leave-one-method-out panels; the LOMO range is the jackknife proxy CI (residual bootstrap is degenerate because within-method seed variation is much smaller than between-method variation).
3. **Permutation test** = method-label shuffle (N=2000, seed 20260705); fraction of permuted η² ≥ observed gives the p-value.

## Falsifiable Headlines (measured live)

| H | Claim | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | η²(algo, zvf_risk) on the full 9 is large (point ≥ 0.50, LOMO min ≥ 0.65) | **point=0.763, LOMO=[0.6835, 0.8274]** — every LOMO stays ≥ 0.68 |
| **H2** | η²(algo, zvf_risk) on the 4-method N2 subset is ALSO large (point ≥ 0.50) | **point=0.6698, LOMO=[0.4191, 0.7772]** — yes, CONTRAST with iter 89 row 106 (per-step zvf, η²=0.045 on same 4 methods) |
| **H3** | algo > seed axis on zvf_risk (full 9) | **algo=0.763, seed=0.0071** — algorithm axis is 107× the seed axis on the panel level |
| **H4** | permutation p < 0.01 on both panels (full 9 + n2_subset) | **p_full9=0.0, p_n2=0.0015** — both highly significant |
| **H5** | SCAFGRPO is the most load-bearing method on the algorithm axis | **SCAFGRPO rel_drop = -10.42%**; next is AERO +8.44% (sits between clusters) |
| **H6** | Adding ES to the 7-method GRPO-family subset does not increase η²(algo, zvf_risk) by > 0.10 | **delta = +0.050** (0.6835 - 0.6336) — within the 0.10 threshold |

## Sharpest finding

**The algorithm axis is y-axis-conditional, not method-set conditional.** The same 4 GRPO-family methods that have η²(algo, zvf) = 0.045 on the per-step zvf trace (iter 89 row 106) have η²(algo, zvf_risk) = 0.670 on the zvf130 risk-index. The y-axis choice (per-step contrast-yield vs risk-index summary) changes the algorithm visibility by 15×.

This is a **y-axis-specific extension of the Estimator-Equivalence Principle** of `FRONTIER_INSIGHTS.md` Round 1. The principle is true for the per-step zvf trace but NOT for the zvf130 risk-index. The "GRPO-family algorithm invisibility" is a property of how we measure, not of the algorithms themselves.

## Cross-paper coupling

- (i) **P5 iter-89 row 106** — direct y-axis comparison. iter 89 reports η²(algo, zvf) = 0.045 on the 4-method N2 per-step zvf trace; iter 101 reports η²(algo, zvf_risk) = 0.670 on the same 4 methods but on the zvf130 risk-index. The 15× difference is the y-axis-conditional finding.
- (ii) **P6 iter-90 row 107** — iter 90 measured that all 9 methods are significantly below GRPO on zvf_risk (26/36 pairs SIG, SCAFGRPO is the lowest at 0.225); iter 101's algorithm-axis η²=0.763 IS the iter-90 "all below GRPO" finding measured at the variance level.
- (iii) **P5 iter-93 row 109** — iter 93 reported per-axis η² on the 98-cell mega corpus (model_family × task_slice × G × t × seed); iter 101 applies the same decomposition to a 9-method × 5-seed panel that is structurally different (no model_family axis, the algorithm axis is the entire stack signal).
- (iv) **FRONTIER_INSIGHTS Round 1 (Critic Degeneracy Hypothesis)** — the hypothesis states that within a family (GRPO), the algorithm axis is invisible. Iter 101 shows this is **y-axis-conditional**: true on per-step zvf (iter 89), false on the zvf130 risk-index (this iter). The "estimator equivalence" claim is therefore restricted to the per-step y-axis.
- (v) **P5 iter-80 row 95 / iter-81 row 96** — MIN-REPORT Items 13-17 (yield-residual) live on the per-step zvf axis; iter 101 motivates a complementary Item 18 (zvf130_risk_residual) on the risk-index axis, which would close the y-axis coverage of the MIN-REPORT standard.

## Operational recommendation

Report algorithm-axis η² on the y-axis that consumers care about:
- For stack-conditioning on **per-step contrast-yield** (P7 controller-relevant), the algorithm axis is small (η² ≤ 0.05 within GRPO-family) and the algorithm label is invisible.
- For stack-conditioning on the **zvf130 risk-index** (P6 registry-relevant), the algorithm axis is large (η² = 0.76 on full 9) and SCAFGRPO is the load-bearing outlier.

MIN-REPORT coverage should be extended with an **Item 18 (zvf130_risk_residual)** = zvf_risk_obs - zvf_risk_iid_baseline, computed from the (mean_zvf, lag1_zvf, slope) triplet on the per-method trajectory. This would close the y-axis coverage gap and let consumers filter cross-method claims on the y-axis they care about.
