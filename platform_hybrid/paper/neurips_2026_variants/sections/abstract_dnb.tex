\begin{abstract}
We present \ours{}, a tier-classified collection of RL post-training runs
on language models across heterogeneous stacks (four open RL libraries and
the closed Tinker API), with a run manifest that records, for every
(backend, model, task) cell, whether it completed, how many seeds it has,
and which run files back its numbers. The manifest lists 79 run records:
27 in Tier~\textbf{A} (completed, at least three seeds; inferential
eligibility separately required), 43 in Tier~\textbf{B} (completed single-
or two-run case studies, descriptive only), and 9 in Tier~\textbf{C}
(failed, partial, or quarantined), plus six planned cells that never
started. Every reported training-reward number is recomputed from a named
run file. Most completed language-model cells are short single-seed GSM8K
runs on Tinker; the open-stack multi-seed cells are a TRL Qwen2.5-0.5B
arithmetic cell and three classic-RL PPO pipeline checks.

Zero-Variance Fraction (ZVF), the share of rollout groups with identical
rewards, is logged where the step logs allow; its expectation
$\mathbb E[\mathrm{ZVF}_t]=p^{G}+(1{-}p)^{G}$ under conditionally i.i.d.\
binary rewards at fixed success probability $p$ is a mathematical statement,
not an empirical tier. A separate five-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K held-out
evaluation shows $+1.3$pp over a single base-model run (one-sample
$t(4){=}1.32$, $p{=}0.26$), which is not evidence of a gain. Earlier
pooled and stratified ZVF--reward correlations, a token-budget group-size
reanalysis, and compute/carbon tables could not be traced to released
inputs and are withdrawn; we document what was removed and why.

The historical artifact URL (not a verified complete release inventory) is
\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/agentic-grpo-bench-anon-2C6B}.
\end{abstract}
