\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\ours{} is a tier-classified record of RL post-training runs across
libraries, algorithms, and model families. The current release supports
three conservative statements: (i) ZVF/GU are \emph{descriptive}
diagnostics of when binary-reward GRPO groups stop producing within-group
contrast, and we make no claim that they predict final reward; (ii) the
completed cells are dominated by short single-run Tinker GSM8K case
studies, and the few multi-seed LM cells are closed-backend training
rewards plus one open-stack arithmetic cell (mean accuracy 0.73); and (iii)
the separate five-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K held-out evaluation does not show a
gain over the base model ($p{=}0.26$). Several analyses in earlier drafts
could not be traced to released inputs and were withdrawn; immutable cohort
IDs, raw per-group logs, and released checkpoints are required before this
collection can support a reproduction or coverage claim.
