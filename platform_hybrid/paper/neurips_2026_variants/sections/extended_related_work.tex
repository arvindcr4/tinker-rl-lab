\section{Extended Scope Notes for ZVF}
\label{sec:extended-related-work}

\ours{} defines Zero-Variance Fraction (ZVF) for grouped rollouts with scalar
rewards. It is informative only when within-group reward equality is a plausible
failure mode of the optimizer. Concurrent 2025--2026
work~\cite{nan2025ngrpo,le2025rlzvp,zhang2025scaffgrpo,feng2025lens,pikus2025hardexamples,han2026ebpo}
independently identifies and addresses the same within-group zero-variance
failure mode, with RL-ZVP~\cite{le2025rlzvp} naming it ``zero-variance
prompts'' in the same sense we use it. This appendix records the boundaries
we can defend from our artifact and from established process-reward and
concurrent zero-variance work; we do not claim those papers validate the
specific ZVF predictiveness numbers reported here.

\subsection{Sampling Structure}
\label{sec:ext:tree-prm}

The measurements in this paper use independent sampled completions within each
GRPO group. We do not evaluate tree-structured, shared-prefix, or otherwise
correlated group samplers. If a future sampler intentionally couples group
members, ZVF must be reported alongside an estimate of intra-group correlation;
otherwise a zero-variance event can reflect the sampler rather than only the
policy or reward function.

\subsection{Dense Process Rewards}

Process reward models such as Let's Verify Step by Step~\cite{lightman2023prm}
replace a single terminal outcome with step-level supervision. Under dense
step rewards, exact equality of aggregate trajectory rewards can become rare
even when learning is unhealthy. In that regime, ZVF can approach zero for both
healthy and collapsed runs, so it must be replaced or augmented with a
per-step reward-variance, effective-rollout, or effective-rank diagnostic.

\subsection{PPO-Style Stability Metrics}
\label{sec:ext:stppo}

PPO-style trainers often expose token-level clip fractions, approximate KL, and
entropy. These metrics are not substitutes for ZVF: they measure policy-update
geometry rather than within-group reward contrast. We therefore treat them as
complementary observables. The combined alarm that remains defensible is
descriptive, not validated:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}_t =
\bigl(\mathrm{ZVF}_t > \tau_z\bigr)
\wedge
\bigl(\mathrm{clip\_frac}_t > \tau_c\bigr).
\label{eq:combined}
\end{equation}
This rule should be calibrated before use; we do not claim a universal
threshold or a measured false-positive rate.

\subsection{RL-Free or Hybrid Alignment}
\label{sec:ext:dar}

ZVF is undefined when there are no sampled rollout groups. In RL-free
preference-regression regimes, a possible surrogate is per-minibatch
gradient-norm variance,
\[
\mathrm{GNV}_t =
\mathrm{Var}_{i \in \mathcal{B}_t}
\left[\,\lVert \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_i(\theta_t) \rVert_2\,\right].
\]
This is a proposal for future instrumentation, not a result measured in the
current artifact.
