\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{Artifact card.}
This workshop submission is an \textbf{artifact note} accompanying a release
that contains: \textit{(i)}~step-level aggregate reward traces (mean, peak,
last-10) for every reported run; \textit{(ii)}~per-step Zero-Variance
Fraction (ZVF) and Gradient Utilization (GU) traces for every reported run;
\textit{(iii)}~a per-run manifest (Appendix~\ref{sec:run-manifest}) listing
backend, hardware, hyperparameter mapping, and evidence grade;
\textit{(iv)}~parser scripts that decode the released JSONL into a single
tidy table; and \textit{(v)}~a documented anonymous repository at
\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/agentic-grpo-bench-anon-2C6B} for
code, logs, and Docker images. We deliberately do \emph{not} claim a
``first comprehensive'' GRPO benchmark, a frontier ranking, or a definitive
algorithm comparison.

\paragraph{Claim hierarchy (one sentence).}
The mechanical ZVF-to-centered-reward-contrast identity is an A-grade
definitional claim; later, separate five-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K evidence
($+1.3\,$pp; one-sample seed-level $p{=}0.26$ against a fixed baseline, not a
paired-seed test) is future-paper evidence; the pooled ZVF--last-10
correlation ($r{=}{-}0.769$, $N{=}15$) is a B$^{*}$-grade descriptive
finding that is task-confounded and collapses within-task; everything
else (frontier-scale Tinker rows, tool-use, group-size sweeps) is C-grade
case study and is released for honesty rather than as benchmark fact.

\paragraph{Why this is an artifact note rather than a benchmark.}
Reinforcement-learning post-training now drives much of modern language-model
reasoning and alignment~\citep{ouyang2022training,guo2025deepseekr1nature},
but empirical claims are hard to compare:
PPO~\citep{schulman2017proximal}, GRPO~\citep{shao2024deepseekmath}, and
DPO~\citep{rafailov2024direct} are typically reported on different model
families with different framework defaults, different rollout regimes, and
different reward parsers. Rather than declare a winner, we release the
underlying traces and a candid evidence-grade table
(Table~\ref{tab:evidence-grade}) so that downstream readers can re-pool
or stratify as they see fit. The release explicitly distinguishes
multi-seed open-stack arms (5 seeds) from single-seed closed-backend
\tinker{} case studies; the workshop paper is the honest tour of what those
artifacts do and do not support. Discussion prompts for the workshop are
in Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}.
