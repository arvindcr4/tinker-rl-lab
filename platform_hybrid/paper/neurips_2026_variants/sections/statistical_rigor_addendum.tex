\section{Statistical Rigor Addendum: Evidence Tiers and Survival}
\label{sec:stat_rigor}
\label{app:stat-rigor-addendum}

This appendix separates replication metadata from inferential eligibility.
Earlier survival decisions mixed unresolved task identities, reconstructed seed
summaries and dependent trajectory samples. Those significance decisions are
withdrawn; deleting rows from a multiplicity family cannot repair its inputs.
No reanalysis is claimed by this source correction.

\subsection{Evidence tiers}
\label{sec:stat-rigor-addendum:tiers}

We use the manifest policy consistently: A denotes completed cells with at
least three independent seeds at fixed configuration; B denotes completed
single-seed descriptive cells; C denotes failed, partial or unstarted cells.
These are metadata categories, not thresholds that validate a statistical test.
Wave-6 is 12 configurations with one seed each (B), not 12 replications.
Backend openness, horizon, endpoint and task identity are separate requirements.
Inference requires actual seed outcomes, a declared comparison and an eligible
independent unit. Temporal reward samples are not independent training seeds.
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tiers}
\text{inferential eligibility}\ne\text{replication label alone}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{BH correction after Tier-C exclusion (W5)}
\label{sec:stat-rigor-addendum:bh}

Before analysis, define the eligible independent-unit comparisons and their
hypothesis-family IDs. For valid sorted p-values $p_{(1)},\ldots,p_{(m)}$,
BH adjusted values are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bh-restricted}
q_{(i)}=\min\{1,\min_{j\ge i}m p_{(j)}/j\}.
\end{equation}
At fixed ranks, increasing $m$ makes rejection harder, not anti-conservative.
Dropping tests after seeing results does not justify a smaller family. No FDR
guarantee is attached to historical step-level or synthetic-sample tests.
Independent resampling of two arms is unpaired; a paired bootstrap must
preserve genuine seed or prompt pairs. Zero pooled variance makes a standardized
effect undefined, even if the unstandardized difference is zero.

\subsection{Claims that are explicitly downgraded (W4)}
\label{sec:stat-rigor-addendum:downgrades}

Single-run trajectories remain descriptive telemetry. Neither step-wise
Welch tests nor labeling p-values ``descriptive'' supplies valid treatment
inference. Reconstructed seed vectors cannot support an empirical bootstrap.
The later $82.0\%\to83.3\%$ comparison is described elsewhere as a five-seed
one-sample test against a fixed baseline; the paired and two-sample descriptions
are withdrawn here pending identified observations and evaluation items.
A reported nonsignificant result is inconclusive, not evidence of no effect.
Item-level uncertainty and training-seed uncertainty target different populations.

\subsection{Reproducibility artifact}
\label{sec:stat-rigor-addendum:repro}

The historical analysis drivers and TSVs require a separate generator-side
audit. A future release must bind each table to run IDs, hashes, actual seed
outcomes, task/split/evaluator identities, endpoint and family ID. Determinism
alone does not validate scientific inputs or uncertainty. This source revision
neither regenerates stored results nor certifies the existing pipeline.
