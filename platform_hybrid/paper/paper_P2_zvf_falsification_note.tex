\title{The Zero-Variance Fraction Is an Overdispersion Summary:\\
\large A Correction Note on a Retracted ``Sign-Wrong Bernoulli'' Result}
\author{Arvind C R\\
PES University\\\texttt{arvindcr4@gmail.com}}
\date{18 October 2026}

\begin{abstract}
Group-relative policy optimization (GRPO) trains on groups of $G$ rollouts per
prompt; the zero-variance fraction (ZVF) is the share of groups whose binary
rewards are constant. An earlier version of this note claimed that the i.i.d.\
Bernoulli model $\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}=p^G+(1-p)^G$ is ``sign-wrong'':
a mean per-group divergence of $+0.1224$ on Qwen3-8B/GSM8K and a separately
computed step-level divergence on synthetic arithmetic. \textbf{We withdraw that
claim.} The positive value is a sign-guaranteed artefact of a plug-in estimator
($h_G(k/G)\ge\mathbf{1}\{k\in\{0,G\}\}$ for every group; its expectation is
about $+0.105$ at $G=8$, $\bar p=0.694$ under exactly i.i.d.\ Bernoulli rollouts).
For the step-level estimator, the nonpositive population Jensen gap competes
with positive finite-sample plug-in bias; its sign is not guaranteed.
Under conditionally independent rollouts,
$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}]=\mathbb{E}_i[h_G(p_i)]$ and the excess over
$h_G(\bar p)$ would reflect prompt-difficulty dispersion; we use the excess only
as an overdispersion summary, because the present observations cannot apportion
it between that mechanism and conditional rollout dependence.
On the 600 GSM8K groups, observed ZVF is $0.158$, the homogeneous-binomial
value is $0.054$, and a two-parameter beta-binomial fitted to these same groups
has expected ZVF $0.170$ (marginal intra-class correlation $0.160$). That
total-only agreement hides cell-level misfit: the fit implies $4.2$ all-fail and
$98.1$ all-success groups against $19$ and $76$ observed, and it is rejected
(Pearson $\chi^2=82.2$, df $6$). ZVF is the same object, up to aggregation
level, as the Advantage Collapse Rate of \citet{he2026advantage},
and the Jensen excess was proved by \citet{nie2026gradstarvation}; we claim no
new diagnostic or theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}

GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath} centres each reward on its group mean, so a
group with constant rewards contributes no gradient.
For a group of $G\ge2$ binary rewards with $k$ successes write
$\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}=\mathbf{1}\{k\in\{0,G\}\}$ and
$h_G(p)=p^G+(1-p)^G$. If prompt $i$ has latent success probability $p_i$ and
its $G$ rollouts are conditionally i.i.d., then
$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}\mid p_i]=h_G(p_i)$ and, over a prompt
population with $\bar p:=\mathbb E_i[p_i]$,
$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}]=\mathbb{E}_i[h_G(p_i)]\ge h_G(\bar p)$,
with equality only when all prompts share one $p$ ($h_G$ is strictly convex
for $G\ge2$). Define $\mathrm{pass@}G(p):=1-(1-p)^G$ \citep{chen2021codex}.
Then $\mathrm{pass@}G(p)-p^G=1-h_G(p)$ is an algebraic identity, not an
empirical finding. Its sample counterpart is
$\mathbf1\{k\ge1\}-\mathbf1\{k=G\}=1-\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}$.

\section{Why the retracted divergence carried no information}

\paragraph{The plug-in estimator is sign-guaranteed.}
The retracted GSM8K statistic was
$\delta_i = h_G(\hat p_i)-\mathbf{1}\{k_i\in\{0,G\}\}$ with $\hat p_i=k_i/G$
estimated \emph{from the same group}. If $k_i\in\{0,G\}$ both terms equal $1$;
otherwise the indicator is $0$ and $h_G(\hat p_i)>0$. Hence $\delta_i\ge0$ for
every group, and a positive mean is guaranteed whenever any group is mixed
(84.2\% are; Table~\ref{tab:main}). Under the exact i.i.d.\ Bernoulli null
with $G=8$ the expected value is
$\sum_{k=0}^G \binom{G}{k}\bar p^k(1-\bar p)^{G-k}\,[h_G(k/G)-\mathbf{1}\{k\in\{0,G\}\}]
\simeq+0.105$ at $\bar p=0.694$ ($+0.108$ under the fitted beta-binomial).
The empirical plug-in mean is $0.2807$, minus observed ZVF $0.1583$, giving
$+0.1224$. Simulating 600 independent groups gives 95\% sampling bands for the
mean of $[0.095,0.114]$ under the homogeneous binomial and $[0.097,0.118]$
under the fitted beta-binomial; $+0.1224$ lies above both, so the observed
$k$-histogram is not reproduced by either null, but a positive value is still
guaranteed by construction and carries no sign information. The bands treat
groups as independent, which the 103 repeated-problem pairs
(\S\ref{sec:gsm8k}) violate. On the same groups the pooled plug-in residual
$h_8(\bar p)-\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}=-0.104$ has the opposite sign: the sign
is set by the choice of estimator. The number therefore cannot evidence
``anti-herding''.

\paragraph{The step-level estimator has competing biases.}
The synthetic-arithmetic statistic used an estimated step-mean success rate:
$\delta_t=h_G(\hat{\bar p}_t)-\overline{\mathrm{ZVF}}_t$, where
$\hat{\bar p}_t=(B_tG_t)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{B_t}k_{ti}$ and
$\overline{\mathrm{ZVF}}_t=B_t^{-1}\sum_i\mathbf1\{k_{ti}\in\{0,G_t\}\}$.
For latent prompt probabilities, convexity implies
$h_G(\mathbb E_i[p_i])-\mathbb E_i[h_G(p_i)]\le0$.
The finite-sample expectation adds the nonnegative term
$\mathbb E[h_G(\hat{\bar p}_t)]-h_G(\bar p_t)$, so a negative sign is not
implied by the population inequality alone. Recomputing the 480 aggregate rows
with source \texttt{groupsize\_zvf\_sweep} in
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/zvf_contrastive_yield.tsv} gives $-0.0668$
($-0.067$ rounded), across $G\in\{2,4,8,16\}$ with 120 rows per $G$
(per-$G$ means $-0.010$, $-0.055$, $-0.094$, $-0.109$). The rows are the step
logs of 12 GRPO runs (Qwen2.5-0.5B \citep{qwen2024qwen25}, synthetic arithmetic,
40 steps, 3 seeds per $G$); the same value is recovered from the per-run
JSON step logs under
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/modal_registry/modal_artifacts/tinkerrl-results/groupsize_zvf/}.
The earlier $-0.0703$ (459 steps) is withdrawn: its subset/weighting provenance
is not reconciled. Run metadata record $B_t=16$ prompts per step, but the logs
store only the step-mean reward and step ZVF, not per-group success counts, so
neither a finite-sample null nor a causal dependence mechanism is identified.
With only three seed clusters per $G$ no reliable interval is available, and the
historical step-iid interval is withheld.

Observed ZVF is $2.9\times$ the homogeneous-binomial value.
The beta-binomial is an unconstrained two-parameter maximum-likelihood fit
(Nelder--Mead on log parameters); its implied mean $a/(a+b)=0.690$ is not
fixed at the empirical $0.694$. Its ZVF agreement within $0.012$ is an
in-sample total-only match produced by offsetting errors: the fit
under-predicts all-fail groups ($4.2$ vs.\ $19$) and over-predicts all-success
groups ($98.1$ vs.\ $76$), and the $k=0$ cell alone contributes $53.0$ of the
Pearson $\chi^2=82.2$ (df $6$, $p\approx10^{-15}$; likelihood-ratio
$G^2=59.0$, $p\approx10^{-10}$). The fitted beta-binomial is therefore rejected;
it is a descriptive overdispersion summary, not held-out prediction or mechanism
identification. Overdispersion relative to a single binomial is not in doubt:
the variance of $k$ is $2.2\times$ the binomial value. The unpooled Pearson
statistic against Binomial$(8,\bar p)$ is not used because two expected cells
are below 1 ($0.046$ and $0.83$ at $k=0,1$); the likelihood-ratio $G^2=341.3$
exceeds every value in $20{,}000$ parametric-bootstrap replicates
($p<10^{-4}$), and pooling $k\le2$ (expected $7.5$) gives Pearson
$\chi^2=323.8$ (df $5$).

The 600 groups contain 503 distinct problems by question text: 85 appear in
two seeds and 6 in all three, giving 103 repeated-problem pairs, consistent
with independent seed-shuffles of the 1{,}319-problem test split. Success
counts of repeated problems correlate across independent runs (Pearson
$r=0.65$), which persistent prompt-level difficulty predicts and within-group
rollout dependence alone does not. This is a post-hoc check; it does not bound
any additional within-group dependence, so the decomposition of the excess ZVF
remains unidentified. The excess of all-fail groups may also partly reflect
answer truncation: the JSON schema matches
\nolinkurl{platform_hybrid/experiments/tinker_direct_eval.py} (raw prompt with a
\verb|\boxed{}| answer suffix, boxed-or-last-integer parser, default
\texttt{max\_tokens}${}=512$), but the token limit is not recorded in the result
files and only 200-character completion prefixes are stored, so truncation
cannot be audited from these artefacts.

\paragraph{Prospective test (not executed).}
With $M\ge G$ independent calibration rollouts and $m_i$ successes for prompt
$i$, predict held-out ZVF using
$\widehat h_i=[\binom{m_i}{G}+\binom{M-m_i}{G}]/\binom{M}{G}$
(with binomial coefficients zero when the upper argument is below $G$).
This is unbiased for $h_G(p_i)$ under conditional i.i.d.\ sampling, unlike
$h_G(m_i/M)$; it is the same hypergeometric construction as the unbiased
pass@$k$ estimator of \citet{chen2021codex}. Average over prompts and assess held-out error with
prompt-clustered uncertainty. No results of this prospective protocol are claimed.

\section{Relation to prior work}
Nothing here is new. ZVF is equivalent, up to aggregation granularity, to the
Advantage Collapse Rate \citep{he2026advantage}, which is defined as the
proportion of training batches with ineffective gradients rather than of
groups. Beta-binomial modelling of per-prompt success probabilities is already
used to predict pass@$k$ scaling \citep{kazdan2025passk}. \citet{nie2026gradstarvation}
prove that the true degeneracy rate exceeds the i.i.d.\ Bernoulli prediction
by Jensen's inequality, which is the corrected reading above; DARA
\citep{zheng2026dara} uses the complementary active-group density in
multi-reward RL. DAPO's dynamic sampling \citep{yu2025dapo}, GRESO
\citep{zhang2026greso}, VCRL \citep{jiang2025vcrl}, and online difficulty
filtering \citep{bae2025online} act on degenerate groups. The only
contribution of this note is the retraction and the GSM8K numbers.

\section{Non-claims}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No claim that the Bernoulli model is ``sign-wrong'' (withdrawn).
  \item No claim that ZVF predicts held-out collapse or ranks trainers; the
    earlier pooled Spearman $\rho=0.27$ is withdrawn (17 finite cells, 9 of
    them simulated, mixed outcome definitions).
  \item No use of \texttt{variance\_mitigation.tsv} (the earlier draft's
    comparison table behind that $\rho$) or any simulated panel.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reproducibility}
The script \nolinkurl{platform_hybrid/analysis_publication_fixes/p2_delta_artifact.py}
recomputes retained GSM8K group summaries and the descriptive 480-row
arithmetic mean from the named checked-in files, not every historical number
quoted for retraction. Repository: \url{https://github.com/arvindcr4/tinker-rl-lab}.
The GSM8K inputs live in \nolinkurl{platform_hybrid/experiments/results/};
outputs are written to a caller-selected new JSON file via \texttt{--output}.
That output includes the beta-binomial expected counts and the Pearson
statistic against the fitted beta-binomial. The likelihood-ratio statistics,
pooled test, parametric bootstrap, simulated null bands and repeated-problem
statistics are computed from the same three JSON files, and the arithmetic
cross-check from the per-run step logs, by a short
supplementary script that is not yet in the repository.
No held-out prediction experiment or immutable archive is supplied here.
