\begin{abstract}
Group-relative policy optimization (GRPO) trains on groups of $G$ rollouts per
prompt; the zero-variance fraction (ZVF) is the share of groups whose binary
rewards are constant. An earlier version of this note claimed that the i.i.d.\
Bernoulli model $\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}=p^G+(1-p)^G$ is ``sign-wrong'':
a mean per-group divergence of $+0.1224$ on Qwen3-8B/GSM8K and a separately
computed step-level divergence on synthetic arithmetic. \textbf{We withdraw that
claim.} The positive value is a sign-guaranteed artefact of a plug-in estimator
($h_G(k/G)\ge\mathbf{1}\{k\in\{0,G\}\}$ for every group; its expectation is
about $+0.105$ at $G=8$, $\bar p=0.694$ under exactly i.i.d.\ Bernoulli rollouts).
For the step-level estimator, the nonpositive population Jensen gap competes
with positive finite-sample plug-in bias; its sign is not guaranteed.
Under conditionally independent rollouts,
$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}]=\mathbb{E}_i[h_G(p_i)]$ and the excess over
$h_G(\bar p)$ reflects prompt-difficulty dispersion. The present observations
cannot distinguish that mechanism from conditional rollout dependence.
On the 600 GSM8K groups, observed ZVF is $0.158$, the homogeneous-binomial
value is $0.054$, and a two-parameter beta-binomial fitted to these same groups
has expected ZVF $0.170$ (marginal intra-class correlation $0.160$). This
is the same object as the Advantage Collapse Rate of \citet{he2026advantage},
and the Jensen excess was proved by \citet{nie2026gradstarvation}; we claim no
new diagnostic or theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}

For a group of $G\ge2$ binary rewards with $k$ successes write
$\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}=\mathbf{1}\{k\in\{0,G\}\}$ and
$h_G(p)=p^G+(1-p)^G$. If prompt $i$ has latent success probability $p_i$ and
its $G$ rollouts are conditionally i.i.d., then
$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}\mid p_i]=h_G(p_i)$ and, over a prompt
population with $\bar p:=\mathbb E_i[p_i]$,
$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}]=\mathbb{E}_i[h_G(p_i)]\ge h_G(\bar p)$,
with equality only when all prompts share one $p$ ($h_G$ is strictly convex
for $G\ge2$). Define $\mathrm{pass@}G(p):=1-(1-p)^G$.
Then $\mathrm{pass@}G(p)-p^G=1-h_G(p)$ is an algebraic identity, not an
empirical finding. Its sample counterpart is
$\mathbf1\{k\ge1\}-\mathbf1\{k=G\}=1-\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}$.

\section{Why the retracted divergence carried no information}

\paragraph{The plug-in estimator is sign-guaranteed.}
The retracted GSM8K statistic was
$\delta_i = h_G(\hat p_i)-\mathbf{1}\{k_i\in\{0,G\}\}$ with $\hat p_i=k_i/G$
estimated \emph{from the same group}. If $k_i\in\{0,G\}$ both terms equal $1$;
otherwise the indicator is $0$ and $h_G(\hat p_i)>0$. Hence $\delta_i\ge0$ for
every group, and a positive mean is guaranteed whenever any group is mixed
(84.2\% are; Table~\ref{tab:main}). Under the exact i.i.d.\ Bernoulli null
with $G=8$ the expected value is
$\sum_{k=0}^G \binom{G}{k}\bar p^k(1-\bar p)^{G-k}\,[h_G(k/G)-\mathbf{1}\{k\in\{0,G\}\}]
\simeq+0.105$ at $\bar p=0.694$ ($+0.108$ under the fitted beta-binomial).
The empirical plug-in mean is $0.2807$, minus observed ZVF $0.1583$, giving
$+0.1224$. The number therefore cannot evidence ``anti-herding''.

\paragraph{The step-level estimator has competing biases.}
The synthetic-arithmetic statistic used an estimated step-mean success rate:
$\delta_t=h_G(\hat{\bar p}_t)-\overline{\mathrm{ZVF}}_t$, where
$\hat{\bar p}_t=(B_tG_t)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{B_t}k_{ti}$ and
$\overline{\mathrm{ZVF}}_t=B_t^{-1}\sum_i\mathbf1\{k_{ti}\in\{0,G_t\}\}$.
For latent prompt probabilities, convexity implies
$h_G(\mathbb E_i[p_i])-\mathbb E_i[h_G(p_i)]\le0$.
The finite-sample expectation adds the nonnegative term
$\mathbb E[h_G(\hat{\bar p}_t)]-h_G(\bar p_t)$, so a negative sign is not
implied by the population inequality alone. Recomputing the 480 aggregate rows
with source \texttt{groupsize\_zvf\_sweep} in
\nolinkurl{experiments/results/zvf_contrastive_yield.tsv} gives $-0.0668$
($-0.067$ rounded), across $G\in\{2,4,8,16\}$ with 120 rows per $G$.
The earlier $-0.0703$ is withdrawn: its subset/weighting provenance is not
reconciled. Raw within-step group structure and $B_t$ are not established by
these aggregate inputs; neither a finite-sample null nor a causal dependence
mechanism is identified. The historical step-iid interval is withheld.

Observed ZVF is $2.9\times$ the homogeneous-binomial value.
The beta-binomial is an unconstrained two-parameter maximum-likelihood fit
(Nelder--Mead on log parameters); its implied mean $a/(a+b)=0.690$ is not
fixed at the empirical $0.694$. Its ZVF agreement within $0.012$ is an
in-sample descriptive fit, not held-out prediction or mechanism identification.
ICC denotes marginal intra-class correlation $1/(a+b+1)$ under that model.
The unpooled Pearson discrepancy is approximately 8119, but its sparse
expected tail counts (about $0.046$ and $0.83$ at $k=0,1$) invalidate an
asymptotic chi-square test; no corresponding significance claim is retained.
Different seed problem sets do not identify conditional dependence separately
from latent prompt heterogeneity. Exact unique problem overlap, split and
decoding provenance must be audited before stronger generalization claims.

\paragraph{Prospective test (not executed).}
With $M\ge G$ independent calibration rollouts and $m_i$ successes for prompt
$i$, predict held-out ZVF using
$\widehat h_i=[\binom{m_i}{G}+\binom{M-m_i}{G}]/\binom{M}{G}$
(with binomial coefficients zero when the upper argument is below $G$).
This is unbiased for $h_G(p_i)$ under conditional i.i.d.\ sampling, unlike
$h_G(m_i/M)$. Average over prompts and assess held-out error with
prompt-clustered uncertainty. No results of this prospective protocol are claimed.

\section{Relation to prior work}
Nothing here is new. ZVF is equivalent, up to aggregation granularity, to the
Advantage Collapse Rate \citep{he2026advantage}. \citet{nie2026gradstarvation}
prove that the true degeneracy rate exceeds the i.i.d.\ Bernoulli prediction
by Jensen's inequality, which is the corrected reading above; DARA
\citep{zheng2026dara} uses the complementary active-group density in
multi-reward RL. DAPO's dynamic sampling \citep{yu2025dapo}, GRESO
\citep{zhang2026greso}, VCRL \citep{jiang2025vcrl}, and online difficulty
filtering \citep{bae2025online} act on degenerate groups. The only
contribution of this note is the retraction and the GSM8K numbers.

\section{Non-claims}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No claim that the Bernoulli model is ``sign-wrong'' (withdrawn).
  \item No claim that ZVF predicts held-out collapse or ranks trainers; the
    earlier pooled Spearman $\rho=0.27$ is withdrawn (17 finite cells, 9 of
    them simulated, mixed outcome definitions).
  \item No use of \texttt{variance\_mitigation.tsv} or any simulated panel.
\end{itemize}
