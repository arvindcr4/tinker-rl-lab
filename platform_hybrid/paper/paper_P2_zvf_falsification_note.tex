\begin{abstract}
Group-relative policy optimization (GRPO) trains on groups of $G$ rollouts per
prompt. The zero-variance fraction (ZVF) is the share of groups whose rewards
are constant---the groups that contribute no within-group learning signal.
Under an i.i.d.\ Bernoulli success model with success probability $p$,
$\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}=p^G+(1-p)^G$. We show that this sampling model is
systematically wrong on measured groups, and that the \emph{sign} of the error
depends on the task regime. On Qwen3-8B/GSM8K ($G{=}8$, 600 groups) the mean
divergence
$\delta=\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}-\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is
$+0.1224$ with bootstrap 95\% CI $[+0.1115,+0.1337]$. On synthetic arithmetic
with Qwen2.5-0.5B ($G\in\{2,4,8,16\}$, 459 logged steps) the same divergence is
$-0.0703$ with CI $[-0.0818,-0.0590]$. Both intervals exclude zero; the signs
are opposite. Consequence: Bernoulli-derived iso-$G$ sizing and prompt filters
are miscalibrated in both directions. We do not claim that ZVF predicts
training failure, rank algorithms, or support a controller.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}

For a group of $G$ binary rewards $(r_1,\ldots,r_G)\in\{0,1\}^G$, write
\[
\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}
  = \mathbf{1}\{r_1=\cdots=r_G\},
\qquad
\hat p = G^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^G r_i,
\qquad
\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}(\hat p,G)=\hat p^G+(1-\hat p)^G.
\]
The per-group divergence is
$\delta=\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}(\hat p,G)-\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}$.
Under the i.i.d.\ Bernoulli model, $\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{obs}}]
=\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}(p,G)$, so $\mathbb{E}[\delta]=0$. Empirically
$\delta$ is not mean-zero.

\paragraph{Lemma (algebraic identity under the model).}
If rewards are i.i.d.\ Bernoulli$(p)$, then
$\mathrm{pass@}G-p^G=1-\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}(p,G)$.
This is an identity, not an empirical discovery. We therefore do not report
``verification to machine precision on $N$ tasks'' as a result.

\section{Result}

Bootstrap the mean of group-level (GSM8K) or step-level (arithmetic) $\delta$
with a fixed RNG seed (\texttt{Random(20260802)} for GSM8K; 5000 resamples for
arithmetic).

On real GSM8K groups the i.i.d.\ model \emph{over}-predicts zero variance
(positive $\delta$): groups are more mixed than Bernoulli$(\hat p)$ expects.
On saturated synthetic arithmetic the model \emph{under}-predicts zero variance
(negative $\delta$): observed groups are flatter than the Bernoulli formula.
The opposite signs matter operationally: a planner that sizes $G$ from
$\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}$ will request too little diversity on reasoning
traces and too much on easy arithmetic.

\paragraph{Do not pool ZVF across tasks.}
A single cross-experiment correlation that mixes GSM8K reasoning with synthetic
arithmetic (or pools failed verifiers with live runs) is not an estimand we
report. Prior pooled Spearman figures from heterogeneous cells are retired
with the 46-page draft.
