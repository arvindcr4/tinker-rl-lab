\paragraph{Evidence scope after correction.}
This version retains the direct 16-run length-trace audit: five seeds per
algorithm on arithmetic and three per algorithm on capped GSM8K-CoT.
It does not retain the pooled benchmark ANOVAs, iteration-history
cross-correlations, shock/OU damping analyses, window-level significance
claims, efficiency inversions, or proposed controllers as executed results.
Those analyses require estimator and provenance reconciliation; they do not
support the bounded descriptive result below. Mean step-level lengths cannot
identify correctness-conditioned length effects or a causal verbosity mechanism.
