\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:p1-discussion}
The most useful result here is a boundary on what can be claimed. On this
benchmark, neither mean GRPO training reward $\bar{R}$ nor fitted endpoint ceiling $R_{\max}$ exhibits a measurable, monotone dependence
on model size, and no universal saturation exponent is identifiable; the honest
description is a local, stack-conditioned taxonomy of endpoint behavior rather than a
Chinchilla-style power law in $N$. Several methodological limitations qualify even this modest
statement:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item \textbf{Step-1 pre-saturation on GSM8K.} Modern instruction-tuned checkpoints (`Qwen3.5-4B`, `Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct`, `DeepSeek-V3.1`, `Kimi-K2-Thinking`, `Qwen3-30B-MoE-Inst`, `Qwen3-235B-MoE`) already achieve $R(1) \in [0.75, 1.00]$ on GSM8K at step~1. Consequently, fitting $R(t) = R_{\max}(1 - e^{-\lambda t})$ with $R(0)=0$ forces $\lambda$ to the optimizer upper bound ($\lambda = 10$), and even with a free baseline offset $c \in [0,1]$ the remaining headroom $(1 - c)$ is smaller than the binomial rollout noise floor $\sigma_{\text{step}} \in [0.074, 0.119]$ at $G \in \{8, 16\}$ over $T \le 30$ steps. Evaluating post-training scaling laws on harder, unsaturated reasoning benchmarks (such as AIME, OlympiadBench, or competition coding) over $T > 200$ steps is essential before concluding whether GRPO rate constants scale with parameter count.
  \item \textbf{Single-seed frontier API traces and summary-reconstructed diagnostics.} The frontier-scale anchors are single-seed \textsc{Tinker} API runs and must be read as descriptive case studies rather than powered multi-seed replications. In addition, the exploratory 12-anchor phase-score and piecewise-slope audits in \S\ref{sec:scaling-law-iter33}, \S\ref{sec:scaling-law-iter37} (synthetic branch), and \S\ref{par:scaling-iter65} operate on traces reconstructed from six per-anchor summary statistics rather than raw step logs, and four extended-pool probes (`Qwen3-32B`, `Qwen3.5-27B`, `Qwen3-30B-MoE`, `Qwen3-30B-MoE-Inst`) have short horizons ($n_{\text{steps}} \in \{3, 4, 5\}$). We therefore restrict inferential claims to the raw per-step trajectories ($n_{\text{steps}} \in \{20, 30, 40\}$) and treat the 12-anchor reconstructed batteries as exploratory sensitivity checks.
  \item \textbf{Endogeneity of post-hoc capability clustering and variance starvation.} Partitioning anchors by Ward $k=2$ clustering on $R_{\max}$ (\S\ref{par:scaling-iter133}) is a descriptive variance decomposition of the outcome distribution rather than an exogenous causal predictor. However, the same partition aligns closely with exogenous pre-RL attributes---instruction tuning and step-1 baseline accuracy $R(1) \ge 0.50$---and with rollout-level zero-variance fraction (\textsc{zvf}). Disentangling parameter scale from within-group contrast starvation requires active curriculum or rollout filtering, such as dynamic sampling in DAPO~\citep{yu2025dapo}, unbiased advantage normalization in Dr.\ GRPO~\citep{tong2025drgrpo}, or online difficulty filtering~\citep{bae2025online, jiang2025vcrl}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Submission scope.}
For a workshop-short / limits-audit venue we claim only the descriptive flat
slope, geometric falsification of the clean three-phase hypothesis, and
non-identifiability of a universal saturation exponent on the released
single-seed-heavy corpus. We do \emph{not} claim a powered multi-seed
cross-scale test; that remains future work and is not required for the
identifiability boundary already established here.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:p1-conclusion}
We asked whether GRPO post-training obeys a clean scaling law in model size and
found, on a controlled cross-library and cross-scale benchmark, that it does not---at least not
one identifiable from $2.4$ orders of magnitude ($4$B--$1$T parameters) of short-horizon GSM8K evidence. The
cross-scale mean-reward and ceiling slopes are statistically flat, a universal three-phase saturation
hypothesis is geometrically falsified, and the Nemotron-3-Super-120B-A12B LatentMoE trace is best treated
as a format-collapse structural outlier ($55\%$ zero-reward steps). What survives is a taxonomic, endpoint-level
description of the ceiling, dominated by pre-existing instruction-following capability and rollout contrast rather
than an added $\log_{10} N$ term, and a concrete experimental roadmap:
re-plotting post-training scaling on usable contrastive compute $C_{\text{eff}}$, extending horizons beyond $T^\star > 200$ steps on unsaturated benchmarks, and running multi-seed matched-budget evaluations. The released traces, telemetry, adapters, and scripts make that follow-up directly reproducible.
