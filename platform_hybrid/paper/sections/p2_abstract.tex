% Pillar-2 (ZVF) standalone abstract
\begin{abstract}
In the submitted GRPO runner, a prompt has a zero centered reward-contrast term
when all $G$ sampled completions receive identical rewards: the group-centered
advantages are zero. This does not assert that the total gradient vanishes. The
\emph{Zero-Variance Fraction} (ZVF)---the share of groups in this
degenerate state---is therefore a natural diagnostic of when binary, verifiable
rewards stop training the policy. We study ZVF as one pillar of \ours{}, a
benchmark of 70+ RL runs across seven libraries and five model families
($0.6$B--$\sim$671B) on GSM8K, HumanEval, and tool-use tasks. We characterize ZVF
descriptively and, deliberately, decline to promote it to a causal or
incrementally predictive statistic. Three stored GSM8K group tensors recompute
their reported ZVF values exactly (0.130, 0.190, and 0.155; pooled 0.1583).
An earlier pooled ZVF--outcome correlation ($\rho\approx0.27$) is withdrawn: only
17 of its 23 cells had a finite ZVF, 9 of those were simulation rows, and the
outcome column mixed held-out accuracy with training reward. The same ZVF can
describe mastery or incapacity. Under conditionally i.i.d.\ rollouts, batch ZVF
equals $\mathbb{E}_i[h_G(p_i)]$ with $h_G(p)=p^G+(1-p)^G$, so its excess over
$h_G(\bar p)$ measures prompt-difficulty dispersion: on GSM8K the observed $0.158$
is $2.9\times$ the homogeneous value $0.054$ and a beta-binomial fit (intra-prompt
correlation $0.16$) predicts $0.170$. ZVF is equivalent to the Advantage Collapse
Rate~\cite{he2026advantage}, and the Jensen excess was shown
in~\cite{nie2026gradstarvation}; we claim no new diagnostic. A separate variance-mitigation table is a declared simulation
projection used for sensitivity analysis; it is not evidence that any named
method mitigates failure. Under the stated iid-Bernoulli model, the relation
$\operatorname{pass@}G-p^G=1-\mathrm{ZVF}$ follows algebraically. It identifies
ZVF with contrastive group yield under that model, not with held-out improvement
or causal intervention value. We release code, logs, and the three per-group
reward tensors used for the measured recomputation.

\end{abstract}
\paragraph{August 2026 evidence boundary.} ZVF is a descriptive accounting
quantity. Homogeneous rewards zero the submitted centered reward-contrast term,
not necessarily a total gradient in objectives with KL, clipping, or auxiliary loss.
