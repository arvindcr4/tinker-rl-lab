\section{The ZVF Diagnostic: Results}
\label{sec:p2-results}
We evaluate ZVF along three axes: its behaviour under variants that act on
zero-variance groups, its association with training failure, and its coupling to
nuisance variables. In the measured run matrix, the only collapse cells with
logged ZVF are two $G{=}1$ tool-use cells whose ZVF is $1$ by construction, so we
claim no association between ZVF and collapse. An earlier
revision also reported $\rho \approx 0.27$ with final held-out accuracy; that
figure is \emph{withdrawn} (17 finite cells, only 8 from measured runs, mixed
outcome definitions; Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-experiment:corr}). We make no
claim that ZVF predicts outcome. Likewise withdrawn is the ``anti-herding''
divergence $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}=+0.1224$ and every quantity derived from it
(iso-$G$ corrections, $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$ triggers in later sections): when
$\mathrm{ZVF}_{\mathrm{iid}}$ is evaluated at the same group's plug-in $\hat p$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{div}}\ge0$ for every group by construction (and is $+0.105$ in
expectation under exactly i.i.d.\ rollouts), and when it is evaluated at a pooled
$\bar p$, the population gap is $\le0$ by Jensen's inequality, offset in
finite samples by a positive plug-in bias.
Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-source-falsification} gives the corrected reading: ZVF
is a convex functional of the prompt-difficulty distribution.

The subsections that follow develop this picture. We first compare GRPO with
four variants that act on zero-variance groups or on GRPO's normalisation in a
matched 8-seed Qwen3-8B/GSM8K campaign, where held-out accuracy is unchanged
across arms although about seven in ten groups are zero-variance in every arm that
keeps them. We then summarise ZVF across the measured run matrix with a
ZVF-free failure taxonomy. We connect ZVF to
the gradient through the utilization identity $\mathrm{GU} = 1 - \mathrm{ZVF}$, and
audit its coupling to reward sparsity, group size, and baseline accuracy so that
its correlations are read as descriptive rather than causal. The final subsection
presents an external frontier-model cross-examination that isolates ZVF's core
defect---it assigns the same value to a mastered prompt and an unsolvable one---and
proposes magnitude- and sign-aware replacements (a pairwise-contrast density and a
learning-adjusted rollout quality score).
