\section{Group Size: Results}
\label{sec:p3-results}
We report three measured settings and one analytical audit. On a saturated
addition task (Section~\ref{sec:group-size}) held-out accuracy is flat across
$G\in\{2,\dots,16\}$, with $G{=}2$ retaining $100.3\%$ of $G{=}16$. At equal
compute on an unsaturated multiplication task, and for four Qwen3-8B/GSM8K
adapters (Section~\ref{sec:group-size-scaleup}), larger groups again do not raise
held-out accuracy, including at $G{=}32$. In every setting the zero-variance
fraction falls with $G$ and exceeds the homogeneous closed form, the signature
of prompt-difficulty heterogeneity. Section~\ref{sec:group-size-iter27} gives the
seed-level statistics for the saturated sweep. We then make the preference view
explicit: the group-centred GRPO update equals an all-pairs contrast over the
group, so zero-variance groups contribute nothing and $G$ sets how many endogenous
preference pairs a prompt supplies; this identity is an exact algebraic rewriting
whose direct gradient-vector validation remains future work. The final subsections
add an external frontier-model cross-examination, including a $2{\times}2$ design
that would separate variance-reduction from contrast-density, and small-scale
robustness checks.
