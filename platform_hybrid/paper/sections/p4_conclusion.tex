\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:p4-discussion}
The retained audit concerns average logged length in two named short-horizon
setups, not a universal verbosity-trap test. The GSM8K-CoT completion cap is
part of the estimand. Correctness-stratified rollout lengths and a controlled
uncapped comparison are needed to investigate causal length bias. Absence of
a rising mean under a cap is not evidence of practical equivalence or of
zero length-mediated effect. Multiple metrics from the same seed trajectory
are not independent replicates; the seed budgets are five and three, not
the number of windows or training steps.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:p4-conclusion}
Mean completion lengths decline in these recorded capped, short-horizon
runs. This descriptive observation neither refutes Dr.\,GRPO's theoretical
normalization correction nor establishes general held-out superiority.
The earlier claim that a non-significant permutation result was decisive,
and the proposed causal mediation and controller claims, are not retained.
