\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:p4-discussion}
The retained audit concerns average logged length in two named short-horizon
setups, not a universal verbosity-trap test. The GSM8K-CoT completion cap is
part of the estimand. The correctness-stratified rerun
(Table~\ref{tab:p4-length-stratified}) finds no widening incorrect-minus-correct
length gap at 30--40 steps, including at a 512-token cap that few rollouts
reach, and the rarely capped 200-step runs (Table~\ref{tab:p4-long-horizon}) show
the gap narrowing rather than widening. These remain three-seed, 1.5B-model
runs; larger models, longer horizons and a causal test of length bias are
still untested. Absence of
a rising mean under a cap is not evidence of practical equivalence or of
zero length-mediated effect. Multiple metrics from the same seed trajectory
are not independent replicates; the seed budgets are five and three, not
the number of windows or training steps.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:p4-conclusion}
Mean completion lengths decline in these recorded runs, from the capped
30--40-step runs to the 200-step runs at a 1024-token cap. This descriptive observation neither refutes Dr.\,GRPO's theoretical
normalization correction nor establishes general held-out superiority.
The earlier claim that a non-significant permutation result was decisive,
and the proposed causal mediation and controller claims, are not retained.
