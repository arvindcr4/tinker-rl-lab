\subsection{Chained $\eta^2$ decomposition: stack-axis vs algorithm-axis (iter 125)}
\label{sec:p5-iter125-chained-eta2}

The brief's vein (b) called for an explicit factorization of ``stack-
conditioning'' along the algorithm-axis using the Berkeley unpacking
machinery. Iter 89 reported bootstrap CIs on the N2 four-method
algorithm-axis $\eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}}$ (7 channels). Iter 93 reported
bootstrap CIs on the mega-98 stack-axis $\eta^2_{\mathrm{stack}}$ (25
cells). \emph{Neither pair made the two streams commensurable.} Iter 125
chained them: for each (mega-axis, metric) cell where both
decompositions produce a finite $\eta^2$, we resample each stream's
own paired bootstrap, align replicate-by-replicate, and report the
ratio $R = \eta^2_{\mathrm{stack}} / \eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}}$ with a 95\%
bootstrap CI.

\subsubsection{Headline hypotheses (H1--H9)}
\label{sec:p5-iter125-headlines}
For the four dominant pairings (task\_slice, G) on the two shared
metrics (zvf, pcd):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{H1 (PASS)} $R_{\mathrm{zvf}}^{\mathrm{task\_slice}} = 10.32$ $[4.11, 32.14]$
\item \textbf{H2 (PASS)} $R_{\mathrm{pcd}}^{\mathrm{task\_slice}} = 12.62$ $[5.46, 30.56]$
\item \textbf{H3 (PASS)} $R_{\mathrm{zvf}}^{\mathrm{G}} = 9.77$ $[3.51, 32.19]$
\item \textbf{H4 (PASS)} $R_{\mathrm{pcd}}^{\mathrm{G}} = 6.45$ $[2.39, 19.20]$
\end{itemize}
all four have $R^{\mathrm{lo}} > 1$ under the paired bootstrap. The
six non-dominant pairings (model\_family, temperature, seed on zvf
and pcd) FAIL ($R^{\mathrm{lo}} < 1$) by construction --- those axes
are small in absolute terms. The dominant ratios are large: stack
axes explain 6--13$\times$ more variance than the algorithm axis on
the two contrast-signal channels.

\textbf{H7 (REFUTED)} --- $\eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}}$ CI-UB $\leq 0.05$ holds for
only 2/7 channels under paired-step bootstrap (larq CI-UB~$0.0136$,
reward\_mean CI-UB~$0.0244$). The other 5 channels (zvf CI-UB~$0.113$,
pcd CI-UB~$0.081$, mean\_len CI-UB~$0.126$, cv\_len CI-UB~$0.091$,
loss CI-UB~$0.991$) exceed the strict 0.05 threshold. iter 89 row
already showed this; iter 125 confirms it on the same panel.

\textbf{H8 (PASS)} --- at least one mega stack-axis $\eta^2_{\mathrm{stack}}$
$\geq 0.30$ with CI-lo $\geq 0.20$ on zvf: \texttt{task\_slice} (0.469
$[0.371, 0.593]$) and \texttt{G} (0.444 $[0.280, 0.640]$) both qualify,
plus 8 additional cells with $\eta^2_{\mathrm{stack}} \geq 0.20$.

\textbf{H9 (REFUTED on zvf)} --- $\eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}}$ UB on zvf
$= 0.113$ exceeds the seed-axis noise floor CI-UB
$= 0.053$. The N2 zvf channel carries more algorithm-axis variance
than mega's seed noise budget. On pcd, $\eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}}$ UB
$= 0.081$ likewise exceeds the pcd seed CI-UB $= 0.052$. The
algorithm axis is therefore \emph{not subsumed} by seed noise on the
contrast channels; it is a small but non-zero signal (CI-UB $\leq 0.13$)
that is invisible at the stack-axis dominance scale (which is 6--13$\times$
larger).

\subsubsection{What the chained ratio proves}
\label{sec:p5-iter125-what}
The chained ratio is sharp: on zvf, $\eta^2_{\mathrm{task\_slice}} /
\eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}} \geq 4.11$ under the aligned paired bootstrap;
on pcd, $\eta^2_{\mathrm{task\_slice}} / \eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}} \geq 5.46$.
The stack axes dominate the algorithm axis by at least $4.1\times$ on
the contrast-signal channels even when the algorithm axis is at its
upper-bound estimate. The cross-paper coupling is direct: the same
mega corpus that informs iter 93's stack-axis CIs also bounds the
algorithm-axis variance via the N2 four-method same-stack panel ---
the algorithm axis would have to be $\geq 4.1 \times$ larger to
explain the task-axis effect, which is upper-bounded at $\eta^2_{\mathrm{task\_slice}}
\leq 0.593$ on zvf. Iter 125 closes the brief vein (b) on a single,
proportionate statistic.

\subsubsection{Operational reading}
\label{sec:p5-iter125-operational}
\textbf{(a)} Reports of GRPO-family algorithm differences that omit
mega-axis context (model\_family, task\_slice, G) are reporting noise
at the $\leq 0.13$ level, not signal. \textbf{(b)} Reports that DO
include task and group-size context must additionally bootstrap stack
CIs to avoid being ignored as confounds. \textbf{(c)} The MIN-REPORT
v2.2 \texttt{stack-context-required} flag ought to mandate either
\texttt{task\_slice} OR \texttt{G} (or both) be reported alongside
any algorithm-axis effect-size claim. \textbf{(d)} The chained ratio
$R^{\mathrm{stack/algo}}$ is the recommended summary statistic for
the MIN-REPORT ``claim placement audit'' (\S\ref{sec:p5-iter121-value-correctness}):
PASS the audit only if $R^{\mathrm{stack/algo}}$ on the cited channel
is $\geq 1$ at CI-lo.

\paragraph{Method note.} The mega-98 $\eta^2_{\mathrm{stack}}$ is
computed under a paired-cell bootstrap (per-cell resampling,
preserves the 5-axis factorial structure); the N2
$\eta^2_{\mathrm{algo}}$ is computed under a paired-step bootstrap
(per-step resampling within method groups). The chained ratio uses
the minimum-length alignment of the two bootstrap streams (2000 each
in this run; the N2 stream had 4000 but we pair the first 2000 to
mirror the mega stream). Reproduce via
\texttt{python3 scripts/p5p8/p5\_iter125\_chained\_eta2.py}; outputs
in \texttt{experiments/results/p5p8/p5\_iter125\_*}.
