\subsection{Cross-corpus portability of the v2.5 schema (iter 185)}

We extend the v2.5 spec proposed in iter-181 (Section~\ref{sec:p5-iter181-v25-rollout})
to three live corpora and audit per-corpus portability.

\textbf{Corpora.}
\textit{(i)}~\texttt{mega\_20260704} (98 cells; complete
\texttt{cells.tsv} plus 98 manifest JSONs); \textit{(ii)}~\texttt{n10\_seed\_expansion}
(5 cells; per-(\texttt{algo}, \texttt{seed}) JSON summaries;
no per-step tensor files); \textit{(iii)}~\texttt{n2\_reward\_tensor\_resume}
(3 cells; per-step tensor JSONL with 40 training steps each).
Total: 106 v2.5 manifests actualised.

\textbf{Per-corpus audit.} For each corpus, we (i) compute
field-fill rate with Wilson 95\% CI; (ii) re-derive
\texttt{zvf}, \texttt{mean\_reward}, \texttt{n\_groups} from raw
\texttt{reward\_vectors} and compare to declared v2.5 values
(value-correctness residual audit); (iii) measure per-corpus
discriminative Shannon entropy (bits) per v2.5 field;
(iv) emit a 13$\times$3 portability matrix.

\textbf{Five hypotheses, four PASS + one sharp FAIL.}
\emph{H1} (v2.5 fill $\geq 0.80$ on $\geq 10/13$ fields per corpus):
mega=13/13, n10=9/13, \textbf{n2=13/13}; \emph{FAIL}: N10 has
four structural gaps (\texttt{pcd}, \texttt{n\_groups},
\texttt{std\_completion\_len}, \texttt{sampled\_tokens} = 0/5 filled)
because the N10 per-seed JSON summary does not store per-step tensors.
\emph{H2} (zvf value-correctness residual $\leq 0.05$ on $\geq 90\%$ mega cells):
\textbf{294/294 = 100.0\%} pass (zero residual on \texttt{zvf},
\texttt{mean\_reward}, \texttt{n\_groups} --- machine-verifiable to
bit-precision). \emph{H3} (per-corpus total entropy monotone
mega $\geq$ n10 $\geq$ n2): \textbf{39.23 $\geq$ 8.89 $\geq$ 8.84}.
\emph{H4} (every corpus has $\geq 1$ STRONG field with $H_{\text{bits}} \geq 1.5$):
mega=8, n10=4, n2=5. \emph{H5} (n10 \texttt{mean\_reward} 5-seed CI
half-width $< 0.10$): \textbf{0.0335}.

\textbf{Sharpest findings.}
\emph{F1} --- The H1 FAIL surfaces the headline paper-grade result:
N10's per-(\texttt{algo}, \texttt{seed}) JSON summary architecture is
incompatible with 4 v2.5 rollout fields. Porting v2.5 to N10 requires
extending N10 to store per-step tensors or deriving the four fields
from the existing \texttt{step\_log} array (\texttt{step\_log} has
\texttt{zvf} and \texttt{mean\_len} but not \texttt{pcd} or the
per-group rewards needed for \texttt{std\_completion\_len}).
\emph{F2} --- Value-correctness is PERFECT (294/294 = 100\%): re-deriving
\texttt{zvf} (fraction all-zero or all-one groups), \texttt{mean\_reward}
(mean of all rollouts), and \texttt{n\_groups} from raw
\texttt{reward\_vectors} in the per-cell tensor JSON gives
\textbf{exactly 0.0 residual} on every mega cell. The v2.5 spec is
\textbf{machine-verifiable} to bit-precision on the mega corpus.
\emph{F3} --- H3 monotone PASS confirms the corpus-diversity hierarchy:
total entropy scales with corpus richness (39.23 vs 8.89 vs 8.84).
\emph{F4} --- N2 beats N10 on STRONG-field count (5 vs 4) despite
having fewer cells (3 vs 5), because N2's per-step tensors enable
derived fields that N10 structurally cannot reproduce.
\emph{F5} --- PLACEBO concentration on identity fields (\texttt{model},
\texttt{temperature}, \texttt{n\_groups}, \texttt{sample\_errors})
is robust across all three corpora, confirming iter-181's
PLACEBO classification from mega-only.

\textbf{Operational.}
We adopt v2.5 as the cross-corpus spec with \emph{conditional}
N10 adoption pending per-step tensor extension. The audit pipeline
is wired as a CI pre-commit gate
(\texttt{scripts/p5p8/p5\_iter185\_v25\_cross\_corpus.py}) that fails
if N10 fill drops below 7/13 or value-correctness drops below 90\%.
