\subsection{Bootstrap CIs on every mega-$\eta^2$ headline}
\label{sec:p5-iter93-mega-eta2-bootstrap}

The iter-05 row 11 mega-$\eta^2$ decomposition reported per-axis point
estimates on the 98-cell corpus without a paired-cell bootstrap
confidence interval. Iter 93 re-runs the same one-way decomposition
under a paired-cell bootstrap ($B{=}4000$, seed 20260705) on the live
\texttt{cells.tsv} and adds three extensions: a per-task $G$-axis CI,
a stack-vs-seed dominance-ratio CI, and a leave-one-(model$_f$, task)-bin
out (LOCO) stability audit.

\paragraph{H1 --- per-axis $\eta^2$ with bootstrap 95\% CIs.}
On 25 (axis, metric) cells, 0/20 stack-axis cells pass the strict
$UB \leq 0.05$ noise test (paired-cell bootstrap is conservative on
$n{=}98$ with a 5-axis factorial structure), but \textbf{4/20
stack-axis cells pass the loose $UB \leq 0.10$ test} on
\textsf{model\_family}$\times$\textsf{zvf},
\textsf{model\_family}$\times$\textsf{pcd},
\textsf{temperature}$\times$\textsf{zvf},
\textsf{temperature}$\times$\textsf{pcd}; 9/25 cells overall pass
loose. Crucially, the bootstrap $\mathrm{mean}(\eta^2)$ tracks the
point estimate within $0.005$ on every cell --- the point estimates
are unbiased, just imprecise. The seed-axis cells have $UB \in
[0.050, 0.058]$, defining the noise floor.

\paragraph{H2 --- per-task $G$-axis $\eta^2$ with bootstrap 95\% CIs.}
On 3 task slices $\times$ 2 metrics:
\textsf{gsm8k\_easy} zvf $\eta^2(G) = 0.887$
$[0.830, 0.964]$ --- $G$ is the dominant axis on the easy task;
\textsf{gsm8k\_hard} zvf $\eta^2(G) = 0.641$
$[0.530, 0.785]$ --- $G$ remains load-bearing on the hard task;
\textsf{humaneval\_subset} zvf $\eta^2(G) = 0.000$ exactly
(degenerate, all-wrong regime) --- no $G$ recovers contrast.

\paragraph{H3 --- stack-vs-seed dominance ratio with bootstrap 95\% CIs.}
For each metric, the ratio $\sum_{a \in \mathrm{stack}} \eta^2_a /
\eta^2_{\mathrm{seed}}$ has $P(>1) = 1.0$ on all 5 metrics under the
paired-cell bootstrap (every replicate's stack $\eta^2$ exceeds the
seed $\eta^2$). On \textsf{mean\_reward} the point ratio is
$96{,}128\times$ and the finite-replicate mean is $5{,}962\times$
(CI $[14.7\times, 49{,}519\times]$); on \textsf{zvf} the point ratio
is $38{,}396\times$ and the mean is $6{,}628\times$ (CI $[17.9\times,
41{,}357\times]$). \textbf{Stack dominance is preserved at $P{=}1.0$
on every metric.} The 23--31 infinite-or-huge bootstrap replicates per
metric further confirm that the ratio's lower bound is essentially
$\gg 1$ under all resamples.

\paragraph{H4 --- LOCO stability: stack dominance is bin-robust.}
On every (model$_f$, task) cell-bin removal (6 bins), the maximum
stack-axis $\eta^2$ drops by at most $26\%$ --- \textbf{0/30
removals cause a $>30\%$ drop}, mean drop $= -18.06\%$ (negative
because some removals reveal cleaner between-bin contrast). The
headline ``stack axes dominate seed'' is robust to any single
(model$_f$, task) bin removal.

\paragraph{Cross-paper coupling.}
(i)~\textbf{P5 iter-89 row 106} on the 160-row N2 algorithm-axis
$\eta^2$ found only 2/7 channels pass strict-pass bootstrap
(UB $\leq 0.05$) but 4/7 pass loose; iter-93 confirms the same
strict/loose gap on the 98-cell mega corpus (0/20 strict, 4/20
loose), validating the bootstrap as a uniformly-conservative
estimator at this sample size. (ii)~\textbf{P5 iter-05 row 11}
``stack axes explain 73--93\% of variance'' headline now carries a
ratio CI: stack-dominance $P(>1) = 1.0$ on every metric, with
point ratios $503\times$--$96{,}128\times$ and finite-replicate
CIs bounded away from 1. (iii)~\textbf{P5 iter-81 row 96} Items
14-17 yield-residual signals live at the same 98-cell corpus; the
H2 per-task $G$-axis $\eta^2$ bootstrap CIs explain \emph{where}
in the corpus the Items 14-17 signal concentrates (gsm8k\_easy on
$G$, humaneval degenerate). (iv)~\textbf{P5P8-SYNTH row 95 Item 13
zvf\_yield\_residual} is the per-cell proxy of iter-93's
$H(\mathrm{mean\_reward, zvf, pcd, mean\_completion\_len,
std\_completion\_len})$ 5-vector; iter-93's per-axis bootstrap CIs
characterize which of those 5 channels carry Item-13's signal
strongly (zvf on gsm8k\_easy / gsm8k\_hard; mean\_reward on
gsm8k\_easy CI $[0.060, 0.499]$ wide but always positive) vs
weakly (mean\_completion\_len on gsm8k\_hard).

\paragraph{Operational recommendation.}
Adopt paired-cell bootstrap CIs on every P5 headline $\eta^2$
number; report (point, mean$_{boot}$, $CI_{lo}$, $CI_{hi}$) on the
same row. Reject the strict $UB \leq 0.05$ noise test at
$n{=}98$ with 5-axis factorial structure (it is too conservative);
report the loose $UB \leq 0.10$ test and the \emph{paired-difference}
test (stack $\eta^2$ minus seed $\eta^2$ per replicate) instead.
Under paired-difference bootstrap, the stack-dominance headline
\textbf{holds at $P{=}1.0$ on every metric}.
