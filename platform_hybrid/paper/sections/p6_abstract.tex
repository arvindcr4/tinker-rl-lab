\begin{abstract}
The deltas that define GRPO variants---DAPO's asymmetric clip, dynamic sampling,
and token-level loss; Dr.~GRPO's dropped length and std normalization; GSPO's
sequence-level importance ratio---live in paper appendices and framework
defaults, not in any machine-readable form. The consequence, documented across
the \ours{} companion papers, is that an algorithm \emph{label} underdetermines the
\emph{update rule} actually executed. In our audits, two runs both labelled
``GRPO, Qwen3-8B, GSM8K'' reached final reward $85.6\%$ and $5.0\%$ ($17\times$);
$64\%$ of that gap is an unreported checkpoint switch (Base for the $85.6\%$
run, Instruct for the $5.0\%$ run), and the remainder is confounded by reward function, prompt template,
decoding, batch shape, data subset, and loss implementation (the same exhibit as
the companion MIN-REPORT-RL position paper). Separately, a managed-runtime arm labelled ``dapo'' was an
advantage-clip variant with no dynamic sampling. These audits motivate \textsc{GRPO-Registry}, a resource that makes
such discrepancies queryable rather than presenting a new performance result:
(i) a JSON schema whose stack records carry the seven-field run-manifest block
of the eight-item MIN-REPORT-RL standard (loss form; reference policy and KL handling;
sampler/backend/precision \textbf{including base-checkpoint identity: revision/hash};
per-step ZVF/GU telemetry; group-size schedule;
held-out split; decontamination and parser probes), with held-out pass@$k$
curves recorded as the eighth, evaluation-report requirement, plus per-component
variant-delta status; (ii) 18 variant-delta records, three of them (DAPO, Dr.~GRPO, GSPO) verified
against their source papers; (iii) 28 seed stack entries, 17 derived from
this benchmark's released config dumps (two are dry-run placeholders), an open
Colab trainer audit, a managed-runtime head-to-head, a same-stack four-method
run, and two W\&B runs, plus 11 risk-index fixtures that describe no training
run; and (iv) a reference CLI implementing registry
queries, a 0--100 MIN-REPORT auditor badge, and \texttt{stackdiff}, which maps a
pair of entries to a flip-risk level (R0--R5) computed only from fields known
on both sides, plus a coverage score and an underspecified flag
(Section~\ref{sec:p6-query}). The idea that undisclosed implementation details
move RL results is old \citep{henderson2018deep, engstrom2020implementation};
what we add is a machine-readable record and a diff over it. The registry, schema, and CLI are
released with the benchmark.
\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This is a \emph{machine-readable resource paper}, not a second independent
ranking of GRPO variants. The $17\times$ same-label exhibit is intentionally
shared with the companion MIN-REPORT-RL position paper and is cited
here only to motivate the schema; it is a confounded observation, not a
backend effect, and we do not re-claim it as empirical science. The load-bearing contribution is the seven-field stack schema, seed
catalog, auditor badge, and \texttt{stackdiff} tooling. Growing the catalog
beyond the seed entries is future community work, not a precondition for
releasing the schema and seeds.
\end{abstract}
\paragraph{Evidence boundary.} Registry entries report stated
provenance and conflicts; they do not reconcile unresolved exact-ID/model
conflicts or validate nominal algorithm comparisons.
