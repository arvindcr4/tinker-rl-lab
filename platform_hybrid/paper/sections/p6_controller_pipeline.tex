\paragraph{Controller predictions: closing the registry--controller pipeline.}
The iter-66 row 77 \texttt{measured\_yield\_residual} block reports a
\emph{measurement}; the iter-67 row 78 controller counterfactual reports an
\emph{intervention}. A downstream consumer who reads only the registry should
not have to re-run the iter-67 script to ask \emph{which trigger the iter-51
adaptive-G controller should use on this stack}. Iter 70 closes this gap by
attaching a third additive-optional block,
\texttt{controller\_predicted\_savings\_per\_rollout}, that lifts the iter-67
paired-step bootstrap summary into both record types:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{stack\_record.outcomes.controller\_predicted\_savings\_per\_rollout},
        populated on the $4$ N2 stack entries (grpo/aero/areal/gift).
  \item \texttt{variant\_delta\_record.controller\_predicted\_savings\_per\_rollout},
        populated on the $3$ N2 variant deltas (aero/areal/gift), as a sibling
        of \texttt{measured\_yield\_residual}.
\end{itemize}
Each block carries the $15$ per-method controller predictions ($3$ triggers
$\times$ $5$ thresholds): \texttt{trigger}, \texttt{threshold}, \texttt{fires},
\texttt{saved}, \texttt{missed}, \texttt{saved\_per\_fire},
\texttt{savings\_per\_rollout\_\{pt,lo,hi\}} (paired bootstrap percentile),
and \texttt{cost\_ratio\_pt} (the rollouts-used ratio at the chosen
threshold). All fields nullable; \texttt{additionalProperties: false}; the
patch is purely additive ($34/34$ schema PASS confirmed by both the new
script and canonical \texttt{registry\_validate.py}).

\paragraph{Sharpest finding: $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$-triage stays best even
\emph{at fixed cost}.}
The block lifts the row 78 headline into a registry-readable form. At matched
cost ratio $\approx 1.45$--$1.55$, the registry now self-reports the
saved-per-fire ordering $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}\geq\tau > \mathrm{Y^{obs}}\leq\tau
> \mathrm{ZVF^{obs}}\geq\tau$ on every N2 method. The single highest
saved-per-fire entry is GIFT under \texttt{ddiv\_triage} at $\tau=0.05$
($19$ saves / $13$ fires / \texttt{saved\_per\_fire}$=1.4615$), confirming
that the iter-67 sign-flip on GIFT (raw $\mathrm{ZVF^{obs}}=0.770$ is the
\emph{worst} signal for GIFT; $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}=0.039$ is the
\emph{best}) is now a registry-readable fact, not a high-level claim.

\paragraph{Why this matters.}
The block is the third additive-optional extension after iter-28
\texttt{outcomes.ci\_method} and iter-66 row 77
\texttt{measured\_yield\_residual}; together the three blocks implement a
\textbf{measurement $\to$ trigger $\to$ registry} pipeline: an entry can now
declare \emph{how its CI was computed} (iter-28), \emph{what its signed
yield-residual is} (iter-66), and \emph{which controller trigger dominates on
its panel} (iter-70) in three independent fields, all backward-compatible,
all nullable on entries that have not been audited on the N2 corpus. The
$N{=}7$ audited entries (grpo/aero/areal/gift as stacks + aero/areal/gift as
deltas) cover the entire N2 4-method panel; the remaining $27$ entries ship
\texttt{null} (unreported $\to$ auditor scores as gap).
