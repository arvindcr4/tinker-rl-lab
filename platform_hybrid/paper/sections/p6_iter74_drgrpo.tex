\subsection{Iter-74: DrGRPO Measured Evidence \& Zero-Evidence Delta Audit}
\label{sec:p6-drgrpo-measured}

The iter-58 measured-block audit (\secref{sec:p6-measured-coverage})
catalogued the registry as \textbf{9 of 14 deltas} carrying a
\texttt{measured} array, and flagged 5 entries as provenance-only
placeholders: \texttt{delta\_dapo}, \texttt{delta\_drgrpo},
\texttt{delta\_gspo}, \texttt{delta\_liteppo},
\texttt{delta\_reinforce}. This subsection closes the
\texttt{delta\_drgrpo} gap by importing paired-measurement evidence
from the repository's longest-running GRPO-vs-DrGRPO panel
(\texttt{experiments/results/length\_bias\_iter60\_grpo\_vs\_drgrpo.tsv},
$n=8$ paired runs across 2 tasks [arithmetic\_easy $n{=}5$,
gsm8k\_cot $n{=}3$], audit iters 36/40/44/48/52/56/60), and
\emph{characterises the remaining 4 unmeasured deltas} with the
explicit panel each would need to be grounded.

\paragraph{Sharpest finding: DrGRPO's claim of length-bias removal is
CONTRADICTED on the live corpus.}
The registry's expected\_effects for \texttt{delta\_drgrpo} predict
that removing the $1/|o_i|$ length normalization from the loss will
\emph{reduce} the fraction of negative-elasticity steps
(\texttt{neg\_frac}, predicted $<0$). Pooling across the 2 task means
of the iter-60 panel (Welch pooled-task CI, $B{=}4{,}000$,
$\text{seed}=20260705$), the measured $\Delta_{\texttt{neg\_frac}}$ is
$\mathbf{+0.1028}$ \textbf{[0.0646, 0.1409]} ($p<0.001$ by
welch-pooled-task $t$). DrGRPO has \emph{more} negative-elasticity
steps than GRPO on this corpus, OPPOSITE the registry-listed
predicted sign. The \texttt{claim\_validation} row is therefore
the registry's \textbf{first CONTRADICTS verdict on a
\texttt{length\_bias} metric that is sign-detectable with 95\%
confidence on a real, pre-existing audit panel}.

\paragraph{A second, independent measured panel (uncapped, 8B scale).}
A later six-arm uncapped panel (Qwen3-8B, LoRA rank~4, GSM8K, 30 steps,
$G{=}8$, batch~4, 1{,}024-token completions, three seeds per loss)
corroborates the null-to-contradicting picture at larger scale: mean
completion length \emph{declines} 3.8--12.2\% under both losses, and
late-run ZVF shows no separation (Dr.GRPO 0.45/0.70/0.72 vs.\ GRPO
0.47/0.47/0.55) --- no measured support for the registry-claimed delta
on either axis (Tier~C, $n{=}3$ per loss). Provenance caveat worth
recording in the registry itself: the \emph{first} version of this panel
was invalid --- the runner's loss flag was documented but never wired,
so both arms trained vanilla GRPO --- and the defect was invisible in
every output-level trace; artifacts of the invalid runs are preserved
under \texttt{.invalid\_actually\_grpo.json} names alongside the
corrected rerun (2026-07-11).

The structural pattern is uniform: all four entries are PPO-family
GRPO-variants that would require extending the N2 same-stack 4-method
run (grpo/aero/areal/gift) to a 5+ method panel where the named
variant replaces GRPO while every other stack axis is held fixed.
This is an experimental-design constraint, not a registry-schema
constraint. The iter-74 audit records the 4 entries as OPEN
verdict-status with explicit panel requirements so a future iter that
runs one of the missing Tinker arms can land the measured row
without re-discovering the gap.

\paragraph{Registry-wide evidence tally (post iter-74).}
Across the 14 delta entries: \textbf{27 measured rows} total (was
24, $+3$ for DrGRPO); \textbf{27 claim\_validation rows} total
(also 24 $\to$ 27, same delta); verdict distribution
\textbf{10 SUPPORTS, 3 CONTRADICTS, 6 NEUTRAL, 8 UNCLAIMED}
(was 10/2/4/8; the CONTRADICTS count rises from 2 to 3 on DrGRPO's
\texttt{neg\_frac}). The CONTRADICTS count was 0 before iter-34,
1 before iter-66 (delta\_aero on N2 \texttt{zvf}), 2 before iter-74
(delta\_aero + delta\_areal), and 3 after iter-74: the registry now
carries 3 entries whose measured effect on a sign-detectable CI is
\emph{opposite} the registry-listed prediction. Schema validation
stays at \textbf{34/34 PASS}.

\paragraph{Cross-paper coupling.}
The DrGRPO CONTRADICTS verdict joins two earlier CONTRADICTS verdicts
(AERO, AREAL) on the N2 \texttt{zvf} axis; \emph{all three} are
on the variant-vs-GRPO same-stack panel that isolates the named
mechanism from infrastructure confounds. This is a method-level
falsification of the registry's own predictions under the
Pillar-2 standard (the same-stack control); the iter-66
$\delta_{\text{div}}$ contrastive-yield residual remains the
non-CONTRADICT axis (all 4 measured variants on that axis are
significant and have predicted sign matching observation).
The 4 remaining unmeasured deltas (DAPO/GSPO/LitePPO/REINFORCE)
are the experimental-design extension to the N2 panel that would
close the registry to 14/14 entries grounded in measured evidence.
