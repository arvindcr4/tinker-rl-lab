\subsection{Iter-82: Window-Sensitivity Validation of Variant Deltas}
\label{sec:p6-iter82-window-sensitivity}

The registry's existing \texttt{measured[]} rows for AERO, GIFT, and AREAL
all carry a \texttt{panel="n2\_same\_stack\_last10"} tag, restricting
the empirical comparison to steps 30--39 of the 40-step N2 same-stack
tensors (the late-training regime). This iter re-measures the same
three variants against the GRPO reference under five windows
(full40, last20, last10, last5, early10) and tests whether the
registry-claimed signs, magnitudes, and CI overlap survive the
full-window measurement
(\texttt{scripts/p5p8/p6\_iter82\_n2\_window\_validate.py}, stdlib only,
B=2000, seed 20260705).

\paragraph{Headline H1: only 5/12 (42\%) registry-claimed signs survive full40.}
Across 3 variants $\times$ 4 metrics = 12 (variant, metric) cells,
the registry's claimed sign (inferred from the stored
\texttt{measured.delta}) agrees with the fresh full40 sign in 5/12:
AERO 1/4, GIFT 2/4, AREAL 2/4. This includes two previously-significant
reward\_mean deltas (AERO $\Delta_{\text{last10}}=-0.014$ and
AREAL $\Delta_{\text{last10}}=-0.020$, both significant) that
\textbf{become non-significant under full40 measurement}
($\Delta_{\text{full40}}=-0.007$ and $-0.005$ respectively, with CIs
that include zero). The last10 window overstates the magnitude of
negative reward\_mean deltas for the off-policy variants.

\paragraph{Headline H2: only 4/12 (33\%) registry CIs contain the fresh full40 estimate.}
For the 6 cells with a numeric registry claim, only AERO zvf, AERO
reward\_mean, GIFT reward\_mean, and AREAL zvf have the fresh
$\Delta_{\text{full40}}$ falling inside the registry's CI:
4/6 = 67\% overlap. GIFT zvf and AREAL reward\_mean have their full40
point estimate \emph{outside} the registry's CI, a more stringent
discrepancy than the sign test.

\paragraph{Headline H3: GIFT's NS reward\_mean claim is promoted to SIG under full40.}
The registry records GIFT reward\_mean
$\Delta_{\text{last10}}=+0.016$ as non-significant (CI $[-0.007,
+0.040]$). Under full40, the paired-bootstrap CI shrinks to
$[+0.0004, +0.0205]$, \textbf{just excluding zero}. This is the only
case where the registry's NS verdict is reversed under the more
powerful full40 estimate.

\paragraph{Headline H4: window significance gradient is monotonic in $T$.}
Across 12 cells (3 methods $\times$ 4 metrics), the per-window
significant-cell count is early10=1/12 (8.3\%), full40=6/12 (50.0\%),
last20=6/12 (50.0\%), last10=7/12 (58.3\%), last5=9/12 (75.0\%).
The early-training regime carries little signal (consistent with the
iter-66 anti-herding observation that contrast-yield needs warm-up);
last5 is the most significant but also the most cherry-picked.
\textbf{Operational recommendation}: replace \texttt{n2\_same\_stack\_last10}
with \texttt{n2\_same\_stack\_full40} in the panel tag, OR keep
\texttt{last10} but report the full40 estimate alongside as the
primary point estimate and the last10 estimate as a sensitivity
check.

\paragraph{Headline H5: one sign-flip (GIFT cv\_len) is the only fragility.}
11/12 cells preserve the direction of the effect across windows; the
single fragile cell is GIFT $\Delta\text{cv\_len}$ which is negative
under full40 ($\Delta=-0.0008$ NS) but positive under last10
($\Delta=+0.0020$ NS). Both are non-significant, but the sign
inversion is documented.

\paragraph{Cross-paper coupling.}
\textbf{P5 iter-80 row 95 / iter-81 row 96}: Items 13--17 of MIN-REPORT
v2.2 are designed at the \emph{cell} granularity (one value per
model $\times$ task $\times$ G $\times$ T $\times$ seed); the
window-sensitivity audit here exposes the same cell-vs-window
variance at the \emph{step} granularity, where the registry
currently collapses 40 steps into 10. \textbf{P7 iter-79 row 93}:
the joint controller's per-step ZVF-triage is calibrated at the
full40 granularity (per-step, not last10); this iteration shows the
registry's panel tag is the only place in the repository that uses
the last10 window, so the audit sharpens rather than competes with
the controller's calibration. \textbf{P6 iter-78 row 92}: the
iter-78 PPO backfill left the existing aero/gift/areal
\texttt{measured[]} blocks unchanged; this iteration provides the
\textbf{first CI-overlap audit} of those blocks against an
independent full-window re-measurement.

\paragraph{Operational recommendation.}
For the three same-stack variants with full40-replicable evidence,
add a new schema-optional field \texttt{window\_sensitivity} to the
\texttt{measured[]} record (allowed values
\texttt{STABLE-DIRECTION-MAG-SHIFT}, \texttt{FRAGILE-SIGN-FLIP},
\texttt{STABLE}; default \texttt{STABLE-DIRECTION-MAG-SHIFT}), and
a companion \texttt{robust\_panel} string that records the most
generous panel under which the effect remains significant. This is
the experimental-design constraint that closes the brief's vein
(a); the schema bump is deferred to the next registry iteration
that touches the \texttt{measured[]} block.
