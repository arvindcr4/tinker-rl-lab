\subsection{Iter-58: Measured-Block Provenance \& Coverage Audit}
\label{sec:p6-measured-coverage}

The iter-50 health audit and the iter-54 missing-delta gap together
validated the registry against the \emph{schema} and against the
\emph{implementation cross-reference}. This subsection closes a third,
unaddressed vein: an audit of the \texttt{measured} block itself---
the array that grounds every delta entry's claim in measured evidence.
The audit answers four questions:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Block presence}: which of the 14 \texttt{delta\_*.json}
records carry a \texttt{measured} array, an \texttt{expected\_effects}
array, and a \texttt{claim\_validation} array?
\item \textbf{Source resolution}: does every measured row's
\texttt{source} path resolve on disk, and what is the freshness
(\texttt{mtime} age in days) of each source?
\item \textbf{Coverage grid}: which (\texttt{panel},
\texttt{metric}) cells are filled across the 14 entries? The panel set
in scope is \{\texttt{n2\_same\_stack\_last10},
\texttt{zvf130\_5seed}\}; the metric set is
\{\texttt{zvf}, \texttt{reward\_mean},
\texttt{zvf\_risk\_mean}, \texttt{mean\_zvf}\}.
\item \textbf{Cross-panel agreement}: for entries with the same
conceptual metric measured under both N2 and ZVF130, do the two
panels agree on the direction of effect?
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Falsifiable headline (re-run 2026-07-05).}
\textbf{9 of 14 deltas (64\%)} carry a non-empty \texttt{measured}
array; \textbf{5 of 14 (36\%)} ship as provenance-only
placeholders: \texttt{delta\_dapo}, \texttt{delta\_drgrpo},
\texttt{delta\_gspo} (have arXiv citations and stack entries but no
same-stack panel data on the canonical N2/Z130 source TSVs), plus
\texttt{delta\_reinforce} and \texttt{delta\_liteppo} (the iter-54
provenance-only additions). The registry-wide \texttt{claim\_validation}
tally is \textbf{10 SUPPORTS, 2 CONTRADICTS, 4 NEUTRAL, 8 UNCLAIMED}
across the 24 validation rows total. \textbf{0 measured row's source
path is missing}---every cited \texttt{.tsv} resolves on disk and
the audit's freshness window is bounded by the repository's most recent
N2/Z130 writes. The cross-panel agreement finds \textbf{2 of 3 entries
agree on direction}: AERO (\texttt{Delta\textsubscript{N2 zvf}} =
$-0.0250$, \texttt{Delta\textsubscript{Z130 risk}} = $-0.1476$)
and AREAL ($-0.0563$, $-0.2458$) both push ZVF negatively on both
panels; \textbf{GIFT is the lone cross-panel dissenter}
($+0.1250$, $-0.2632$)---a structural disagreement that
\secref{sec:p6-measured} reconciles (GIFT reweights groups, so raw
N2 zvf can rise while bounded-Z130 risk falls).

\paragraph{The 36\% empty-measured gap as a quantified backlog.}
The 5 empty-measured entries split into two structural classes:
\textbf{(i) source-data-unavailable} ($\texttt{delta\_dapo}$,
$\texttt{delta\_drgrpo}$, $\texttt{delta\_gspo}$)---each has a
registered arXiv citation and at least one stack entry claiming the
delta, but no same-stack panel run exists in
\texttt{experiments/results/n2\_reward\_tensor\_resume/} or
\texttt{experiments/results/zvf\_iter130\_method\_risk.tsv};
\textbf{(ii) provenance-only design} ($\texttt{delta\_reinforce}$,
$\texttt{delta\_liteppo}$)---the iter-54 additions deliberately
shipped with \texttt{measured}=[] as a structural placeholder, with
the criterion for a measured row stated in \texttt{notes}. The
audit's \texttt{empty\_measured\_gap} list surfaces class (i) as
the actionable backlog: 3 entries are awaiting a same-stack panel run
on the repository's existing N2/Z130 harness before they can carry a
measured row.

\paragraph{Why this matters.} Three reviewer-facing consequences.
First, the audit is a one-shot script ($\sim$280\,LoC, stdlib only)
that produces machine-readable TSVs plus a sidecar
\texttt{registry/measured\_block\_audit.json} cache; re-running it
takes $\leq 1$ second and the freshness window is bounded by the most
recent N2/Z130 write. Second, the coverage grid
(\tableref{tab:p6-measured-coverage}) maps every (\texttt{panel},
\texttt{metric}) cell to a count of populated entries, giving the
registry a machine-readable surface for \emph{which} cells are still
empirically thin---e.g. the \texttt{reward\_mean} column on the
\texttt{zvf130\_5seed} panel is $0/14$, which is a real evidence gap
(rather than a known absence). Third, the cross-panel agreement row
for GIFT (\texttt{same\_sign=False}, both rows individually significant)
is the first \emph{positive finding} this iteration surfaces: a
measurement-confirmed sign disagreement between two same-method
panels, with the GIFT reweighting story as the candidate
explanation. This is a finding, not a regression---it is the kind
of cross-panel consistency check that a registry without an MBPCA
audit would silently mask.

\paragraph{Cross-paper coupling.}
The N2 panel ${}\leftrightarrow{}$ ZVF130 panel reconciliation
sharpens the iter-34 measured block: GIFT's prior
\texttt{claim\_validation} \texttt{zvf\_risk\_mean} verdict was
SUPPORTS, and that verdict survives the cross-panel audit at the
metric level (risk reduction is real) even though the raw-ZVF
direction disagrees at the panel level. The framing for the
canonical reader is therefore: \emph{GIFT is risk-favouring but
ZVF-raising}---a sharper statement than the prior one-line claim
that ``GIFT helps signal starvation''.

\paragraph{Reproduction.}
\begin{verbatim}
python3 scripts/p5p8/p6_measured_coverage.py  # writes 4 output files
python3 registry/query.py measured-coverage   # prints the audit
python3 registry/query.py measured-coverage --delta delta_gift
                                               # per-entry deep view
\end{verbatim}
Outputs: $\texttt{experiments/results/p5p8/p6\_measured\_coverage.tsv}$
(14 per-entry rows); $\texttt{p6\_measured\_coverage\_grid.tsv}$
(14 $\times$ 8 grid); $\texttt{p6\_measured\_cross\_panel.tsv}$
(3 cross-panel rows); $\texttt{p6\_measured\_coverage\_summary.json}$
(headline numbers). Sidecar cache:
$\texttt{registry/measured\_block\_audit.json}$ (refreshed each run).

\paragraph{Backward compatibility.}
The audit is \emph{additive}: it reads from existing
\texttt{measured} / \texttt{source} fields and writes a sidecar
JSON cache plus TSV outputs. No entry is modified.
\texttt{registry/query.py} gains \texttt{measured-coverage} as a
third-tier reporting subcommand (additive, the existing eight
subcommands untouched). \texttt{registry/query.py validate}
remains at \textbf{34/34 PASS} after the registry is unchanged.
