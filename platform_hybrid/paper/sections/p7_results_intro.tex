\section{From Diagnostic to Controller: Results}
\label{sec:p7-results}
The results are organized as a ladder from theory to intervention. We first
state the signal-starvation model and its three theorems, pairing each with
the empirical check that could have falsified it
(Section~\ref{sec:p7-theory}). We then zoom out to population scale, where
the theory predicts---and a 368-run audit confirms---that ZVF is U-shaped in
accuracy, which is precisely why ZVF alone cannot steer a controller: it
aliases mastery with incapacity. Section~\ref{sec:p7-ushape} introduces the
pairwise-contrast density (PCD) as the disambiguating statistic and reports
the micro-jitter falsification that separates the two. Section~\ref{sec:p7-e1}
validates the gradient-level assumption (T3) in an open backward pass, at
honest toy scale. Sections~\ref{sec:p7-controller} and \ref{sec:p7-rules}
operationalize the theory: a \texttt{zvf-triage} callback, an adaptive-$G$
controller, a four-arm audit against fixed recipes, and the design rules the
audit supports.

Two findings from the companion papers of \ours{} (the Pillar-2 diagnostic
paper and the Pillar-5/6 reporting papers) frame everything that follows.
First, recipe labels
are unreliable across stacks: in a 12-cell head-to-head on one closed stack
(Qwen3.5-4B, GSM8K, 3 seeds each), \texttt{dapo}, \texttt{drgrpo},
\texttt{grpo}, and \texttt{gspo} land within noise on last-10 reward
($0.744$, $0.742$, $0.723$, $0.710$) while their mean ZVFs ($0.578$, $0.500$,
$0.567$, $0.511$) stay far from the $0.00$ that true dynamic sampling
produces in an open trainer---the same ``DAPO'' label yields mean ZVF $0.58$
on the closed stack and $0.00$ in the open reimplementation. Second, the
stack itself can dominate the visible label: a nominal backend audit holding
$G$, learning rate, data, and seed fixed moved final reward from $5.0\%$ to
$85.6\%$, but also bundled a different base checkpoint. The $17\times$ ratio
is therefore an under-specification warning, not a backend causal estimate.
Both findings dictate our method: all
interventional comparisons in this paper run inside a \emph{single auditable
open reimplementation}, so that the controller is compared against what
recipes actually do, not what their labels claim.
