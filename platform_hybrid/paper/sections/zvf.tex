\subsection{ZVF Under Variance-Mitigation Variants: a Matched 8-Seed Comparison}
\label{sec:zvf-cross-library}
Several GRPO variants act on zero-variance groups or on GRPO's normalisation:
DAPO~\citep{yu2025dapo} over-samples and filters zero-variance groups,
AERO~\citep{zhang2026aero} adapts rollout allocation,
Dr.~GRPO~\citep{tong2025drgrpo} removes the $1/\sigma$ and length normalisers, and
GSPO~\citep{qwen2025gspo} uses sequence-level importance ratios. We compare them
with vanilla GRPO in a matched campaign (\texttt{e1-confirmatory-*}, exported to
\texttt{experiments/results/e1\_confirmatory\_wandb\_export.tsv}): Qwen3-8B on GSM8K
with an exact-match reward, TRL \texttt{GRPOTrainer}, $G{=}4$ generations per
prompt, $30$ optimizer steps at learning rate $10^{-6}$, $\beta{=}0$, maximum
completion length $1024$, temperature $1.0$. All five arms use the \emph{same}
eight seeds ($11, 23, 37, 53, 71, 89, 107, 131$), so every contrast with GRPO is
paired by seed, and each run is scored on the same $500$ held-out GSM8K problems.
Adapters are released on the Hugging Face Hub (repository names in the export).

\paragraph{Reading the table.}
No variant differs from GRPO on held-out accuracy: every paired difference is
within $\pm0.005$, no paired $t$-test approaches significance, and every variant
is equivalent to GRPO within $\pm0.02$ by seed-level TOST. Mean ZVF is
$0.69$--$0.71$ for GRPO, AERO, Dr.~GRPO and GSPO, so about seven in ten groups carry
no group-relative signal in every arm that keeps zero-variance groups. DAPO
eliminates them entirely, yet its held-out accuracy is the same. AERO reaches the
same accuracy with $44\%$ of GRPO's generated tokens.

\paragraph{What this does and does not show.} Removing zero-variance groups (DAPO)
or changing the normaliser (Dr.~GRPO, GSPO) did not change held-out accuracy in
this regime, so a high ZVF was not by itself a measurable cost here. The regime is
narrow: $30$ steps at learning rate $10^{-6}$ is very short training, and no
base-model held-out score on these $500$ problems exists, so we cannot say how far
any arm moved from its starting point. The null therefore rules out
\emph{large} short-horizon differences, not effects that would appear over longer
training, at higher learning rates, or on harder tasks. Other variance-mitigation
methods (CPPO, Scaf-GRPO, NGRPO, MC-GRPO, ES, AReaL, GIFT) have no completed runs
in this campaign and are not evaluated.

\subsection{What ZVF Adds Beyond Mean Reward}
\label{sec:zvf-residualised}
ZVF and mean reward are mechanically related: more all-correct groups means
higher reward and higher ZVF. We do not claim ZVF is an incremental predictor of
outcome above mean reward, and the earlier pooled ZVF--outcome correlation is
withdrawn (Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-experiment:corr}). Its value is
interpretive, as the following case shows.

\paragraph{The clearest case of ZVF-beyond-reward: the all-correct wall.}
A two-seed matched-budget panel (2{,}560 rollouts per arm:
$G{=}2\times160$ steps vs.\ $G{=}16\times20$ steps, Qwen3-8B/GSM8K,
LoRA rank~4, batch~8) supplies the sharpest instance of ZVF carrying
information mean reward cannot. Late in the $G{=}2$ arms, mean train
reward sits near 1.0 --- by the reward axis alone, training is
succeeding --- while $\mathrm{ZVF}$ sits at $0.75$--$1.0$: nearly every
group is all-correct and has zero centered reward contrast in the submitted
GRPO term; this does not imply that every total-gradient contribution vanishes.
The $G{=}16$ arms at
the same rollout budget hold $\mathrm{ZVF}\le0.25$. Reward reports
\emph{where the policy is}; ZVF reports \emph{whether training is still
moving} --- and at the high-accuracy end the two disagree completely.
Artifacts: \texttt{tinker-runs/results/er2b\_*.json}.

\subsection{Why We Treat ZVF as Diagnostic, Not Causal}
\label{sec:zvf-caveat}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Symptom, not cause.} In the collapses we measured (Nemotron-120B and
the two tool-use cells) ZVF is high when reward is low; nothing in our data shows
that ZVF precedes or causes collapse.
\item \textbf{No outcome effect of removing it here.} In the matched comparison
(Table~\ref{tab:zvf-by-library}) DAPO drives ZVF to zero without changing held-out
accuracy, so on that short Qwen3-8B/GSM8K horizon ZVF did not limit the outcome.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Recommended use.} Report ZVF alongside mean reward in any GRPO
ablation table, together with its signed split (all-correct versus all-wrong
groups, Section~\ref{sec:zvf-signed}), and read a high value as a statement about
how many groups carry signal, not as a failure trigger.
