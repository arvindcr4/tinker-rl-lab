\subsection{Iso-Yield Curves Across Libraries (withdrawn)}
\label{sec:zvf-iso-yield}
\label{tab:zvf-isoyield-y80}
\textbf{Withdrawn.} This subsection derived minimum-$G$ ``iso-yield'' curves and
$20$--$25\%$ rollout savings from the ``anti-herding'' divergence
$\delta_{\mathrm{div}}=+0.122$. That divergence is an estimator artefact
(Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-source-falsification}): with the i.i.d.\ prediction
evaluated at each group's own plug-in $\hat p=k/G$ it is non-negative for every
group by construction, with expectation $+0.105$ under exactly i.i.d.\ rollouts.
It also applied that GSM8K value to the simulated variance-mitigation libraries.
No iso-yield curve, saving, or ranking from it is claimed. The artifacts
(\texttt{experiments/results/zvf\_iter38\_isoyield.tsv}) are retained only for
the audit trail.

\subsection{ZVF as a Failure-Mode Classifier (withdrawn)}
\label{sec:zvf-classifier}
\label{tab:zvf-classifier-conf}
\textbf{Withdrawn.} An earlier revision reported that a 3-NN classifier on
(mean ZVF, last-10, peak) recovered failure modes on 14 pooled rows with
$11/14$ accuracy and that dropping ZVF lowered this to $9/14$. The ground-truth
labels were themselves threshold rules on mean ZVF, last-10 and peak, so the
comparison is circular; nine of the 14 rows are simulated variance-mitigation
libraries; and the classifier never predicted \texttt{collapse} or
\texttt{drift} correctly (it predicted \texttt{plateau} for 12 rows). The
ZVF-free taxonomy of Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-experiment} is the only
failure labelling we use.

\subsection{Headline Numbers and Cross-Reference (withdrawn)}
\label{sec:zvf-iter38-headline}
The three headline numbers of this iteration ($\delta_{\mathrm{div}}=+0.122$,
the iso-yield savings, and the $78.6\%$ classifier accuracy) are withdrawn for
the reasons above.
