\subsection{Dynamic Iso-Yield Group Sizing (Iso-G) (withdrawn)}
\label{sec:zvf-isog}
\label{tab:zvf-isog-savings}
\label{sec:zvf-isog-predictions}
\label{tab:zvf-isog-preds}
\label{sec:zvf-isog-repro}
\textbf{Withdrawn.} This subsection and its pre-registered predictions sized $G$
per prompt to reach a target contrastive yield after correcting the i.i.d.\
prediction by the ``anti-herding'' divergence $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$, and reported
$17.2\%$--$68.0\%$ rollout savings. Because $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$ is an estimator
artefact (Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-source-falsification}), the corrections,
savings, triggers and prediction checks are withdrawn. Per-prompt iso-$G$ sizing
under the plain i.i.d.\ model, $\min\{G: 1-p_x^G-(1-p_x)^G \ge Y\}$, needs no
correction term but has not been evaluated prospectively. The artifacts
(\texttt{platform\_modal/scripts/zvf\_iter46\_isog.py},
\texttt{experiments/results/zvf\_iter46\_*}) are retained only for the audit
trail.
