\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR), and in particular Group
Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), has become the de facto default recipe for
post-training reasoning-capable language models. Yet reported gains are hard to
reproduce: results are spread across incompatible frameworks (TRL, veRL,
OpenRLHF, Tinker), are frequently single-seed, and rarely expose the internal
training signal that would explain \emph{why} a run learned or failed to learn.

This consolidated report covers both project phases. \textbf{Phase 1} built a
unified, provenance-tracked benchmark and diagnostic harness for GRPO/RLVR
post-training, organised around a single measurable quantity --- the
\textbf{Zero-Variance Fraction (ZVF)}, the fraction of prompt groups whose
within-group reward variance is zero and which therefore contribute no
reward-derived advantage signal. Eight focused studies (P1--P8) were run on that
harness. The honest Phase-1 headline is a set of strong negatives: on the fixed
40-step GSM8K study, $72$--$77\%$ of prompt groups carry zero within-group reward
variance (P2); a single-seed group-size sweep establishes no robust optimum (P3);
at a matched ${\sim}30$k-token budget the baseline and curriculum arms both
average $+0.028$ held-out gain; and the apparent step-1 predictability of
per-layer adaptation (top-$k$ overlap $=1.0$ at 1.5B) collapses to chance
($0.083\pm0.048$) under a scaled 3B, four-seed re-test.

\textbf{Phase 2} extended the work along two axes. First, it converted the
diagnostic into an \emph{auditable conformance and decision layer}: a fail-closed
conformance chain from objective equation to gradient relation, adaptive decision,
and training record, with a frozen CPU differential (14 intended cases per stack
for TRL and verl, plus a shared 36-case controller matrix) and cost-accounting
corollaries of the binary-starvation model. Second, it executed a
\textbf{fourteen-lane external evaluation campaign} (E1--E14) against held-out
agent and RL benchmark suites under strict receipt, budget, and decontamination
governance. Three replacement scopes closed strictly complete (E8 LAB-Bench
1967/1967 across 8 categories; E10 AgentDojo 97/97 benign utility; E11
VerilogEval 312/312 native checks), one closed terminal-complete at 99.95\%
coverage (E14 Omni-MATH 4426/4428 accepted, official accuracy 51.31\%
reproduced), and the original-contract E1 and E11 suites carry full verified
scores (E1 SWE-bench Pro 2/731 $=0.274\%$ pass@1; E11 129/312 $=41.35\%$
pass@1). The remaining lanes are recorded as honestly partial or externally
blocked, each with an immutable evidence trail.

The report's central methodological commitment is that \textbf{no number is
invented and no lane is silently rounded up}. Original-contract and
replacement-scope denominators are never pooled, no cross-suite average is
reported, and no baseline-gain or causal-capability claim is made. The
consolidated ledger is gated by a deterministic verifier that re-derives eleven
specific claims from surviving receipts --- exact divisions, receipt presence,
sealed-artefact hash equality, and lost-source absence probes --- and reports
\textbf{11/11 PASS}. The contribution of this work is therefore best understood
as an auditable boundary between what was measured, what was proven, and what
remains open.
