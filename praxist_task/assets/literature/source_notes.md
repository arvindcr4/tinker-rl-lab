## Metric boundary

The primary E9 result is reportable only after all 75 official competitions have native outcomes, aggregated as Any Medal (%) and, for a benchmark-comparable study, repeated across at least three seeds with mean and SEM. Low/Medium/High/All breakdowns should accompany the aggregate.

Valid-submission rate, native-grade coverage, failure taxonomy, elapsed time, and cost are useful operational secondary measures. They never replace the primary metric. The current 37/75 unique native-grade coverage is therefore coverage only; the E9 full-suite score remains `null`. Of the 38 competitions without a native grade, 13 have only failed receipts and no saved submission, while 25 have no run receipt; there is no further native-only saved-submission regrade available in the current artifact set.
