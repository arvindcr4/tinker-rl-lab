## 2. Consensus failure modes across papers

These recur across at least four of the six panels and are therefore portfolio properties, not single-paper accidents. Each item cites the per-paper weakness IDs that establish it.

1. **Receipts bind reports to themselves, not to executions.** Hash/format/internal-consistency checks are presented in "verified/governance/conformance" language while checkpoints, corpora, training source, or raw payloads are private, absent, or narrative-only, and verifiers either fail on the live tree or check the receipts' own fields. (`01` R1-W1, R3-W3; `02` R1-W1, DA-MAJOR-2; `03` DA-MAJOR-1; `04` R1-W4/DA-MAJOR-1 — benign-by-rerun exception; `05` R3-W2/DA-MAJOR-2; `06` R3-W2, DA-CRITICAL-3.) 04 is the partial counter-example: its replay numbers reproduce exactly on re-execution, with only a stale source hash (`04` R1-W4).
2. **Circular or degenerate validation.** Controls/fixtures whose outcome is fixed by construction are reported alongside genuine measurements: identical reductions in the balanced control (01), self-divided receipt arithmetic (02), ZVF validated against the same tensors that define it (03), injected-offset fixture with cancelling position channel (04), mechanically coupled PTD/ZVF correlations (05), plug-in counterfactual that forces zero rescue (06, via `03` R2-W4 analogue). In each case the manuscript body mostly discloses the degeneracy; the failure is that the display/abstract still carries evidentiary weight. (`01` R1-W2/W3; `02` R1-W1; `03` DA-CRITICAL-1; `04` R1-W2; `05` DA-MAJOR-1; `06` R2-W3, DA-CRITICAL-2.)
3. **Titles/abstracts assert the strong version the body disavows.** "Decision Insufficiency" (01), "Governance"/"Fourteen-Lane" (02), "Stack-Conditioned Measurement" (03), proposition-dressed arithmetic (04), "Reward Contrast, Not Algorithm Labels" (05), abstract +5.0pt/campaign/C1 foregrounding (06). All six panels make some form of this charge; all six credit the body caveats as genuine. The portfolio risk is citation-level propagation of the strong version.
4. **Null, underpowered, or ceiling-bound contrasts carry framing weight.** Inconclusive paired pilots (02: 17 contrasts, k≤1 discordant in several lanes), ceiling PPO-vs-GRPO at 0.98–1.00 (03, 06), n=2 diagnostic record (05), 6-of-24-units campaign (01). Panels agree the null *language* is usually correct ("inconclusive ≠ equivalent"); the defect is prominence — tables, contribution bullets, and ledger chapters sized as if an effect had been measured.
5. **The "GRPO-trained" actor is, at available resolution, indistinguishable from base, and its training is unauditable.** 35/40 zero-loss steps (02), 80 draws with unrecovered rows/code (03, 06), null paired reruns in every lane (02, 03, 06). No panel finds a demonstrated training benefit anywhere in the portfolio. Lane scores shared across 02/03/06 are therefore base-model-plus-serving-stack measurements until shown otherwise (`02` R2-W2).
6. **Undisclosed slicing of one programme into minimum publishable units, without cross-citation or a programme map.** Every panel's DA seat raises salami/overlap (see §3). No manuscript among the six contains an overlap statement naming its siblings, shared tables, or unique delta — the single partial exception is 05's one-sentence thesis-Table-6.2 note (see §3F).
7. **Harness/serving artefacts dominate headline scores, but scores are printed where they will be scraped without the qualifier.** Patch corruption 593/713, unparsed 1,259/1,967, extraction failures 150/183, cap splits, AgentDojo 0.907 benign-utility-only (02, 03, 06); window mislabels "26 logged"/"last-10" over 3–6-point traces (05). Panels consistently require the qualifier *inside* the table cell/caption, not in distant prose.
8. **Bibliographic and build hygiene below the papers' own stated standard.** Uncited entries: 17/35 (01), 11/29 (04), 108/261 (05); future-dated/grey 2026 sources load-bearing for benchmark identity (02, 03); dual/stale PDFs disagreeing on pages/hashes (02); non-anonymous preprint builds (04, 05); broken symlinks (01). Individually minor; collectively they are why "scientifically valid" and "submission-ready" diverge across the whole portfolio.

What the portfolio does *well*, by consensus: claim-bounding in body text, preserved withdrawals/errata, denominator discipline, no-pooling of original/replacement scopes, and — where artifacts exist — numbers that trace exactly under independent recomputation (01 campaign receipts, 02 later-replacement `result.json` files + E11 bootstrap, 03 sweep/tensor stats, 04 replay, 05 held-out/lagged/Wave-6 traces, 06 tensor recomputations). The editorial problem is not honesty; it is that honesty is currently doing the work that contribution, binding, and disclosure should do.
