## 3.2 R1 — Methodology

**Strengths.** Preregistration discipline is exemplary in one respect: the positive control was descoped rather than gate-weakened (`main.tex:630-638`), all 31 manifest IDs are crosswalked (Table `tab:crosswalk`, verified against `supervisor_state.json`: 10/14/1/2/4), and joint-zero is stored as a relation with null cosine instead of a fabricated angle (Eq. `eq:gradient-relation`, `main.tex:376-388`; verified in all 600 receipts). The frozen bundle verifies end-to-end (see R1-W1 for the live-tree caveat).

**R1-W1 — Reproducibility receipts do not bind to the current repository; the verifier fails out of the box.** Anchor: Reproducibility Statement, `main.tex:833-849`; Limitations, `main.tex:809-812`. Evidence: `python3 zvf-program/flagship/paper/verify_claims.py` on the live tree aborts with `AssertionError` in `verify_s1` (line 264): 6 of 8 frozen source hashes per stack have drifted (`reference.py`, `receipt.py`, adapters, tests; only `S1_AMENDMENT.md`/`fixtures.py` match), while both receipt JSON hashes still match the freeze. Inside the extracted `review_bundle.zip` the same verifier passes (exit 0). What is wrong: the paper's central selling point is fail-closed binding, yet a reviewer following the repo (rather than the zip) hits an unlabelled failure, and the manuscript never states that the live tree is *expected* to fail. "Use the hashes, not the directory name" (`main.tex:810-812`) understates it: the named entry point is broken. Fix: (i) state in the Reproducibility Statement that verification is bundle-only and the live tree has diverged (list the drifted files or freeze a tag/commit that passes); (ii) ideally restore the frozen S1 sources at a tagged commit so the documented command passes in-repo. Until then every "Verified" cell in Table `tab:status` needs the qualifier "in bundle."

**R1-W2 — The balanced "negative control" is analytically degenerate, so its gate failure cannot be interpreted.** Anchor: `main.tex:683-701`. Evidence (authors' own, correct): with all rows active at equal length, per-completion and global reductions coincide, and intended-vs.-native differ only by the scalar s/(s+10⁻⁴), predicting cosine 1 and relative-ℓ₂ ≤ 2.8×10⁻⁴. Stored relative-ℓ₂ reaches 0.0177 — 60× larger — "not explained by the objective definitions. We did not isolate their source" (`main.tex:696-699`). What is wrong: the sole completed intended-full cell fails its registered gate 69/100 vs. 95/100 (`main.tex:703-707`, independently reproduced) *for an unknown reason* the authors attribute, without a test, to bfloat16/separate backward passes. A conformance paper whose one completed gate fails from unisolated numerics has not demonstrated conformance methodology — it has demonstrated an unexplained residual. The control also cannot detect reduction bugs by construction (the two reductions are identical there), so "negative control" overstates its discriminating power. Fix: isolate the residual on CPU/float64 replay of a few stored steps (same θ, both objectives, single backward graph) and report whether 0.0177 collapses to ≤2.8×10⁻⁴; if checkpoints are private, say the gate result is uninterpretable rather than "failed," and downgrade Table `tab:status` "Balanced gradient relations: Verified" to receipt-integrity only. Add one non-degenerate balanced variant (unequal active lengths with all rows retained) in any resubmission design, even if only in S1.

**R1-W3 — The registered joint gate has a redundant arm; preregistration did not catch it.** Anchor: `main.tex:705-710` ("the cosine bound is never binding" since rel-ℓ₂ ≤ 0.01 ⇒ cos ≥ 0.99995 > 0.999). What is wrong: a two-criterion gate where one criterion is mathematically implied by the other is a design error that a fail-closed protocol paper should own as such, not report as a curiosity. It also means all "cosine ≥ 0.999844" headline language (abstract, `main.tex:80-81`) is close to content-free: near-collinearity at that level is guaranteed by the scalar-multiple structure (R1-W2). Fix: label this a preregistration defect, remove cosine extrema from the abstract, and in future gates use non-redundant criteria (e.g., rel-ℓ₂ plus update-level or loss-level tolerance).

**R1-W4 — Proposition 3 assumes the reversal it "proves"; n does no work.** Anchor: `main.tex:516-544`, esp. Δ>0 at Eq. `eq:delta` and "The prior n failures … do not enter a posterior calculation" (`main.tex:540-541`). What is wrong: given Δ>0, opposite optima and the minimax value cmΔ/(cm+Δ) are arithmetic, not insight; the observed history n, the batch-prepayment ("All m costs are paid even if an early rollout succeeds," `main.tex:505-506` — no real controller prepays blindly when it could stop sequentially), and the exclusion of sequential stopping/choice of m/reverification (`main.tex:542-543`) remove exactly the structure that makes the operational problem hard. As a *witness* it is valid; as "Decision Insufficiency" it is over-titled (cf. EIC-W2). Fix: demote to Remark/Example, state plainly that any two-point prior with sign-changing value gives the same regret form, and either add the sequential version (optimal stopping vs. E₀/E_q gives a non-trivial threshold) or stop claiming decision-theoretic contribution weight.

**R1-W5 — No uncertainty quantification anywhere; n=100 steps with 62–65% joint-zero is ~35 informative receipts per unit.** Anchor: Table `tab:runs`; `main.tex:642-654`. Final accuracies 17–20/128 from a 20/128 start are within binomial noise of each other (SE ≈ 3.5/128), correctly not interpreted (`main.tex:725-726`) — but then the table's accuracy columns, and charged-token totals without FLOP/wall-time counterparts in-paper, invite the comparison the text forbids. Fix: add exact binomial intervals or drop accuracy to the appendix receipts; report the intermediate 0–100 curves' joint-zero-adjusted effective step count so readers see how little learning signal the design contained.

**R1-W6 — Controller matrix does not exercise its own branches.** Anchor: `main.tex:344-363`: with n=2 histories, Wilson bounds are 0.658/0.342, so "the two full-triage drop branches are not exercised by the 36 frozen cases." A 36-case matrix whose headline policy's distinctive branches never fire is coverage theatre. Fix: add frozen cases with pooled histories large enough to cross 0.05/0.95, or state the matrix tests only the escalate/keep/recheck paths.

## 3.3 R2 — Domain (GRPO / RLVR / RL stacks)

**Strengths.** Positioning against starvation prior work is honest (`main.tex:99-102`, `196-200`); the S1 objective is fully specified (Eqs. `eq:s1-advantage`/`eq:s1-objective`, thresholds 10⁻⁸ vs. campaign 10⁻¹² reconciled at `main.tex:295-300`); native verdicts are framed as "different from this reference," not bugs (`main.tex:324-326`) — the right norm, consistent with Engstrom et al.

**R2-W1 — S1 "arms" borrow algorithm names for fixture encodings that omit those algorithms' defining mechanisms.** Anchor: `main.tex:246-255` (disclosure), Table `tab:s1-cases`. DAPO-without-dynamic-sampling/token-loss, DrGRPO-with-per-completion-reduction, and AERO-as-Beta-branch are disclosed, but every table/verdict still speaks of "DAPO," "DrGRPO," "AERO" arms and native materiality per algorithm name (e.g., `main.tex:910-914`). Disclosure in prose does not survive table-level citation. Fix: rename arms to the semantic tested (clip-high, unscaled, seq-ratio, posterior-branch) with algorithm citations as motivation only.

**R2-W2 — Native verl coverage is near-empty; the two-stack framing oversells.** Anchor: Table `tab:s1` (verl: 1 material, 4 not-tested of 5); `main.tex:911-913`. What was actually differentially tested natively is: TRL on 4 single-fixture arms, verl on 1 (GRPO base). Each native verdict rests on *one fixture per arm* (`main.tex:915-918`). Claims phrased at stack level ("14 intended cases for each of TRL and verl," abstract `main.tex:64-66`) describe the intended integration, not native conformance evidence. Fix: report native evidence as 5 single-fixture probes total per stack in the abstract/results, and expand native fixtures beyond base cases (zero-variance and selected-row fixtures are precisely where stacks diverge).

**R2-W3 — Directly relevant zero-variance exploitation work sits uncited in the bibliography; canonical statistics sources are missing.** Anchor: `flagship.bib` contains `le2025noprompt` ("No Prompt Left Behind: Exploiting Zero-Variance Prompts…") and `schulman2017ppo`, neither cited in `main.tex` (verified: 18 cited of 35 bib entries; 17 uncited, including HiPPO/RoFormer/LMU entries belonging to the sibling spectral paper — bibliography pollution from a shared .bib). Conversely the Wilson interval (used at `main.tex:354-359`), the value-of-information result (Blackwell), and sequential-testing background for retry/stop problems carry no citations. I name no new RL papers here beyond what the repo itself contains; the missing *kind* of source is: (i) the zero-variance-prompt exploitation line the authors already have in-bib, (ii) primary sources for Wilson/Blackwell/SPRT. Fix: prune the .bib to cited entries, cite or consciously omit `le2025noprompt` with a distinguishing sentence, add the three statistics citations.

**R2-W4 — Verifier-failure motivation is never instantiated.** Anchor: four-cause ambiguity (`main.tex:104-113`, TinyV/VerifyBench) vs. campaign: strict `####` parser, no false-negative audit, no execution-failure arm (`main.tex:649-651`). The paper motivates triage among causes it never measures; the 62–65% joint-zero rate is consistent with a too-hard/too-long generation contract (512-token thinking-mode saturation, `main.tex:622-629`) rather than any of the four causes distinguished. Fix: either measure parser false-negative rate on a hand-checked sample of joint-zero groups, or scope the motivation to the two causes the evidence touches (impossible vs. unlucky).

## 3.4 R3 — Perspective (operator / downstream reader)

**Strengths.** The cost accounting distinction — groups-to-contrast falls with G while rollouts-to-contrast cannot beat 1/ε for complete groups (Prop. 2) — is the paper's most usable takeaway for practitioners budgeting rollouts, and it is stated with correct scope (`main.tex:494-499`, `773-777`).

**R3-W1 — The operational prescription is not implementable as stated.** Anchor: "expose uncertainty rather than convert a homogeneous group directly into 'retry' or 'drop' … justified only when its expected decision value exceeds its cost" (`main.tex:792-797`). No estimator for that expected value, no prior over the four causes, and no cost units are given; Prop. 3's B, c, q are unmeasured in the campaign. An operator finishing this paper knows to be careful and nothing else. Fix: give one worked, measurable instantiation (e.g., Wilson-upper-bound triage already in the controller matrix) with its charged-cost accounting on the accepted corpora — descriptive, no new training needed — or label the paragraph as agenda, not consequence.

**R3-W2 — Cost model ignores the dominant real costs the campaign itself incurred.** Anchor: Prop. 2 counts rollouts; campaign ledger charges generation/diagnostic/evaluation (`main.tex:406-408`, verifier checks four FLOP fields). Diagnostic double-backward passes and corpus generation — the conformance overhead a practitioner must pay to *use* this protocol — are never totalled in-paper against the 396k–400k charged tokens. Fix: report protocol overhead (diagnostic FLOPs / optimizer FLOPs) from the existing ledgers; a conformance method must price itself.

**R3-W3 — Reproducibility burden is transferred, not discharged.** Anchor: `main.tex:756-760`: checkpoints/corpora/predictions "checked only for hash format, which does not authenticate them." Combined with R1-W1, an independent reader can verify internal consistency of JSON the authors generated, and nothing about whether those JSONs came from the claimed A100 runs. That is provenance *formatting*, one tier below the paper's rhetoric. Fix: ship at least one unit's corpus + checkpoint (or a small-model CPU end-to-end rerun) so one gradient receipt is independently recomputable; state the trust tier plainly in the abstract-level claims.

## 3.5 Devil's Advocate

### Strongest Counter-Argument (≈260 words)

This manuscript is an audit trail in search of a result. Strip the scaffolding and three claims remain: homogeneous binary groups occur with probability p^G+(1−p)^G (prior work, §2); waiting for a rare success costs ~1/ε samples (a one-line union bound dressed as Proposition 2); and if one *assumes* a batch is worth buying under E_q (Δ>0) and worthless under E₀, then the two states prefer opposite actions (Proposition 3 — the assumption *is* the theorem, with the observed history n explicitly doing no work). The empirical programme that was supposed to give these observations teeth collapsed in an instructive but fatal way: the positive control was unbuildable because thinking-mode generation saturates the 512-token cap — a property a one-group pilot would have revealed before freezing a 31-job registration — and the negative control was constructed so that the two reductions under test are *identical* and the two objectives differ only by a known scalar, guaranteeing the near-unit cosines the abstract then reports as findings. The single completed mechanism cell failed its own gate for reasons the authors did not isolate, in a paper whose thesis is that failures must be isolated before interpretation. What the elaborate receipt machinery finally certifies is the internal consistency of JSON files, verified by a script that fails on the very repository it ships in, over gradient numbers nobody can recompute because checkpoints and corpora are private and their hashes are checked "only for format." Publish this and the field learns that one group's campaign did not run — documented beautifully. Documentation is not a contribution until someone else can build on it, and nothing here — no released corpus, no recomputable run, no non-degenerate control, no usable controller rule — can be built on.

### Issue List

- **DA-CRITICAL-1 (evidence tier vs. claim; §5/Table `tab:status`):** "Verified" labels rest on receipt-internal consistency, not recomputation; live-tree verifier fails (R1-W1, R3-W3). The paper's core promise is exactly the tier it does not reach.
- **DA-CRITICAL-2 (circular validation; §5.3):** The negative control cannot fail on reduction (reductions coincide) and cannot fail on direction (scalar-multiple gradients); its only live degree of freedom — relative-ℓ₂ — failed unexplained. Self-referential: receipts generated by the campaign code are "verified" by checking the receipts' own fields (`verify_claims.py` campaign scope; CLAIM_AUDIT C11 "Receipt-verified").
- **DA-CRITICAL-3 (claims stronger than evidence; §4.3/title):** "Decision Insufficiency" from a Δ>0 two-action witness (R1-W4). Null-result over-read risk is low (authors are careful), but title-level over-read by *citing* papers is near-certain.
- **DA-MAJOR-1 (foreseeable design failure; §5.2):** CV≥0.35 positive control under a saturating 512-token thinking-mode contract; no pre-freeze pilot evidence is shown. Preregistration without a feasibility pilot converted a debugging step into 14 descoped jobs.
- **DA-MAJOR-2 (salami/overlap, visible from repo text):** Three same-programme manuscripts share the starvation spine: this paper; "Zero Advantage Is Not Missing Information" (spectral auxiliary advantages; shares bib — hence 17 uncited entries — and background claims); "When Can We Skip a Group?" (next-submission skeleton testing the controller this paper only proposes). Individually defensible; collectively they slice one programme (background → protocol → intervention) into minimum publishable units, and the dual NeurIPS/TMLR builds of *this* text compound the concern. Cross-citation and a programme map are absent from the reviewed text.
- **DA-MAJOR-3 (reproducibility receipts not binding to claimed runs):** W&B/HF identifiers are asserted present by the verifier but no independent anchor (public commit, timestamped log) is opened in-review; acceptance = provenance state by the authors' own definition (`main.tex:601-603`).
- **DA-MINOR-1:** Gate redundancy (R1-W3) and dead controller branches (R1-W6) — preregistration quality issues in a preregistration-advocacy paper.
- **DA-MINOR-2:** Bibliography pollution and uncited PPO (R2-W3); 2026 arXiv IDs unverifiable in this review (Unresolved).

### Ignored Alternative Explanations

(1) The 0.0177 relative-ℓ₂ residual may be a genuine objective-implementation difference (e.g., padding/masking in the native path on the 2/100 non-4096-token steps, `main.tex:688-690`) rather than bf16 noise — never tested. (2) The 62–65% joint-zero rate may reflect parser strictness or prompt-difficulty mismatch rather than latent-p rarity; no reward-distribution diagnostic is shown. (3) Native "material differences" in S1 may be reference-choice artefacts (the reference is the authors' encoding; TRL's native semantics are the *deployed* semantics for most users, inverting who should conform to whom).

### Missing Stakeholder Perspectives

Framework maintainers (would they accept this reference as normative?); practitioners who need a *default action* under uncertainty, not abstention; compute providers/funders reading a 6-of-24-units campaign as resource stewardship; future meta-scientists needing machine-readable failure taxonomies rather than prose postmortems.

### Observations (Non-Defects)

The refusal to weaken the CV gate post hoc, the exhaustive 31-ID crosswalk, null-cosine storage, and the explicit "not a scientific negative result" framing (`main.tex:85-89`) are genuinely good practice and rarer than they should be. Proposition 2's all-G bound is correct and cleanly proved.

### So-what test

If this paper disappeared, what breaks? Practitioners keep using TRL/verl defaults; theorists lose nothing (results are corollaries/witnesses); the only loss is a worked example of fail-closed reporting. That example has value *iff* its receipts bind and one run is recomputable — currently they bind only inside the zip and no run is recomputable. So-what: **not yet passed.**

## Editorial Decision Letter

**Decision: Major Revision** (for a reproducibility / negative-results / TMLR-type track). For a NeurIPS-main-track reading of the same text, this panel's decision would be **Reject**; the authors must choose and declare one target (EIC-W1).

**Consensus:** All seats agree the headline numbers trace faithfully to the frozen receipts (independently reproduced), the claim-bounding is honest, and the campaign establishes no causal/equivalence/controller result. All seats agree the decision witness is valid-but-thin and the balanced control is degenerate. **Disagreement:** EIC/R3 see a publishable protocol paper after reframing; DA argues the contribution collapses without a recomputable run (DA-CRITICAL-1/2). R2 is the most sympathetic to S1 as infrastructure; R1 conditions everything on isolating the relative-ℓ₂ residual.

**Adjudication of DA CRITICALs.** DA-CRITICAL-1: **validated in part** — bundle-internal verification passes and numbers trace, but live-tree failure + format-only hash checks cap the evidence tier below the rhetoric; blocks Accept until R1-W1/R3-W3 remedies land. DA-CRITICAL-2: **validated** — the control's degeneracy is admitted in-text and the unexplained residual is real; blocks any conformance-success claim (the paper, to its credit, claims gate *failure*, which is why this is Major Revision rather than Reject). DA-CRITICAL-3: **validated as framing, rejected as correctness** — the proposition is true; the title/abstract strength is not. No CRITICAL is dismissed; none, given the authors' own non-claims, is irreparable without new GPU runs except full recomputability, for which a single small recomputable unit suffices.

**Separately:** *Scientific validity* — sound within stated scopes; no incorrect proof or fabricated number found. *Venue fit* — poor for main-track ML, plausible for reproducibility/TMLR after reframing. *Submission readiness* — not ready: verifier/repo divergence, bib pollution, and title over-strength are submittability defects regardless of venue.
