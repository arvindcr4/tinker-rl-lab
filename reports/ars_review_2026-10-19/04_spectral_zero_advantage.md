## Seat 2 — R1, Methodology

**Strengths (anchored).** The causal boundary is drawn correctly and precisely: detachment after standardization (Eq. 3, lines 203–211; verified at `objective.py:169`, `250`), gradient defined as `∇_z L` over the log-probability tensor rather than parameters (lines 225–228), no KL/entropy term in the surrogate Eq. (4) and the zero-gradient claim scoped accordingly (lines 228–230). The prospective design in §7 is the strongest section: alignment before training, variance-matched random-score placebo, budget-matched baseline, Holm-adjusted named contrasts, and explicit falsification criteria (lines 506–512) are exactly the controls this literature usually omits.

**R1-W1 — The replay never exercises the paper's central defect.** Anchor: defective padded-grid rule, lines 247–250 ("Padding also contracts the active domain"); replay specification, lines 373–375 ("all rows and tokens active"); `replay.json:grid` (9 cells, full-length only). What is wrong: the audit's headline numerical claim is that masking/padding corrupts Eq. (5), yet every replay cell uses complete masks, so the corruption is asserted analytically and measured nowhere in this revision — the withdrawn quadrature table was not replaced with even a two-row masked-vs-active comparison. The one unequal-length check (lines 423–425) validates the reward-only derivative, not the spectral projection. Fix: add masked cells to the replay (e.g., `L_eff ∈ {L/4, L/2}` per row) reporting coefficient error against the active-token trapezoid reference the paper itself specifies (lines 251–258); this is cheap, CPU-only, and converts the central defect from assertion to measurement.

**R1-W2 — The synthetic fixture can only confirm what it injects (circular validation).** Anchor: fixture definition, lines 376–381 (`z_iℓ = b_i q_ℓ + 0.01ξ`, `b = linspace(−0.1, 0.1, G)`, features `(z_iℓ, t_ℓ)`); result, lines 411–413. What is wrong: per-completion offsets `b_i` are the only systematic between-completion variation; the second feature channel `t_ℓ` is identical across completions and therefore contributes exactly zero to every pairwise distance `d_N(i,j)`, so the spectral score is, up to noise, a monotone function of the injected offsets. A nonzero homogeneous gradient and its exact λ-linearity then follow from Eq. (3) by construction — the "stress test" has no failure mode. Relatedly, with `z^old = 0` and `|z| ≲ 0.1`, importance ratios sit inside the clip band `[0.8, 1.28]`, so the `min(·)` clipping branch of Eq. (4) is never exercised and the cosine column of Table 1 characterizes only the linear regime. Fix: add adversarial fixtures the score should *not* separate (identical trajectories at different lengths/padding; permuted-token controls; ratios driven outside the clip band) and report where the conclusion changes; state plainly in §6 that D=2 is effectively D=1 for pairwise purposes.

**R1-W3 — The entropy gate is dead by construction, yet retains a hypothesis and a section.** Anchor: gate definition, lines 357–365 (per-completion softmax over *token positions*, "not a vocabulary probability distribution"); entropy range 6.23793–7.62457 vs. `τ = 0.5`, lines 417–422; H3, lines 445–446. What is wrong: entropy of an L-position softmax scales towards `log L` (≈6.24 at L=512), so `τ = 0.5` is more than an order of magnitude below every attainable value in the replay; the gate fires in 0 of 18 comparisons and the `τ = 10` control proves only branch reachability. The paper discloses all of this honestly, but then H3 and §5 still present gating as a live component rather than a diagnosed dead branch awaiting the normalized redefinition deferred to lines 498–500. Fix: either implement the mask-aware `H/log L_eff` gate now and re-run the (cheap) replay, or demote H3/§5 explicitly to "component as implemented is vacuous; retained only as audit subject."

**R1-W4 — Reproducibility receipt does not bind to the committed sources.** Anchor: Appendix, lines 546–549 ("Its `source_sha256` map identifies the four implementation files used"); `replay.json:source_sha256` records `objective.py = a744a5c5…`, while the committed `zvf-program/flagship/pilot/objective.py` hashes to `20f420f3…` (the other three files match). What is wrong: as committed, a strict verifier following the appendix fails on 1 of 4 files. I re-ran the recompute script: every number reproduces exactly under the current source, so the drift is numerically benign (consistent with the intervening formatting commit in `git log`), but the receipt as written is presently false. Fix: regenerate `replay.json` from the committed tree (one command, already scripted) so hashes and numbers bind simultaneously, and record the git commit alongside the hashes.

**R1-W5 — Code contract is narrower and less guarded than the mathematics states.** Anchor: manuscript scope `a = |I| ≥ 2` (line 163) and "Single-selected-row sample variance is undefined and is outside the replay's contract" (lines 177–178) vs. `objective.py:77–80` (G=8 groups restricted to exactly 6 or 8 active rows; single active row permitted) and `objective.py:107–111, 163–167` (`std(correction=1)` on one row yields NaN, and `NaN ≤ 1e-12` is false, so NaN advantages propagate instead of the stated zero). What is wrong: the paper's formulas claim generality the artifact rejects (e.g., 7-of-8 selection raises), and the disclosed-but-unenforced a≥2 precondition fails silently rather than loudly. Fix: disclose the {6,8}-of-8 restriction in §3 or §6, and make the artifact raise on a<2 so the contract the paper states is the contract the code enforces.

**R1-W6 — Prospective statistics specified in form, not magnitude.** Anchor: gate sizes (lines 461–471), Eq. (9) `+2` (lines 475–490). What is wrong: nothing justifies 2,000+2,000 groups for a prompt-clustered concordance bound; constant-secondary-label groups — plausibly the majority — are excluded from the AUC (lines 464–466), so the gate could pass on a small unrepresentative subset; `+2` is undefined. Fix: size by target interval width under clustering, require a minimum discordant fraction, define or drop `+2`.

## Seat 3 — R2, Domain

**Strengths (anchored).** Positioning against constituents is unusually candid: "We claim no novelty for its constituents" (lines 156–158), the "quantum-inspired" label of the earlier draft is withdrawn by name (lines 154–156), and the closest-by-purpose work is correctly identified as within-group hidden-state distance credit assignment (`chen2026shear`, lines 137–139) with entropy-guided shaping (`le2025noprompt`, lines 140–141) as the direct zero-variance competitor. All spot-checked 2026 references are real. The exactness boundary in §4.1 — Parseval holds for the reconstructed expansion, not for the equispaced estimator of the unknown trajectory (lines 283–292) — is the kind of distinction this subfield frequently blurs, and it is stated correctly.

**R2-W1 — The feasibility floor is presented as a contribution where the cited literature already owns it.** Anchor: contribution 4 (lines 100–102) and floor derivation (lines 196–200) vs. `nie2026gradient` (cited lines 77, 128), whose abstract proves the degeneracy rate exceeds the i.i.d. Bernoulli prediction via Jensen and reports a 0.69 empirical rate at G=4. What is wrong: `min_p p^G + (1−p)^G = 2^{1−G}` is a one-line corollary of the identity the field already uses, and under the realistic prompt-mixture view (per-prompt `p_x` varying), the operational homogeneous rate is governed by the difficulty distribution, not by this pointwise floor — a limitation the manuscript never discusses even though it determines when the "check" bites. Fix: demote the floor to a remarked corollary with explicit credit, and add the mixture caveat: state what the floor can and cannot falsify once `p` is prompt-dependent.

**R2-W2 — The score's direction is outlier-seeking, and the paper never confronts the sign problem.** Anchor: score definition `s_i = (a−1)⁻¹ Σ_j d_N(i,j)` (Eq. 6, lines 259–265); combined advantage with `λ ≥ 0` implied throughout (Table 1; "matching positive λ," line 469); Limitations, lines 516–518. What is wrong: mean pairwise distance is maximized by the group's most atypical completion. With positive λ, an all-wrong group upweights its most unusual wrong answer and an all-correct group downweights its most canonical correct answer. That may be defensible as an exploration bonus, but it is a substantive domain commitment — arguably backwards for the all-correct stratum — and the manuscript reduces it to one clause ("may reward novelty"). No mechanistic hypothesis is offered for why low-order Legendre modes of a log-probability (or hidden-state) trace should track correctness at all, beyond "trajectories differ." Fix: analyze the outlier-seeking geometry explicitly, consider the signed/median-centered alternatives, and state stratum-specific directional predictions (all-correct vs. all-wrong) that the §7 alignment gate would then test rather than merely screen.

**R2-W3 — The hidden-state path, which carries the domain motivation, is entirely unexercised.** Anchor: "either supplied hidden states or a two-channel fixture" (lines 234–236); all replay features are `(z_iℓ, t_ℓ)` (lines 379–381); `objective.py:139–144`. What is wrong: related work motivates trajectory geometry via hidden states (HiPPO/LMU, SHEAR), but every number in the paper comes from the log-probability-plus-position fixture, whose spectral content is dominated by a smooth ramp (see R1-W2). Layer choice, hidden dimension D≫2 behavior of the Givens schedule (`entropic_gating.py:84–92` targets only tail coordinates), and cost at realistic D are all unknown, yet §7 proposes jumping directly to 1–8B fine-tuning. Fix: scope all motivation sentences to the fixture actually studied, and insert an offline hidden-state trace study (frozen checkpoints, no training) as a gate before any fine-tuning design.

**R2-W4 — Citation hygiene: present-but-uncited canonical entries, missing numerical-analysis anchor.** Anchor: `spectral_audit.bib` (29 entries) vs. 18 distinct `\citep` keys in the text; GSM8K/MATH named without citation at lines 429, 450–451 despite `cobbe2021gsm8k`/`hendrycks2021math` sitting in the bib; clipped surrogate Eq. (4) without `schulman2017ppo`; first- vs. second-order quadrature claims (lines 288–292) with no source. What is wrong: eleven bib entries are never cited, two task names and the PPO clip go unattributed where used, and the order-of-accuracy assertions — load-bearing for the "defective rule" narrative — cite no quadrature reference and give no derivation. Fix: cite the task and PPO sources at first use, prune or cite the remaining entries, and add a standard numerical-analysis reference (or a short appendix derivation) for the equispaced-rule error order. Separately, the std-normalization-bias branch of the GRPO literature (the Dr.GRPO line of work on removing the `1/σ` term) is the obvious missing interlocutor for Eq. (1)'s denominator choice; engage that kind of source when motivating why this objective standardizes reward but epsilon-pads only the auxiliary.

## Seat 5 — Devil's Advocate

### Strongest Counter-Argument

Strip the exemplary bookkeeping away and ask what a skeptical reader learns that was not known before page one. That a centered advantage vanishes when its inputs are identical is arithmetic, not a finding. That Parseval holds for an orthogonal expansion is a textbook identity; that an equispaced sum is not Gauss–Legendre quadrature is a numerical-analysis platitude; that projecting an already-zero coordinate changes nothing is a tautology the paper itself calls "redundant by construction." The one empirical table reports that multiplying a term by λ scales its gradient by λ — a result obtainable by differentiating Eq. (3) on paper, confirmed by a fixture whose only between-completion signal was injected precisely so the score could find it, at a single seed, in a regime where the surrogate's clipping never engages and the headline gating component never fires. Meanwhile the floor result is a corollary of work the paper cites, which additionally supplies the stronger Jensen bound and real training logs. So the manuscript is a rigorous proof that the author's own earlier prototype — "quantum-inspired," benchmark-claiming, now withdrawn — does not work, followed by a competent protocol for experiments not yet run. The field does not lack for unpublished prototypes that do not work; it lacks evidence about which homogeneous-group remedies do. Publishing this at a top venue would reward the *form* of rigor (ledgers, hashes, withdrawals) while the *content* remains three trivialities, a null, and a plan — and would consume a slot, and reader attention, that the paper's own §7 gate could have converted into actual knowledge for roughly the cost of the relabeling study it describes. Honesty is necessary; it is not, by itself, a contribution.

### Issue List

**DA-CRITICAL-1 — Evidence tier: every retained empirical claim is tautological, null, or unmeasured** (dimension: claims vs. evidence; location: Table 1 lines 387–409 with lines 411–413; gate null lines 417–422; masked-defect unmeasured per R1-W1). λ-linearity is algebra, the gate result is "branch never taken," and the padded-grid defect has no measurement. There is no number in the paper whose value was uncertain before the code ran. *So-what test:* delete §6 entirely and no conclusion changes — the surest sign the empirics carry no weight.

**DA-CRITICAL-2 — Circular/self-referential validation** (dimension: validation design; location: fixture lines 376–381, score Eq. 6). The fixture injects ordered offsets `b_i`; the score is a distance that must recover them; the position channel cancels identically. The replay therefore cannot distinguish "spectral projection works" from "any injective per-completion statistic works" — token-mean log-probability would pass the same battery. No placebo statistic is run in the replay even though §7 (correctly) demands one prospectively.

**DA-CRITICAL-3 — Fragmentation with a live stale duplicate** (dimension: publication integrity; location: EIC-W2 anchors). Same thesis/artifact/floor split across this manuscript and "Same Terminal Signal, Different Action," plus a divergent duplicate of this very text retaining withdrawn abstract language. Even absent intent, parallel submission of overlapping contributions without cross-disclosure is the salami pattern, and the duplicate invites accidental submission of disowned claims.

**DA-MAJOR-1 — Receipt binding failure, benign but real** (dimension: reproducibility; location: R1-W4). The appendix's hash claim is false as committed for `objective.py`. Verified non-fatal by re-execution in this review; still, a paper whose selling point is receipts must have receipts that verify.

**DA-MAJOR-2 — Unanalyzed harmful-direction risk** (dimension: objective design; location: R2-W2). Positive-λ outlier-seeking in all-correct groups penalizes canonical solutions; the paper proposes training-scale tests of a score whose expected sign per stratum is never reasoned about. A negative §7 outcome would then be uninformative about whether trajectory geometry failed or merely this aggregation did.

**DA-MAJOR-3 — Null result over-read in reverse** (dimension: interpretation; location: lines 417–422, abstract lines 64–66). "Gate never fires" is a property of an unnormalized threshold against a `log L`-scale statistic, not evidence about entropic gating as an idea; the text mostly respects this, but the abstract's compression invites readers to file gating itself as audited-and-null.

**DA-MINOR-1 — Hedged algebra** (location: line 411, "approximately `0.0187848λ`"). For λ≥0 in the unclipped regime the scaling is exact up to floating point; "approximately" lends empirical texture to an identity.

**DA-MINOR-2 — Proposition theater** (location: lines 180–187). A two-line arithmetic observation in theorem dress inflates the apparent theory content; remark status would be proportionate.

### Ignored Alternative Explanations

(i) Homogeneous groups may be *informative at the prompt level* (difficulty signal for curriculum/filtering) even when they carry no within-group contrast — the paper's within-group framing silently discards this use. (ii) The observed 0.69 degeneracy rate in cited training logs suggests mixture-of-difficulties dominates; under that explanation the binding constraint is prompt distribution, not advantage algebra, and neither floor nor auxiliary score is the right lever. (iii) Any future alignment of `s_i` with corrected labels could be mediated entirely by length or mean-log-probability confounds; §7 residualizes length/entropy (line 466) but the present fixture already entangles score with injected confidence offsets, previewing exactly that failure.

### Missing Stakeholder Perspectives

Verifier maintainers (whose false negatives manufacture all-wrong groups), data curators (difficulty filtering as the cheapest degeneracy control), and training-infrastructure owners (per-step overhead and determinism costs of an O(G²·N·D) pairwise score at production scale) are absent. The paper speaks only to objective designers.

### Observations (Non-Defects)

The withdrawal discipline, claim-to-file appendix, and falsification criteria should be copied regardless of this paper's fate. The duplicate manuscript copy (DA-CRITICAL-3) is a process hazard, not evidence of misconduct. The stale hash (DA-MAJOR-1) was shown numerically harmless by direct re-execution during this review.

## Editorial Decision Letter

**Decision: Reject** — for the inferred top-tier main-track venue, with an explicit resubmission path (execute the §7 alignment gate and return as an empirical paper, or retarget the current text to a negative-results/reproducibility workshop after Must-fix items).

**Consensus vs. disagreement.** All five seats agree the retained claims are *correct* and the evidence hygiene is exemplary; all five agree the contribution weight is insufficient for the inferred venue. Seats differ on salvage value: R1/R3 see a strong workshop paper already present; the Devil's Advocate argues even that over-credits form over content unless R1-W1's masked measurement is added. R2 is the most concerned about the score's unexamined direction (R2-W2); EIC is the most concerned about fragmentation (EIC-W2). No seat recommends acceptance in any main-track reading.

**Devil's Advocate CRITICAL adjudication.** DA-CRITICAL-1 (tautological/null empirics): **validated** — confirmed by inspection of Eq. (3), Table 1, and `replay.json`; blocks Accept. DA-CRITICAL-2 (circular fixture): **validated** — the position channel cancels identically and offsets are injected; blocks any reading of §6 as validation, though the paper itself never claims otherwise, which mitigates severity from fatal-flaw to contribution-weight. DA-CRITICAL-3 (fragmentation/stale duplicate): **validated as a process defect** — overlap and the divergent duplicate are on disk and undisclosed in the text; blocks submission until disclosed and de-duplicated, independent of scientific merit. No CRITICAL is rejected; none is left unresolved.

**Separate judgments.** *Scientific validity:* sound within its narrow scope — identities correct, numbers traced and reproduced, overclaiming absent; the failures are of novelty and evidential weight, not correctness. *Venue fit:* poor for a main-track ML conference; good for a negative-results, reproducibility, or "science of deep learning" workshop. *Submission readiness:* not ready — non-anonymous preprint form (EIC-W3), stale hash binding (R1-W4), undisclosed companion overlap and duplicate copy (EIC-W2).
