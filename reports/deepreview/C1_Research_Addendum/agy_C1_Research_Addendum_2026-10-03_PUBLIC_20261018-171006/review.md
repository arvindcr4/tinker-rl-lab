## Summary

This manuscript reports a frozen policy diagnostic and reference review (Campaign C1) on the GSM8K training split (`openai/gsm8k`, revision `740312add88f781978c0658806c59bc2815b9866`) using `Qwen/Qwen3-8B` (revision `b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218`) with thinking disabled. The study screens 4,096 fixed-order questions with 8 decoded responses each. It identifies 87 eligible questions where all 8 responses are parseable, uncapped, and receive zero exact-match reward from frozen parser v2. It then deterministically selects the first 64 eligible questions and draws 24 fresh responses per question. Under frozen parser v2, 11/64 questions (17.19%; Wilson 95% CI: 9.88–28.21%) achieve at least one exact-match hit. However, two rounds of assistant-assisted reference and response audits reduce the corroborated mathematical recovery count to 3/64 (4.69%). The remaining 8 parser events consist of 3 convention-dependent disagreements, 4 materially ambiguous questions, and 1 match to an erroneous reference answer (`C35`, where all 8 initial answers were actually correct). Across all 64 selected questions, the second review flags 16 clear wrong reference final values, 19 materially ambiguous questions, 2 inconsistent questions, and 4 correct final values with flawed reference explanations. A separate section evaluates an undeployed parser v3 candidate on the final 256 screening questions (2,048 outputs), showing 4 new acceptances and 0 regressions among 2,025 previously valid parses.

## Strengths

- **Clear separation of parser exact-match and mathematical correctness**: The report demonstrates that conditioning on $0/8$ exact-match failures enriches benchmark defects. Only 3 of the 11 subsequent parser hits represent unambiguous mathematical recovery, while 1 hit (`C35`) flips a correct initial answer (`8`) to match a faulty gold label (`6`).
- **Explicit scope and claim hygiene**: The manuscript explicitly states what the diagnostic cannot establish. It avoids claiming population error rates, reinforcement-learning gradient utility, or bitwise hardware reproducibility.
- **Granular accounting of selected cohorts**: Table 1, Table 2, Table 3, and Table 4 account for all 64 selected questions, all 11 parser events, and the exact fresh sample indices that triggered parser rewards.
- **Separation of final-value accuracy from reasoning validity**: In `C00` (Santana presents), the audit notes that fresh index 12 reaches the right number (`8`) via flawed reasoning (`8 - 0`), whereas indices 15 and 23 use valid arithmetic (`11 - 3`).

## Weaknesses

- **Stand-alone scientific incompleteness and missing raw responses**: The manuscript is a derivative research addendum rather than a self-contained ML paper. It omits problem statements, model outputs, parser v2/v3 regular expressions, and assistant review prompts. Furthermore, `[P1]` excludes the raw response corpus and provides only content hashes, which prevents independent verification of the 655 audited responses.
- **Unexamined truncation bias (64 of 87 eligible cases)**: Selecting the first 64 of 87 eligible cases truncates the tail of the deterministic SHA-256 ordering. Because the prospective parser v3 check targets the last 256 screening questions (ranks 3840–4095) and the 64 selected cases stop at rank 3597 (`C63`), the 23 dropped eligible questions overlap with the parser v3 evaluation window without cross-analysis.
- **No human validation of assistant-assisted audits**: All reference reviews, blind reconciliations, and parser v3 checks rely on assistant-assisted annotations. The manuscript does not specify which LLM(s) served as the assistant reviewer or quantify human-assistant agreement.
- **Incomplete inspection of non-event follow-up responses**: The response audit inspects only 143 of the 1,536 fresh responses. It does not check whether the 53 non-event questions contain mathematically correct fresh responses that failed to match the 15+ wrong or 19 ambiguous GSM8K reference answers.
- **Undefined "Historical V2" baseline and "historical smoke cohort"**: The text repeatedly references "Historical V2 evidence" and a "disjoint historical smoke cohort" (1,024 draws) without defining their origin, methodology, or relevance to C1.

## Anchored comments

- `[CRITICAL] p.1: "The public document does not provide full execution provenance... not sufficient to independently reconstruct or authenticate the full execution history."` - A top-tier ML venue requires reproducible artifacts or verifiable raw outputs. Releasing only SHA-256 hashes of model generations (`[P1]`) makes it impossible for reviewers or readers to verify the reasoning audits or parser extractions. Release the full text of all 34,304 screening and follow-up responses (or at minimum all 2,048 responses for the 64 selected cases) in `[P1]`.
- `[CRITICAL] p.2: "All stages were assistant-assisted. Separate masking and repeat review do not provide independent human validation or rule out shared model biases."` - Relying on an unspecified assistant model to adjudicate benchmark ground truth and Qwen3-8B reasoning introduces correlated failure modes, yet the paper never names the judge model or prompts. Identify the exact model(s), temperatures, and system prompts used for Round 1, Round 2, reconciliation, and parser v3 annotations, and add an independent human audit on a stratified subset (or all 64 questions).
- `[MAJOR] p.1: "After the full screen, the first 64 of 87 eligible questions received 24 additional responses each. Selection was truncated deterministically; the 23 other eligible questions were not followed up."` - Deterministic truncation at 64 cases leaves 26.4% of the eligible cohort unexamined (`1,536` vs. `2,088` fresh draws) and creates an arbitrary cutoff at source rank 3597 (`C63`). Generate the remaining $23 \times 24 = 552$ follow-up draws (or report the exact reference-audit categories for the 23 excluded eligible questions) to eliminate truncation bias across the 4,096-question screen.
- `[MAJOR] p.2: "The first output audit covers all 64 cases, all 512 initial responses and 143 required fresh responses: 655 responses in total, including all 38 primary-success outputs."` - Auditing only 143 of 1,536 fresh responses leaves 1,393 fresh responses unexamined. In the 16 cases with a wrong reference answer (`W`), a fresh response that computes the true mathematical answer is scored as `0` by the parser and remains uncounted as a mathematical recovery unless audited. Audit the fresh responses for the 15 non-event `W` cases (360 responses) against the corrected mathematical ground truth to measure true mathematical recovery when the gold label is wrong.
- `[MAJOR] p.2: "The first audit identified five frozen-v2 extraction false negatives and no extraction false positives within its reviewed set"` - If frozen parser v2 suffered 5 extraction false negatives inside the reviewed set of 655 responses, some initial screening responses may have actually contained the reference answer (violating the $0/8$ eligibility rule) or some fresh draws may have achieved uncounted matches. Specify the exact case IDs (`C00`–`C63`) and response indices where these 5 extraction false negatives occurred, and state whether any occurred in the 512 initial screening outputs.
- `[MAJOR] p.4: "The four correct-final cases in the refined second review and the three corroborated correct-final defects are different sets: a notation defect newly identified for Sonia/Joss is not corroborated in both rounds."` - The prose names "Sonia/Joss" without linking it to a case identifier in Table 4 (`C28`, `C38`, `C45`, or `C55`), making it impossible to trace which `R` case corresponds to Sonia/Joss and which 3 `R` cases are corroborated across both rounds. Include the exact extraction specification/regexes for parser v2 and parser v3, and report a secondary table showing how parser v3 scores the 512 initial and 1,536 fresh outputs of the 64 selected cases.

Remove the smoke cohort from the main workload accounting or explain what quality-control metric it established.

## Questions for the authors

1. **Assistant reviewer identity and human oversight**: Which specific LLM(s) and prompting configurations performed the first review, the masked second review, the reconciliation, and the parser v3 annotations? Did a human author independently verify all 64 reference classifications and 126 event responses?
2. **Location and impact of the 5 parser v2 extraction false negatives**: In which specific cases (`C00`–`C63`) and draw indices did the 5 frozen-v2 extraction false negatives occur? Did any of them occur during the initial 8 screening draws, meaning the model actually solved the problem during screening?
3. **True recovery rate on wrong-reference (`W`) questions**: Among the 15 non-event cases classified as having a wrong reference value (`W`), did the model produce the mathematically correct answer in any of the 8 initial draws or 24 fresh draws?
4. **Remaining 23 eligible questions**: What are the source ranks of the 23 eligible questions that fell after rank 3597 (`C63`), and how many of them fall inside the last 256 screening questions used for the prospective parser v3 validation?

## Overall score (1-10) and confidence (1-5)

- **Overall score**: **3 / 10** (Clear Reject for a main conference track at NeurIPS/ICLR. While the audit is transparent, methodologically self-aware, and surfaces concrete GSM8K reference defects—showing that 8 of 11 apparent rollout recoveries on $0/8$-screened items are artifacts of benchmark noise, ambiguity, or wrong gold answers—the submission is an internal campaign addendum (`PUBLIC revision / Scientific-only derivative of the research addendum`) rather than a complete research paper. It lacks related work, formal definitions of parser v2/v3, disclosure of the assistant-reviewer models, audit coverage of non-event wrong-reference rollouts, and complete data provenance.)
