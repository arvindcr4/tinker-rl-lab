## Summary

This manuscript introduces **GRPO-Stack-Audit**, a single-stack preregistered audit protocol for evaluating Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) variants (DAPO, GSPO, Dr.GRPO, and AERO). The authors implement each variant as an isolated hook on a shared Qwen3-8B trainer and run an 8-seed, 30-step experiment on GSM8K across 40 total arm–seed units. Every unit passes a strict artifact reconciliation gate across local manifests, Weights & Biases (W&B) logs, private Hugging Face Hub checkpoints, and 500-prompt held-out evaluations. Using a post-execution finite-sample statistical correction (exact noncentral paired-$t$ power and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control), all four variant comparisons against baseline GRPO yield an `INCONCLUSIVE` verdict. Neither a directional gain nor formal equivalence within the $\pm 0.01$ margin is established ($MDE_{80} \ge 0.01012 > 0.01$). As a secondary cost observation, DAPO drives the late-training zero-variance fraction ($Z_{\text{vf}}$) from $0.693$ to $0.000$, but requires $3.61\times$ more rollouts ($1734$ vs. $480$) and $1.44\times$ more wall-clock time ($4.47\text{ h}$ vs. $3.11\text{ h}$). The paper also reports a small T4 telemetry smoke test ($n=1$ per arm) and two bounded $G=2$ vs. $G=16$ trajectory panels.

---

## Strengths

1. **Clear Isolation of Stack vs. Algorithmic Deltas:** Holding the base checkpoint, LoRA configuration, rollout engine, KL placement, reward parser, and evaluation harness fixed addresses a major confounding factor in the RL-for-LLMs literature.
2. **Honest Disclosure of Negative Results and Engineering Failures:** The paper transparently documents the invalidation of an unwired `--loss drgrpo` flag (p. 2), the post-execution statistical correction that downgraded DAPO from `DISAPPEARS` to `INCONCLUSIVE` (p. 6), and three post-acceptance recovery-finalizer repairs (p. 7).
3. **Rigorous Artifact Provenance Gates:** Requiring reconciliation across local records, W&B runs, checkpoint trees, stack/treatment fingerprints, and 500-row hashed evaluation traces sets a strong operational standard for empirical audits.
4. **Separation of Capability Claims from Compute Cost:** Framing DAPO's zero $Z_{\text{vf}}$ and $3.61\times$ rollout overhead as a mechanical cost observation rather than a capability improvement avoids overclaiming from underpowered accuracy deltas.

## Weaknesses

1. **Design Is Structurally Incapable of Reaching the Equivalence Verdict (`DISAPPEARS`):** The preregistered protocol sets an equivalence margin of $m = 0.01$ with $S = 8$ seeds and $500$ held-out prompts (pp. 3, 5). Even on the lowest-variance comparison (DAPO), the achieved $MDE_{80}$ is $0.01012 > 0.01$ (Table 1, p. 7). As the authors admit on p. 7 ("`disappears` was unreachable a priori at n=8"), the core audit was underpowered by design to confirm one of its primary preregistered verdicts. *[Superseded-verdict note (2026-10-18): the earlier DAPO `DISAPPEARS` verdict is superseded by `INCONCLUSIVE`; see zvf-program/audit/STATISTICAL_REANALYSIS.md.]*
2. **Severe Toy Horizon and Batch Scale (30 Steps, Batch Size 4):** The primary audit runs for only 30 training steps with batch size 4 (120 prompts total, 480 baseline rollouts) using LoRA on Qwen3-8B (p. 3). Published GRPO variants (DAPO, GSPO, Dr.GRPO, AERO) are designed for long-horizon RL scaling where length bias, entropy collapse, and clipping dynamics emerge over hundreds or thousands of steps. Auditing them at 30 steps conflates "no effect at initialization-scale horizon" with "inconclusive algorithmic delta."
3. **Evaluator Replay Instability Exceeds Reported Point Deltas:** On p. 9 ("Replay stability"), replaying GRPO seed 11 shifted its held-out score by $+0.004$ ($0.646 \to 0.650$, 2 of 500 rows changing from unparsed to correct). This $+0.004$ evaluator non-determinism is $4\times$ larger than DAPO's point delta ($+0.00100$) and $80\%$ of GSPO's point delta ($+0.00500$). Furthermore, changing seed 11 by $+0.004$ shifts the 8-seed GRPO mean by $+0.0005$, which directly alters the paired differences reported in Table 1.
4. **Hook Interface Cannot Express the Audited Variants:** The `VariantHook` class in §2 (p. 4) only exposes `on_group_rewards`, `on_advantage`, `on_loss`, and `group_size_rule(step, zvf)`. This interface cannot express DAPO (which requires dynamic rejection resampling loops until $0 < \text{sum(rewards)} < G$ and asymmetric token-level clip bounds inside the PPO ratio), GSPO (which replaces token-level importance ratios with sequence-level likelihood ratios), or AERO (which requires two-stage rollout allocation, selective rejection, and posterior-guided group sampling).
5. **Severe Manuscript Hygiene Failures, Broken LaTeX Tables, Unrendered Placeholder Figures, and an Appended Boilerplate Section (§11):**
   - **Section 11 (p. 12)** is an unintegrated boilerplate dump ("AntiVibe Senior Architectural & Pedagogical Audit (P11)") that refers to the paper by a different title ("Single-Stack Survival Audit") and claims mechanisms never used in the paper (e.g., $\tilde{A}_i = A_i / \sqrt{L_i}$, dynamic group scaling $G \leftarrow \min(2G, 64)$, and adaptive learning-rate boosts when $\|A\|_2 < 10^{-5}$).
   - **Figure 9 (p. 10)** mislabels AERO as "AERO controlled comparison: asymmetric clip" (asymmetric clip is DAPO's mechanism; AERO uses adaptive rollout allocation and selective rejection per p. 3).
   - **Page 10 (Figures 3–10)** consists of 8 uninformative 3-line text-box placeholders not referenced in the main text.
   - **Raw LaTeX `&` delimiters** are printed verbatim in the Verdict table (p. 5), Table 1 (p. 7), and Table 2 (p. 8).
   - **Internal repository scaffolding** (§8 "Absorbed bounded panels (from the demoted workshop artifact)" on pp. 8–9, single-author breach of double-blind anonymity on p. 1, and local file paths) makes the text read like an internal project log rather than a self-contained scientific paper.

## Anchored Comments

- `[CRITICAL] p.12: "11 AntiVibe Senior Architectural & Pedagogical Audit (P11) ... Single-Stack Survival Audit."` - Section 11 is an automated template or internal rubric appended to the PDF after the References. It introduces fabricated algorithmic claims for this study (e.g., $\tilde{A}_i = A_i/\sqrt{L_i}$, $G \leftarrow \min(2G, 64)$, automatic LR boost for $\|A\|_2 < 10^{-5}$) that contradict the shared-stack protocol in §2–§3. **Fix:** Delete Section 11 entirely.
- `[CRITICAL] p.7: "(ii) that any arm is equivalent to GRPO—the smallest achievable MDE80 is 0.01012 against a 0.01 margin, so disappears was unreachable a priori at n=8"` - A preregistered equivalence audit where the sample size ($S=8$) and evaluation set size ($500$ prompts, binomial quantization step $0.002$) make the equivalence verdict mathematically nearly impossible to reach is a major experimental design flaw. **Fix:** Either conduct a prospective power analysis to size $S$ (e.g., $S \ge 16$ seeds) and the held-out set (e.g., full 1,319 GSM8K test set) so $MDE_{80} \le m$, or widen the preregistered equivalence margin $m$ to a realistic value (e.g., $m = 0.02$, which is currently relegated to an unreported secondary sensitivity analysis on p. 5).
- `[CRITICAL] p.9: "GRPO seed 11 moved from 0.646 to 0.650 because two of 500 rows changed from an unparsed completion to a correct completion."` - A held-out evaluation pipeline must be deterministic given a fixed checkpoint, or its variance must be incorporated into the error bars. If evaluator replay alone shifts a seed score by $0.004$ (and changes the 8-seed paired delta by $0.0005$), the evaluation harness (greedy vs. stochastic decoding, vLLM batch invariance, or parser regex timeout) is not locked. **Fix:** Enforce deterministic greedy decoding (or fixed-seed batch-invariant sampling) during the 500-row held-out evaluation, verify zero replay drift across all 40 checkpoints, and report the sensitivity of Table 1 to using $0.646$ vs. $0.650$ for GRPO seed 11.
- `[MAJOR] p.3: "The full audit is locked to Qwen3-8B, GSM8K, 30 training steps, batch size 4, 1,024-token completions"` - Thirty optimizer steps at batch size 4 (120 training prompts total) is far too short to evaluate post-training RL variants whose mechanisms target mid-to-late training pathologies (e.g., length drift in Dr.GRPO, clipping saturation in DAPO, importance-ratio variance in GSPO). **Fix:** Justify why 30 steps is sufficient to trigger these mechanisms, or scale the primary audit horizon to at least 150–300 steps (matching the 160-step regime explored in §8) and report step-wise trajectories for completion length, KL divergence, clip fraction, and $Z_{\text{vf}}$.
- `[MAJOR] p.4: "class VariantHook: ... All hooks are loaded into the same trainer binary, so infrastructure differences are impossible."` - The 4-method `VariantHook` interface shown cannot implement DAPO's dynamic rejection sampling loop (which resamples prompts within the rollout engine before reward/advantage computation), GSPO's sequence-level importance ratio (which requires sequence log-probabilities prior to `loss_terms`), or AERO's rescue rollouts. **Fix:** Replace the simplified pseudocode with the exact hook signature and rollout/trainer control-flow hooks actually used by DAPO, GSPO, Dr.GRPO, and AERO.
- `[MAJOR] p.6: "The locked output schema contains one row per arm and seed plus two generated tables: ... and (ii) last-10 reward, late Zvf/Gu, collapse rate, realized rollout count, group schedule, and wall-clock time."` - The text promises two generated tables in §6, but only Table 1 (accuracy deltas) is included in the manuscript; the secondary telemetry table (training reward, late $Z_{\text{vf}}/G_u$, collapse rate, realized rollouts, and wall-clock time for all 5 arms) is missing. Only DAPO and GRPO rollout/wall-clock numbers are mentioned in prose. **Fix:** Add the full secondary telemetry table for all five arms (GRPO, DAPO, GSPO, Dr.GRPO, AERO) with mean and SD across the 8 seeds.
- `[MAJOR] p.6: "The GRPO held-out exact matches are respectively 0.650, 0.636, 0.634, 0.634, 0.634, 0.620, 0.634, and 0.622; DAPO seeds ..."` - Readers can verify the parametric mean deltas from these raw seed numbers ($\Delta_{\text{DAPO}} = +0.00100$, $\Delta_{\text{GSPO}} = +0.00500$, $\Delta_{\text{Dr.GRPO}} = -0.00200$, $\Delta_{\text{AERO}} = -0.00075$), and the exact noncentral-$t$ $MDE_{80}$ values ($t_{0.975,7} + t_{0.80,7} \approx 2.3646 + 0.8960$; noncentral $MDE_{80}$ matches $0.01012, 0.01185, 0.01483, 0.01319$ to the last digit), which is commendable. However, the secondary sensitivity analyses at equivalence margins $m = 0.005$ and $m = 0.02$ promised on p. 5 ("Sensitivity analyses at 0.005 and 0.02 are secondary") and the 90% CIs required by the `Disappears` rule on p. 5 are never reported in §6 or Table 1. Notice that at $m = 0.02$, all four arms have $MDE_{80} < 0.02$ and 90% CIs inside $[-0.02, +0.02]$, which would make all four variants `DISAPPEARS` at the 2% margin! **Fix:** Add the 90% CIs and the explicit verdicts under the preregistered $m = 0.005$ and $m = 0.02$ sensitivity margins to Table 1. *[Superseded-verdict note (2026-10-18): a DAPO `DISAPPEARS` verdict at the primary 0.01 margin is superseded (`INCONCLUSIVE`); the m=0.02 secondary-margin reading is now reported in P11 as sensitivity only; see zvf-program/audit/STATISTICAL_REANALYSIS.md.]*
- `[MAJOR] p.10: "Figure 9: AERO controlled comparison: asymmetric clip. Seed-paired single-stack evaluation; verdict INCONCLUSIVE."` - Figure 9 attributes "asymmetric clip" to AERO, contradicting §2 (p. 3), where DAPO is defined as "asymmetric clip-higher + dynamic sampling" and AERO is defined as "adaptive rollout allocation, selective rejection, and posterior-guided avoidance of zero-advantage groups." Furthermore, Figures 3–10 on p. 10 are unreferenced in the text and contain only generic 4-box flowcharts. **Fix:** Remove the placeholder figures on p. 10 (or replace them with actual data plots, such as paired seed slopegraphs and step-wise $Z_{\text{vf}}$/rollout curves) and fix the AERO description.
- `[MINOR] p.5, p.7, p.8: "Verdict & Rule", "Arm & \Delta & 95% CI low...", "Arm & Held-out score & Reported Zvf & Rollouts"` - Unescaped or miscompiled LaTeX table column alignment characters (`&`) appear as literal ampersands throughout the rendered tables on pages 5, 7, and 8. **Fix:** Fix the LaTeX tabular formatting so column separators do not render as literal `&` symbols.
- `[MINOR] p.5: "The 90% CI lies inside a preregistered equivalence margin and the exact 80%-power MDE does not exceed that margin."` - In §4 (p. 5), the `MDE80` is computed at two-sided $\alpha = 0.05$ ($t_{0.975, 7}$), whereas the equivalence test (`Disappears`) uses the 90% CI (which corresponds to two one-sided tests, TOST, at $\alpha = 0.05$, i.e., $t_{0.95, 7}$). Mixing a two-sided $\alpha=0.05$ MDE with a 90% CI TOST equivalence rule is slightly conservative/mismatched. **Fix:** Clarify whether `MDE80` is intended for two-sided superiority ($\alpha = 0.05$) or TOST equivalence ($\alpha = 0.05$ one-sided), and state the exact formula used in `aggregate_audit.py`.
- `[MINOR] p.6: "platform_hybrid/registry/query.pystackdiff [2]"` - Missing space between `query.py` and `stackdiff`. Also, no concrete schema or example output of `grpo-stackdiff` is shown in §5. **Fix:** Fix the typo (`query.py stackdiff`) and provide a compact table or listing of the exact manifest keys checked by `grpo-stackdiff`.
- `[MINOR] p.7: "The configurations lists seeds {0, 1}, but the checked-in artifact stores one arm-level aggregate per arm and no per-seed records."` - In §7 (p. 8), the pilot is described first as recording "stored held-out score (within-arm end-of-training accuracy on the synthetic set—not a delta versus GRPO)" under "Pilot results", and then in the next paragraph ("Interpretation") as "reaches the same descriptive delta as Dr.GRPO". **Fix:** Keep terminology consistent in §7: refer to the Table 2 values strictly as raw held-out accuracies ($0.500, 0.575, 0.550, 0.575$), not deltas.
- `[MINOR] p.8: "8 Absorbed bounded panels (from the demoted workshop artifact) ... What is not absorbed. Mislabelled synthetic-as-GSM8K rows..."` - Section 8 reads like internal repository changelog notes about a "demoted workshop PDF" rather than a self-contained experimental section. It also omits the batch/prompt setup details needed to interpret why $G=2 \times 160$ steps reaches $0.9625$ reward while $G=16 \times 20$ steps reaches $0.3891$ reward. **Fix:** Rewrite §8 as a standalone ablation on fixed-rollout-budget allocation ($G=2$ for 160 optimizer steps vs. $G=16$ for 20 optimizer steps), explain the role of optimizer step count ($160$ vs. $20$ gradient updates), and move repository deprecation notes to an appendix or README.
- `[SUGGESTION] p.1: "Arvind C R* PES University arvindcr4@gmail.com August 2, 2026"` - Top ML venues (NeurIPS/ICLR) enforce double-blind peer review, and the manuscript date ("August 2, 2026") and execution logs ("2026-07-11", "2026-07-20") are future-dated or synthetic timestamps. **Fix:** Anonymize author names, emails, W&B run IDs/links, and private Hub commit references for double-blind submission, and verify all dates in the manuscript.

## Questions for the Authors

1. **Equivalence under the preregistered $m = 0.02$ sensitivity margin:** On p. 5, you state that sensitivity analyses at $m = 0.005$ and $m = 0.02$ are secondary. Given the raw seed accuracies in §6 (p. 6), all four variants have $MDE_{80} \in [0.01012, 0.01483] < 0.02$ and 90% CIs well within $[-0.02, +0.02]$. Why were the $m = 0.02$ verdicts omitted from §6, and do you conclude that all four variants are equivalent to baseline GRPO within a $\pm 2\%$ GSM8K accuracy margin at 30 steps?
2. **Evaluator non-determinism on GRPO seed 11:** What caused two of the 500 held-out completions on GRPO seed 11 to change from unparsed to correct during replay (§9, p. 9)? Was held-out evaluation run with temperature $> 0$, or did vLLM prefix caching / tensor-parallel reduction order change the generated tokens?
3. **Training horizon vs. variant mechanics:** In 30 steps on GSM8K, what were the observed trajectory statistics for response length (which Dr.GRPO targets) and clip fraction (which DAPO targets)? Did length bias or clipping saturation actually occur during these 30 steps, or was the horizon too short for the variants' target pathologies to manifest?
4. **AERO and GSPO secondary telemetry:** What were the mean late-training $Z_{\text{vf}}$, realized rollout counts, and wall-clock times across all 8 seeds for GSPO, Dr.GRPO, and AERO (the missing second table promised in §6, p. 6)?
