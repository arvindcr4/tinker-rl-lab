## Summary

This manuscript introduces `GRPO-Registry`, a machine-readable catalog for Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) training stacks and variant deltas. The authors motivate the work with audits from the broader `TINKERRL-BENCH` program. Specifically, two runs labeled "GRPO, Qwen3-8B, GSM8K" produced a 17x performance gap (85.6% vs. 5.0% final reward) due to an unreported base-versus-instruct checkpoint swap and other stack confounds. Similarly, a closed-stack "dapo" run implemented an advantage-clip surrogate without dynamic sampling.

To make these hidden implementation differences queryable, the authors provide:
1. A JSON Schema (`registry/schema.json`) defining `stack` records (using the seven-field run-manifest block of the `MIN-REPORT-RL` standard) and `variant_delta` records (tracking per-component status as `implemented`, `surrogate`, `absent`, or `unknown`).
2. Seed stack records and variant-delta records populated from benchmark config dumps, an open Colab trainer audit, a managed-runtime head-to-head, a same-stack four-method run (`N2`), and a five-seed risk-index batch (`zvf-iter130`).
3. A dependency-free Python CLI (`registry/query.py`) that computes a 0–100 `MIN-REPORT` auditor badge and a `stackdiff` severity verdict on an `R0`–`R5` flip-risk ladder.
4. Extensive audit sections (Sections 9–11) testing same-stack reward and length deltas, measured-versus-claimed variant reconciliation, schema stress testing, and field-level coverage.

While the core concept of a machine-readable stack manifest and `stackdiff` risk ladder is timely and practical, the manuscript itself is in an unedited, unpublishable state. It reads like an automated concatenation of boilerplate text from a parent benchmark paper (Sections 2–3) and raw chronological commit logs (`iter-26` through `iter-126` in Sections 10–11), complete with internal contradictions, retracted statistical metrics still presented as headline findings, and missing sections.

---

## Strengths

- **Clear motivation from concrete failure modes:** The paper grounds its schema design in real post-training failure modes, such as silent base-versus-instruct checkpoint swaps (p. 1, p. 8) and nominal "DAPO" runs on closed runtimes that omit dynamic sampling and use advantage clipping instead of asymmetric probability-ratio clipping (p. 9, Listing 1; p. 13, Listing 3).
- **Explicit epistemic distinction between null and absent:** The schema enforces a strict convention where JSON `null` denotes an unreported configuration leaf, whereas explicit `false`, `0.0`, or `"none"` denotes a reported absence (p. 8, Section 5). This allows the `MIN-REPORT` auditor badge to score reporting transparency rather than hyperparameter choices (p. 11, Section 7.2).
- **Actionable pre-run confound detection via `stackdiff`:** The `R0`–`R5` severity ladder separates outcome-critical differences (`R5`, such as checkpoint identity or reward parser) from algorithmic differences (`R4`) and hyperparameters (`R3`), while isolating unknown fields into a separate coverage score and `UNDERSPECIFIED` flag (p. 11–12, Section 7.3).
- **Candor regarding project-internal reporting flaws:** The authors openly document where their own released configuration dumps were written post hoc and failed to match executed scripts (p. 9, Section 6), where a runner's loss flag was unwired so "Dr.GRPO" actually trained vanilla GRPO (p. 36, Section 11.15), and where their "anti-herding" metric ($\delta_{\text{div}}$) turned out to be a Jensen's inequality artifact requiring retraction (p. 28, p. 39).

## Weaknesses

- **Severe structural incoherence and raw iteration-log dumping:** Starting at Section 10.6 (p. 16) and continuing through Section 11.20 (p. 50), the manuscript degenerates into a chronological sequence of internal agent/engineering logs (`iter-26`, `iter-30`, `iter-32`, `iter-34`, `iter-38`, `iter-42`, `iter-50`, `iter-54`, `iter-58`, `iter-62`, `iter-66`, `iter-70`, `iter-74`, `iter-78`, `iter-82`, `iter-86`, `iter-90`, `iter-94`, `iter-100`, `iter-106`, `iter-118`, `iter-122`, `iter-126`). These subsections repeatedly redefine the size of the registry, contradict earlier tables, reference internal task prompts ("The brief's vein (b) calls for...", p. 37, p. 42, p. 45, p. 47), and use internal shorthand (`P5`, `P6`, `P7`, `P8`, `iter-53 P5 #64`, `row 71`, `JOB B`).
- **Irreconcilable entry counts and CLI specifications across sections:** Depending on the page, the registry contains:
  - 28 stack records and 20 variant-delta records (p. 7, Section 4; p. 14, Section 9.1),
  - 3 variant-delta records shipped (p. 8, Section 5.2),
  - 20 stack records and 11 variant-delta records = 31 entries (p. 9, p. 14, p. 18, p. 20, p. 24),
  - 20 stack records and 14 variant-delta records = 34 entries (p. 25, p. 27, p. 31),
  - 20 stack records and 15 variant-delta records = 35 entries (p. 38, p. 42, p. 44),
  - 24 stack records and 15 variant-delta records = 39 entries (p. 47, p. 48).
  Similarly, the CLI `registry/query.py` is introduced as having "four verbs" (p. 11, Section 7.1), then gains a "fifth subcommand `implementations`" (p. 19), a sixth `drift` (p. 19), seventh and eighth `validate` and `health` (p. 23), ninth `measured-coverage` (p. 34), tenth `coverage` (p. 28), eleventh `antiherding` (p. 29), and twelfth `validate-strict` (p. 48).
- **Unintegrated boilerplate from a companion benchmark paper:** Sections 2 and 3 (p. 3–7) are pasted directly from the general `TINKERRL-BENCH` benchmark paper. They describe classic RL libraries (Stable Baselines3, CleanRL, Tianshou, PufferLib, rl_games, d3rlpy), DPO, SFT, 32+ models up to 671B parameters (DeepSeek-V3.1, Nemotron-120B, Kimi-K2), and a "Legacy 20-test BH table" (Table 5, p. 7) that the caption itself admits includes mathematically undefined $n_1=n_2=1$ Welch $t$-tests. None of these classic RL libraries or frontier 120B–671B models appear in the 28 seed stack entries of `GRPO-Registry` (Table 6, p. 10).
- **Retracted mathematical artifacts left in the main text as active results:** Subsections 11.11 (p. 28), 11.14 (p. 34), and 11.18 (p. 39) begin with a "Retraction notice (2026-10-16)" stating that the anti-herding residual $\delta_{\text{div}} = \text{ZVF}_{\text{iid}} - \text{ZVF}_{\text{obs}}$ is a mathematical estimator artifact (non-negative under plug-in $\hat{p}=k/G$, non-positive in expectation under pooled $\bar{p}$ by Jensen's inequality) and that results ranking or triggering on $\delta_{\text{div}}$ carry no evidential weight. Yet the manuscript leaves all the retracted claims, tables (Tables 19, 22, 25, 26), schema extensions (`outcomes.zvf_antiherding`, `controller_predicted_savings_per_rollout`), and CLI commands (`query.py antiherding`) intact right below the retraction notices.
- **Core flagship variants lack same-stack empirical measurements:** The abstract and introduction center on DAPO, Dr. GRPO, and GSPO as the three verified variant-delta records (p. 1, p. 2, p. 8). However, Section 10.3 (p. 15, Finding B) and Section 11.15 (p. 36, Table 24) reveal that DAPO and GSPO have zero same-stack (`N2`) or multi-seed risk-index (`zvf130`) measurements in the registry (`NO_DATA`), and their Tinker stack records have `outcomes=null`.
- **Single-seed ($n=1$) inference for same-stack variant comparisons:** In Section 3.3 (p. 6), the authors state that single-seed runs ($n=1$) have zero statistical power and cannot support inferential tests or confidence intervals. Yet Section 9 (p. 13–14, Table 7) and subsequent iterations compute paired bootstrap confidence intervals and claim statistically significant reward and length differences across the `N2` four-method run (`grpo`, `aero`, `gift`, `areal`), which was executed with only a single seed (`seed 0`, 40 steps, 16 prompts per step) by resampling across training steps (`last-10` or `full40`). Resampling correlated training steps within a single seed does not measure cross-seed training variance.

## Questions for the Authors

1. **Can DAPO, Dr. GRPO, and GSPO be evaluated on the same-stack (`N2`) and multi-seed (`zvf130`) panels?** Because DAPO, Dr. GRPO, and GSPO are the three primary variants used to motivate the paper (Abstract, Section 5.2), why were the controlled same-stack experiments (`N2`) and 5-seed risk-index runs (`zvf130`) executed on less common variants (`aero`, `areal`, `gift`, `cppo`, `mcgrpo`, `ngrpo`, `scafgrpo`, `es`) while leaving DAPO and GSPO with zero same-stack outcome measurements (`outcomes=null`, Table 24)?
2. **What is the true multi-seed variance of the `N2` same-stack comparisons?** Given your power analysis in Section 3.3 showing that $n=5$ seeds per arm is required to detect even very large effects ($d_{\min}=2.024$) and $n=1$ has zero inferential power, do the $\le 2\text{pp}$ reward differences and $\sim 31$–$53$ token length shifts for `aero` and `areal` hold up across multiple independent training seeds, or are they artifacts of resampling steps within `seed 0`?
3. **What is the authoritative structure and entry count of the released `GRPO-Registry`?** Which directory (`registry/` vs. `platform_hybrid/registry/`) and which entry count (28 stacks + 20 deltas, 20 stacks + 14 deltas, 20 stacks + 15 deltas, or 24 stacks + 15 deltas) represents the actual artifact being released?

## Citations to Verify

Because the manuscript is truncated at page 50 before the Bibliography / References section, none of the numbered citations `[1]` through `[29]` have corresponding bibliography entries in the text. Based on in-text mentions and arXiv identifiers cited in the prose, the following references require verification or full bibliographic inclusion:

- **Missing bibliography for all numbered citations `[1]`–`[29]`:** Every numbered citation in Sections 1–5 (e.g., GRPO `[24, 5]`, DAPO `[27]`, Dr. GRPO `[18]`, GSPO `[29]`, variant tail `[17, 22, 28, 13, 25, 12, 20, 3, 14]`, related work `[21, 8, 15, 7, 2, 23, 26]`, statistical methodology `[1, 4, 11, 9, 6]`) lacks a reference list entry in the provided manuscript.
- **Unverified / internal variant acronyms (`AERO`, `GIFT`, `AREAL`, `CPPO`, `MCGRPO`, `NGRPO`, `SCAFGRPO`, `LitePPO`):** These variants drive almost all empirical tables in Sections 6 and 9–11, yet none of them are cited with author names, titles, or arXiv IDs in the text. On p. 31 (Section 11.12), the authors explicitly note for `delta_liteppo` that *"Citation is a transparent placeholder (arxiv = '') because no peer-reviewed LitePPO paper is verified"*, and on p. 43 (Section 11.18.7) that `Adaptive-G` *"is a repository implementation... not a peer-reviewed paper"*. The authors must verify which of `AERO`, `GIFT`, `AREAL`, `MCGRPO`, `NGRPO`, and `SCAFGRPO` correspond to real published papers versus internal repository inventions.
- **Companion `TINKERRL-BENCH` "Pillar" papers (`Pillar 1`–`Pillar 5` / `P1`–`P8`):** Repeatedly cited in the text without formal bibliographic entries or anonymized supplement links (e.g., p. 1, p. 2, p. 4, p. 5, p. 16).
