## Summary

This paper presents **GRPO-REGISTRY**, a machine-readable catalog and CLI toolkit for auditing Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) training stacks and algorithm variants. The authors motivate this resource with audit findings from their broader *TINKERRL-BENCH* program. Specifically, two runs labeled "GRPO, Qwen3-8B, GSM8K" differed by 17x in final reward (85.6% vs. 5.0%) due to an unreported base-versus-instruct checkpoint swap and other unmatched stack parameters. Similarly, a closed-runtime "DAPO" arm omitted dynamic sampling and used an advantage clip instead of a ratio clip. 

To make these discrepancies queryable before spending GPU compute, the paper introduces four components:
1. A JSON Schema (`schema.json`) with two record types: `stack` (capturing the 7-field run-manifest block of the 8-item `MIN-REPORT-RL` standard) and `variant_delta` (decomposing variants into component changes with implementation statuses: `implemented`, `surrogate`, `absent`, or `unknown`).
2. Seed records derived from the authors' benchmark artifacts (28 stack records and either 3, 11, or 20 variant-delta records, depending on the section).
3. A dependency-free CLI (`query.py`) that provides a 0–100 `MIN-REPORT` reporting-coverage badge and a `stackdiff` command. The `stackdiff` tool evaluates 48 canonical fields to assign a flip-risk severity level from `R0` (no known difference) to `R5` (outcome-critical difference), alongside a field-coverage score and an `UNDERSPECIFIED` flag.
4. Appendices (Sections D–F) that reconcile qualitative variant claims against same-stack (`N2`) and 5-seed (`zvf130`) empirical measurements.

While the problem of under-specified RL post-training stacks is important and the schema/diff design is pragmatic, the manuscript is not ready for publication. It contains live placeholder sections, unremoved internal project notes, contradictory record counts and badge scores across tables and listings, and severe anonymity violations.

---

## Strengths

- **Clear problem motivation:** The paper targets a real reproducibility failure mode in LLM reinforcement learning. Framework defaults and closed launchers silently alter loss aggregation, clipping, KL penalties, and base checkpoints under identical algorithm labels.
- **Explicit null-versus-absent semantics:** Distinguishing JSON `null` (unreported) from explicit `false` / `0.0` / `"none"` (reported-as-absent) is a sound design decision. It allows the auditor badge to measure reporting transparency rather than configuration choices.
- **Separation of known differences from missing data in `stackdiff`:** The `R0–R5` severity ladder computes flip risk strictly from fields known on both sides, while surfacing missing data via a separate coverage score, hidden severity bound, and `UNDERSPECIFIED` flag. This avoids conflating known stack divergence with unreported metadata.
- **Component-level variant tracking:** The `variant_deltas_applied` join structure cleanly exposes partial or surrogate implementations on managed runtimes (e.g., showing that a nominal "DAPO" run only applies an asymmetric clip surrogate without dynamic sampling).
- **Low-friction governance design:** Using flat JSON files in git with a standard-library-only Python CLI avoids single-point-of-failure web infrastructure, directly addressing the archival lessons from the discontinuation of Papers with Code.

## Weaknesses

- **Unfinished manuscript with placeholder sections and internal build artifacts:** Section 5.2 is an explicit placeholder (`[Placeholder — results pending in experiments/results/publication_fixes/.]`). Furthermore, pages 1, 2, 4, 13, and 15–19 leak internal repository notes, iteration logs (`iter-14`, `iter-60`, `iter-178`, `iter-190`, `Pillar 2 (P6)`, `Pillar-4 (P8, fraud scorer)`), and author identities on page 1.
- **Internal contradictions in record counts:** The manuscript cannot agree on how many records exist in the shipped registry:
  - Variant-delta records are stated as **3** verified records (Abstract, p. 1; Sec. 1, p. 2; Sec. 2.2, p. 3), **20** records (Sec. 1.1, p. 3; App. A.1, p. 11; App. D.1, p. 14), **11** records (App. D.1 historical audit, p. 14; App. E.1, p. 15; Table 4, p. 16), and **14** entries declaring claims (App. F.1, p. 17).
  - Total registry entries are stated as **31** (`31/31 schema PASS`, Sec. 2.3, p. 4; `20 stack records and 11 variant_delta records`, App. D.1, p. 14) versus **48** (`28 stack records and 20 variant-delta records`, p. 3, p. 11, p. 14).
  - Section 2.3 (p. 4) claims `ci_method` is populated on **7** entries derived from the N2 run and left null on **24** entries (summing to 31), whereas Table 1 (p. 5) states Family D (the N2 run) has only **3** entries (or 4 including base GRPO) out of **28** stack entries.
- **Contradictory badge scores and coverage numbers between text, tables, and CLI listings:** 
  - Listing 7 (p. 12) claims to be the verbatim output of `python3 registry/query.py badge` and "the source of the badge column in Table 2." Yet its scores directly contradict Table 1 (p. 5) and Table 2 (p. 13): `colab-open_*` is **96** in Listing 7 (which follows Eq. 1: $\lfloor 100 \times 6.75 / 7 \rceil = 96$) but **93** in Tables 1 and 2; `tinker_dapo/drgrpo_qwen3.5-4b` is **62** in Listing 7 but **86** in Table 2; `tinker_grpo_qwen3.5-4b` is **57** in Listing 7 but **86** in Table 2; `trl_grpo_qwen3-8b_gsm8k` is **74** in Listing 7 but **58** in Table 2.
  - Listing 4 (p. 8) states "Seven of twelve stacks fully report KL handling" across 12 listed entries, ignoring the other 16 stack entries in the 28-entry registry.
- **Contradictory empirical numbers between Appendices E and F:** Appendix E and Appendix F analyze the same `zvf130` 5-seed risk index panel, but report incompatible numbers for every variant:
  - In Table 4 (p. 16), `zvf130` $\Delta$ values relative to `grpo` (mean 0.481) are: `aero` $-0.261$, `gift` $-0.367$, `areal` $-0.360$, `cppo` $-0.186$, `ngrpo` $-0.180$, `mcgrpo` $-0.335$, `es` $-0.367$, `scafgrpo` $-0.164$.
  - In Table 5 (p. 18), `zvf130` $\hat{\Delta}$ values on `zvf_risk` are: `aero` $-0.148$, `gift` $-0.263$, `areal` $-0.246$, `cppo` $-0.151$, `es` $-0.273$, `mcgrpo` $-0.174$, `ngrpo` $-0.131$, `scafgrpo` $-0.352$.
  - Yet Section F.5 (p. 19) asserts that "On the 13 measurable pairs where both the registry stores a value and we recomputed from raw, the largest drift is $\epsilon = 10^{-4}$." A difference of $-0.164$ vs. $-0.352$ (`scafgrpo`) or $-0.261$ vs. $-0.148$ (`aero`) is >0.11, not $10^{-4}$—unless `zvf` and `zvf_risk` are different metrics, which is never explained and directly contradicts Section F.1's framing that Section F recomputes Section E's numbers.
  - Similarly, on the `N2` panel, Section E.3 Finding C (p. 16) states "Measured N2 reports $\Delta\text{ZVF} = +0.025$" for `aero` (yielding an `OPPOSE` verdict), whereas Table 5 and Table 6 (p. 18) report $\hat{\Delta} = -0.0250$ for `aero` `zvf` on `n2_last10` (yielding `Supports-NS`). The sign flipped between Appendix E and Appendix F without explanation, while Section F.5 claims zero drift.
- **Unverifiable dependence on unattached "companion pillar papers":** The paper repeatedly defers core definitions, experimental setups, and proofs to "Pillar 1–5" companion papers (`P1 scaling`, `P2 ZVF`, `P3 group size`, `P4 length bias`, `Pillar 5 MIN-REPORT-RL`, `P7`, `P8`), none of which appear in the References section. A standalone conference paper must be self-contained.
- **Thin and insular seed catalog:** All 28 stack entries come from the authors' own benchmark runs. Of these 28 entries, 2 are dry-run placeholders with no outcomes (`verl` and `openrlhf`), 4 are toy-scale synthetic addition runs (`a+b` in `[11,60]` on `Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct`), and 16 are hand-entered records missing run scripts, yielding a median pair coverage of just 0.19 (342/378 pairs flagged `UNDERSPECIFIED`).
