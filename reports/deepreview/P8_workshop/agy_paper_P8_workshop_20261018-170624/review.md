## Summary

This manuscript presents "RL-Finetuning Bench," framed as an exploratory workshop artifact note accompanying a release of step-level reward traces, Zero-Variance Fraction (ZVF) traces, parser scripts, and a structured evidence-grade manifest covering 79 GRPO-style post-training runs across multiple RL libraries, backends (including the closed Tinker API), and open model families (0.6B–671B total parameters). The core substantive takeaways are: (1) a mechanical definition showing that when all completions in a prompt group yield identical rewards ($ZVF = 1$), the group-relative advantage and gradient vanish; (2) a 5-seed negative control on Qwen3-8B / GSM8K showing that GRPO yields a statistically insignificant held-out accuracy gain over the base model (83.3% vs. 82.0%, $t=1.32, p=0.26$); (3) a demonstration that a strong cross-stack correlation between mean ZVF and final reward ($r = -0.769, N=15$) is an artifact of task confounding (collapsing or flipping sign to $r = +0.40, p=0.09$ within GSM8K); and (4) an exploratory reanalysis of group size $G$ under fixed-step versus fixed-token budgets.

While the manuscript's radical candor about negative results, task confounding, failed-run compute costs, and closed-backend limitations is refreshing and aligned with workshop goals, the paper suffers from severe production, structural, and editorial defects. The text reads as an unedited concatenation of multiple parallel drafts ("workshop variant," "ZVF variant," "D&B / DNB variant") stitched together with raw LLM-generated verification boilerplate ("AntiVibe Audit," "P7 iter-134 row 152"), placeholder diagram text, duplicated figures, broken table references, and mathematical inconsistencies. In its current state, it is not ready for publication even at a workshop venue without a thorough cleanup.

---

## Strengths

- **Exemplary scientific candor and negative-result reporting:** The authors explicitly separate definitional truths, multi-seed statistical tests, confounded correlations, and single-seed case studies into an explicit evidence-grade hierarchy (Tables 2, 3, and 6). Highlighting that the pooled $r = -0.769$ ZVF correlation is purely a task confound—and that the 5-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K held-out improvement (+1.3 pp, $p=0.26$) is null—is a genuinely useful service to practitioners.
- **Transparent resource, failure, and closed-API accounting:** Section 7 provides an unusually thorough breakdown of out-of-pocket financial costs (including ~$30–35 in failed runs), hardware assumptions for carbon footprint estimation, and an explicit audit of what the closed-source Tinker API hides (server-side loss, baseline normalization, minibatching).
- **Clear mathematical intuition on ZVF vs. mean reward:** Appendix B.2 cleanly refutes the concern that ZVF is a tautological re-expression of batch mean reward for binary outcomes by showing via $E[ZVF_t] = E_{p_g}[p_g^K + (1-p_g)^K]$ that ZVF depends on the higher moments of the prompt-difficulty distribution $\{p_g\}$ rather than just the mean $\bar{r}$.

## Weaknesses

- **Severe editing artifacts and cross-document stitch seams:** The manuscript repeatedly leaks internal automated-pipeline tags and references to sibling submissions. Examples include references to "the ZVF and D&B variants of this work" (p.2), "Section 3 of the ZVF variant" (p.18), "Table 4 of the DNB variant" (p.18), "P7 iter-134 row 152" in Reference [22] (p.12), and the entire Appendix D ("AntiVibe Senior Architectural & Pedagogical Audit (P8)", pp.15–17).
- **Contradictions between main text and appendices:** Page 2 (lines 67–69) states that bootstrap CIs and permutation $p$-values for the within-GSM8K rows are omitted and out of scope for the workshop note, yet page 15 ("Stratified Diagnostics", lines 572–585) and page 19 (Tables 9 and 10) present those exact 10,000-resample bootstrap CIs, permutation tests, and `pingouin.partial_corr` tables. Similarly, Appendix D claims that the work introduces "closed-loop control dynamics," "dynamic group scaling $G \leftarrow \min(2G, 64)$," and "adaptive reward scaling sentinel with automatic learning-rate boost" (p.17), none of which appear anywhere in the actual experiments or main text.
- **Placeholder figures and exact duplicates:** Figures 2 through 11 (pp.15–16) do not appear to be real figures; they render in the manuscript as 4-line text-box flowcharts (e.g., `Control Task Cohort -> Held-Out Control Task Evaluation Pipeline -> Evaluation Harness -> Held-Out Pass@k Score`) that are never cited in the prose. Worse, Figures 10 and 11 are verbatim duplicates of Figures 8 and 9 on the very same page.
- **Mathematical and notation inconsistencies in Appendices B and C:**
  - The main text (p.2, line 53) denotes group size (completions per prompt) as $G$ and prompt batch as $P_t$. Appendix B.1 (p.12, line 463) switches without warning to $B_t$ for the batch of prompt-groups and $K$ for rollouts per group.
  - Appendix C.2 (p.14, Eq. 3) defines $GE_{\text{tok}}(G, B)$ with $G \cdot K \cdot \bar{L}_{\text{rollout}}$ in the denominator, where $G$ is group size and $K$ is also defined as "the rollouts per group" (line 526), while $B$ on the left-hand side does not appear on the right-hand side. Then Table 7 (p.19) states "$K=1$ rollout per sample", conflating prompt batch size, group size, and rollouts per sample.
- **Mismatch between claimed scope and actual manifest:** The abstract and Section 2 claim "79 GRPO-style runs across 7 RL libraries (TRL, Stable Baselines3, CleanRL, Tianshou, PufferLib, rl_games, d3rlpy)". However, the Run Manifest (Table 6, p.18) sums to 64 runs (`N` column: $22$ in Tier A + $29$ in Tier B + $13$ in Tier C = $64$) and contains **zero** rows for PufferLib, rl_games, or d3rlpy. Furthermore, the CleanRL, SB3, and Tianshou rows in Table 6 are `ppo` on a synthetic `math` (`gen`) task for ~100,000 steps (described in Figure 5 as "Arithmetic MDP Task Encoding vs LLM Generation"), not GRPO post-training of LLMs.
- **Missing and misnumbered tables:** Appendix C.1 (p.13, line 514) and C.4 (p.14, line 545) cite "Table 6" as reporting "$G=4$ as the highest last-10 reward (52.1%) under a fixed-step budget." In this manuscript, Table 6 (p.18) is the Run Manifest and does not contain the number 52.1% or any reward values.

## Questions for the authors

1. **Where is the 5-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K run in the manifest and compute table?** You highlight the 5-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K held-out evaluation (83.3% vs. 82.0%, $t=1.32, p=0.26$) as one of only two primary A-grade inferential claims. Which row in Table 4 (compute spend) and Table 6 (run manifest) corresponds to these 5 seeds, what backend were they trained on, and why did the 1-seed Tinker Qwen3-8B GSM8K run in Table 2 achieve only 34.4% last-10 train reward while the held-out test accuracy reached 83.3%?
2. **What is the true status of the 7 RL libraries and 79 runs?** Why do PufferLib, rl_games, and d3rlpy not appear in Table 6, and what specific MDP task (`math` / `gen` for 100,000 steps) was used for CleanRL, Stable Baselines3, and Tianshou? How does a 100k-step small-MLP arithmetic MDP compare meaningfully against 30-step LLM LoRA fine-tuning?
3. **How were the numbers in Table 7 ("Token-budget-normalized G-sweep") generated?** The caption states that the held-out accuracy cells are "reconstructed point estimates from existing GRPO ablation logs (the `FALLBACK_ROWS` table in the script), not freshly measured per-seed runs," whereas Section 4 (line 80) calls it "A 3-seed token-budget reanalysis at $T \ge 16\text{M}$ tokens." Were any multi-seed Qwen3-8B runs actually executed across $T \in \{1\text{M}, 4\text{M}, 16\text{M}, 64\text{M}\}$ and $G \in \{4, 8, 16, 32\}$, or is Table 7 a synthetic/interpolated table?
