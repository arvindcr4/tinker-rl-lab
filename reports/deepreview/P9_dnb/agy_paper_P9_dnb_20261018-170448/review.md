## Summary

This manuscript introduces **RL-FINETUNING BENCH**, a benchmark artifact designed to audit reinforcement-learning (RL) post-training of language models across heterogeneous software stacks. The authors bundle 79 runs spanning 7 RL libraries (plus the closed-source Tinker API), 5 model families, and parameter scales from 0.6B to 671B across math reasoning (GSM8K, synthetic arithmetic), code generation (HumanEval), and tool-use tasks. The paper organizes its claims into three explicit evidence tiers: Tier A (analytic or multi-seed inferential results), Tier B (single-seed descriptive case studies, primarily on the proprietary Tinker platform), and Tier C (failed or partial runs logged for transparency). Scientifically, the authors formalize the **Zero-Variance Fraction (ZVF)**—the proportion of GRPO prompt groups with zero reward variance—showing via an analytic identity $\text{ZVF}(p, G) = p^G + (1-p)^G$ when binary rewards yield zero within-group gradients, demonstrating that a 5-seed held-out Qwen3-8B GSM8K run provides a non-significant $+1.3\text{pp}$ gain ($p=0.26$), and showing via stratified diagnostics that apparent pooled correlations between ZVF and performance ($r = -0.769$) are confounded by task identity and vanish within GSM8K ($r = +0.40, p = 0.09$).

---

## Strengths

- **Honest Evidence Tiering and Transparency:** The explicit separation of claims into Tier A (inferential), Tier B (descriptive single-seed), and Tier C (failed/partial runs in Table 1, Table 2, and Appendix B) is a refreshing departure from over-claimed empirical RL papers. Disclosing failed runs, JWT token expirations, and exact USD financial costs ($130–$140 out-of-pocket in Table 12) sets a high standard for candor.
- **Important Negative and Confounding Results:** The demonstration that pooled cross-task ZVF-vs-reward correlations ($r = -0.769$) collapse when stratified by task ($r = -0.026$ with task fixed effects in Table 7; $r = +0.401$ within GSM8K in Table 6) is a valuable methodological caution against pooled correlation fallacies in RL post-training diagnostics. Similarly, reporting the non-significant $+1.3\text{pp}$ held-out test delta on Qwen3-8B GSM8K ($p = 0.26$) despite strong training reward curves highlights train-test divergence in short-horizon GRPO.
- **Cross-Framework Standardization Effort:** The unified log parser (`scripts/zvf_compute_cross_framework.py`) and field mapping (Table 9) provide a practical schema for normalizing rollout logs across TRL, Tinker, OpenRLHF, and veRL.

---

## Weaknesses

- **Severe Editorial, Compilation, and Template Artifacts:** The manuscript reads like an unedited concatenation of automated revision patches, multi-paper splits ("vehicles"), and LLM-generated boilerplate. Figure 2's plots do not match its caption at all. Pages 9–12 contain 16 trivial, 3-node placeholder flowcharts (Figures 3–18), where Figures 11–18 are verbatim duplicates of Figures 3–10 with "&" replaced by "and" in the titles. Internal repository notes ("Absorbed satellites (retired)", "P7 iter-134 row 152", "AntiVibe Senior Architectural & Pedagogical Audit (P9)") are left directly in the text.
- **Overstated Cross-Stack and Scale Scope vs. Actual Open-Stack Execution:** While the abstract headlines "7 RL libraries, 5 model families, and total-parameter scales from 0.6B to 671B", Table 2 and Table 8 reveal that all frontier-scale runs (above 8B, except a 4-seed 30-step Llama-3.3-70B train-only Tinker run) are single-seed, 10-to-30-step runs on the closed-source Tinker API without held-out evaluations or raw rollout logs. Furthermore, several of the 7 claimed RL libraries (PufferLib, NVIDIA `rl_games`, `d3rlpy`) have **zero** runs in the run manifest (Table 2 and Table 8).
- **Internal Contradictions in Protocols, Metrics, and Claims:** There are pervasive contradictions across sections and tables regarding tasks, datasets, and hyperparameters (e.g., Qwen2.5-0.5B is listed as GSM8K in Table 2 and Table 12, but as synthetic generated arithmetic in Table 8; the 12-cell Wave-6 ablation is called a multi-seed Tier-A inferential arm in Table 1 and Table 2, despite having only 1 seed per cell). In Appendix G, the paper suddenly claims that "P9 introduces closed-loop control dynamics" and "monotonic ZVF reduction under adaptive control", directly contradicting the Abstract's statement that "We do not headline... an adaptive controller."
- **Fabricated or Hallucinated Reanalysis Data in Appendix E:** Table 10 presents a token-budget-normalized sweep across $T \in \{1\text{M}, 4\text{M}, 16\text{M}, 64\text{M}\}$ and $G \in \{4, 8, 16, 32, 64\}$, where the caption admits the values are "reconstructed point estimates from existing GRPO ablation logs (the `FALLBACK_ROWS` table in the script), not freshly measured per-seed runs" and that $G=64$ was never run. Presenting hardcoded fallback numbers or unvalidated extrapolations as an empirical table is scientifically unsound.

## Questions for the Authors

1. **Where is the 5-seed Qwen3-8B GSM8K control in the manifest?** Your second headline Tier-A claim is a 5-seed held-out Qwen3-8B GSM8K evaluation ($83.3\%$ vs. $82.0\%$, $p=0.26$). On which backend (Modal TRL or Tinker) and for how many steps was this 5-seed experiment run, and why is it absent from Table 2, Table 5, and Table 8?
2. **How do the rows in Table 8 sum to 79 runs?** Summing the `N` column in Table 8 gives $5+5+5+5+12+4 = 36$ (Tier A) $+ 26$ (Tier B) $+ 13$ (Tier C, 8 of which have 0 steps and 0 seeds) $= 75$ (or 67 actually started runs). How is the headline number of "79 runs" in the Abstract and Section 1 computed?
3. **Which RL libraries were actually executed?** What runs correspond to PufferLib, NVIDIA `rl_games`, and `d3rlpy` (claimed in Section 3), and why are CleanRL, SB3, and Tianshou listed as running "PPO (synthetic math)" when SB3, CleanRL, and Tianshou are standardly classic tabular/continuous-control RL libraries rather than LLM RL post-training frameworks?
4. **What is the actual data in Figure 2 and Table 10?** Why does Figure 2 show plots for `n=600` per-problem ZVF and `Qwen2.5-0.5B/arith.` while its caption describes $N=15$ pooled cross-task scatter and model-family breakdowns? And where did the `FALLBACK_ROWS` numbers in Table 10 come from if no $G \times T$ sweep was executed?
