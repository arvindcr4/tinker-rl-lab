This source-only review revision dated 18 October 2026 responds to three critiques covering the opening chapters, implementation/results, and campaign/synthesis. No new model training, benchmark execution, paid grading, or replacement of stored experimental results was performed.

The full point-by-point response accompanies this copy.

Citations and questioned model/package identities were checked against primary sources as of 18 October 2026; public existence does not authenticate a historical execution.

This is an unsigned draft for the author's review. It does not certify institutional approval, signature, authorship verification, or completeness of the AI-use declaration. The public derivative retains its privacy redactions and does not silently restore private provider exports. Historical released PDFs remain unchanged. The source package and build manifest distinguish newly generated report files from original experimental records; hashes demonstrate byte identity, not execution authenticity.

The C1 extension preserves its frozen 11/64 parser endpoint and three conservatively corroborated numerical witnesses on the same denominator. Reference errors, ambiguities and unit mismatches limit interpretation. Historical V2, C1, and later parser candidates remain separate measurements.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\thispagestyle{plain}

This report studies group-relative reinforcement-learning post-training of
large language models through an explicit record of the training and
evaluation stack. Controlled comparisons seek to isolate the factor under
test; remaining differences and missing evidence limit attribution.
It makes four contributions.

\textbf{(1) A recomputable signal-starvation diagnostic and a loss-form
audit.} The Zero-Variance Fraction (ZVF) is logged per step and recomputed
from stored reward tensors, with a closed-form Bernoulli null. Reading the
runners' code distinguishes custom REINFORCE group-mean loops from clipped
surrogates, nonzero-KL configuration records and unknown managed settings.

\textbf{(2) Stack-conditioned reporting.} A minimum reporting standard and a
machine-readable stack registry with a \texttt{stackdiff} tool that flags
label-flip risk between two runs from their records alone.

\textbf{(3) Evidence triage and bounded empirical findings.} The contaminated, saturated addition comparisons are excluded as estimator evidence. Five interrupted cross-scale anchors cannot identify an asymptotic scaling law. Synthetic and script-imputed records do not validate ZVF prediction. The unsaturated GSM8K v1 comparison couples a 200-token cap, a cold critic and unequal prompt exposure; its contrast is not an estimator effect. The existing provisional v2 snapshot uses a 512-token cap and warm-started critic, with GRPO minus PPO $+0.0076$ (95\% interval $[-0.0034,+0.0185]$) over seven paired seeds. Its interim TOST does not settle the pending exposure-matched comparison. The eight-seed variant audit has no multiplicity-adjusted rejection and fails its locked equivalence criterion. Capped length studies do not adjudicate uncensored verbosity, and adaptive group-size benefit remains unestablished.

\textbf{(4) An infrastructure and harness audit.} A lightly trained Qwen3.6-35B-A3B LoRA checkpoint (40 recorded steps) is evaluated across fourteen suites using scope-specific graders and receipts. Lost training-source and consumed-row bindings prevent verified held-out or end-to-end reproducibility claims. Thirty-five logged near-zero-loss steps do not establish a count of nonzero-gradient updates. The audit records serving, extraction, truncation and provenance defects; original contracts, exploratory replacement runs and small-scale paired reruns remain separate. The paired trained-versus-base comparisons are inconclusive. No campaign-level capability improvement is claimed.

\textbf{C1 diagnostic extension.} Benchmark reference errors, ambiguous wording and equivalent units limit the interpretation of a new frozen-policy study on public GSM8K train. The first 64 of 87 eligible questions from 4,096 screened questions received 24 fresh responses after eight valid, uncapped parser-zero responses. The frozen endpoint is 11/64 (17.19\%; Wilson 95\% interval 9.88--28.21\%). Assistant-assisted reconciliation corroborates three numerical witnesses on the same denominator, while preserving convention-dependent and ambiguous cases and one wrong-reference match. This is conditional diagnostic evidence, not a population estimate, reasoning validation or training gain. A separate, undeployed parser candidate preserves 2,025 previously valid parse objects and admits four unambiguous extractions in a predefined future subset of 2,048 outputs. It does not alter C1's frozen parser-v2 score.
