This is a distinct scientific-only public revision. The frozen results
and review judgments are unchanged. The public document does not provide
full execution provenance. Public records include the selected-case
classifications, event-response checks, scientific content hashes and
generation settings; they are not sufficient to independently
reconstruct or authenticate the full execution history. The empirical
and review claims below must be read with this source-availability
limitation.

Benchmark reference errors, ambiguous questions and equivalent units
limit what this diagnostic can establish. Its frozen parser-defined
result is \textbf{11/64 questions (17.19\%; Wilson 95\% interval
9.88-28.21\%)}. Assistant-assisted reconciliation corroborates
\textbf{three numeric recovery witnesses on the same 64-question
denominator}. This 3/64 count (4.69\%) describes the support found under
a conservative review rule; it does not replace the frozen endpoint or
estimate a population recovery rate. The other eight parser events
include three convention-dependent interpretations, four materially
ambiguous cases and one match to an incorrect reference answer.

The study contains no training update. It does not measure useful
reinforcement-learning gradients, reasoning improvement, controller
superiority, held-out accuracy or general model capability. The prompts
are public GSM8K training questions and are fresh only to this campaign.
Historical V2 evidence remains separate and unpooled.

\section{Frozen design and selection}\label{frozen-design-and-selection}

C1 completed 4,096 fixed-order screening questions with eight
independently seeded responses per question. Eligibility required all
eight outputs to be parseable, uncapped and assigned zero exact-match
reward by frozen parser v2. After the full screen, the first 64 of 87
eligible questions received 24 additional responses each. Selection was
truncated deterministically; the 23 other eligible questions were not
followed up. The primary endpoint is whether at least one of the 24
fresh responses receives parser-defined exact-match reward one. No
question was removed or relabeled after review.

The new workload contains 32,768 screening responses and 1,536 fresh
follow-up responses, totaling 34,304 new draws. A disjoint historical
smoke cohort contributes 1,024 retained draws imported once, giving
35,328 recorded unique draws. The historical smoke is a separate
quality-control cohort, not part of the primary denominator. The 512
initial responses for the selected 64 questions are already included
within screening, and must not be counted again.

The model and tokenizer were Qwen/Qwen3-8B at revision
\longtok{b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218}, with BF16 and
thinking disabled. The dataset was openai/gsm8k, main configuration,
train split, revision \longtok{740312add88f781978c0658806c59bc2815b9866}.
Generation used temperature 0.7, top-p 0.8, top-k 20, min-p 0, a
1,024-token generation cap and 4,096-token context. The recorded
execution used one NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB, vLLM 0.11.0, PyTorch 2.8.0 and
Transformers 4.57.1, with eager execution enabled and prefix caching
disabled. Fixed generation seeds do not imply bitwise reproducibility
across hardware or execution environments.

The ordering seed was 20261002. Questions were ordered by ascending
SHA-256 of the seed, the literal \texttt{order} and the SHA-256 of each
whitespace-normalized question. The seed scheme used affine multiplier
15485863 modulo 2147483648 and generation-slot offset 262144. Follow-up
slots use the original screening-cohort index, not the selected
position. Exact initial and follow-up seed lists for all 64 selected
cases are provided with the public method record {[}P2{]}.

Screening had 504 parser failures and 19 cap hits among 32,768 outputs.
By design the selected initial groups had neither. Follow-up had ten
parser failures and four cap hits among 1,536 outputs. Those quality
counts do not certify semantic correctness. The primary denominator
remains 64 regardless of later quality flags. Descriptive prefixes of
eight, sixteen and twenty-four fresh draws yield 9/64, 10/64 and 11/64
parser events. They are nested observations, not independent comparisons
or evidence of an optimal rollout budget.

The Wilson interval uses a question-level binomial working model within
the selected cohort. It is not design-based or population coverage: both
outcome-dependent eligibility and fixed-order truncation restrict the
estimand. The observed 87/4,096 eligibility count similarly describes
this fixed screen, not benchmark-wide mathematical failure prevalence.

\section{Review design and coverage}\label{review-design-and-coverage}

The first review solved the questions before comparing reference
answers, then inspected the required model outputs. A second reference
review was separately masked from the first judgments and model outputs.
Final reconciliation compared the exact question, reference and
annotations, retaining disagreements rather than forcing consensus. All
stages were assistant-assisted. Separate masking and repeat review do
not provide independent human validation or rule out shared model
biases.

The first output audit covers all 64 cases, all 512 initial responses
and 143 required fresh responses: 655 responses in total, including all
38 primary-success outputs. The final mechanical coverage receipt
verifies those identities and stored logic; it is not an independent
mathematical adjudication. Six initially missing reasoning-caveat fields
were clarified in a separately attributed supplement without altering
the original fields, scores or verdicts. No coverage or annotation
defect remains unresolved in the final receipt.

For the eleven event questions, reconciliation read all 88 initial
outputs and all 38 primary-success fresh outputs in full. All 126
explicit final-number extractions are faithful and uncapped. Two
unit-sensitive non-events add sixteen inspected initial outputs. This is
not a claim to have validated reasoning in every one of the 1,536 fresh
responses. The first audit identified five frozen-v2 extraction false
negatives and no extraction false positives within its reviewed set;
these selected-case observations do not establish population parser
precision.

The conservative witness rule requires a reference value supported in
both rounds without material ambiguity, eight faithful uncapped initial
claims independently judged wrong, and at least one faithful uncapped
fresh primary-success claim independently judged numerically correct.
Sound final arithmetic and sound visible reasoning are recorded
separately. No post-review filtering changes the 64-question
denominator.

because every physical assumption is unstated; genuinely different
quantities or plausible readings are described case by case. None of
these audit counts estimates whole-GSM8K error prevalence because the
cohort was selected for eight parser-zero outputs and then truncated.

unit-ambiguous and one incorrectly calls 200 the number of individual
plugs. Neither case supports a claim of eight genuine mathematical
errors. The frozen scalar references and rewards remain unchanged.

\section{Separate prospective parser
validation}\label{separate-prospective-parser-validation}

The undeployed parser v3 candidate was frozen at 02:43:30 UTC on 3
October 2026, before the earliest target batch at 02:59:39 UTC. The
fixed target was the last 256 screening questions, eight responses each,
yielding 2,048 future outputs. This was a predefined future subset of an
ongoing study, not preregistration before the entire C1 campaign.
Historical candidate tuning is separate. C1's primary score remains
based on frozen parser v2.

All 2,025 previously valid full parse objects were preserved. Four
previously rejected outputs at four distinct prompts became accepted:
one bold answer heading, two display-box blocks and one hash-prefixed
XML answer block. Two separately blinded, output-only assistant
annotations agreed on the same unambiguous scalar in all four changed
cases. No changed acceptance was ambiguous or incorrectly extracted
under those reviews. Nineteen outputs remained rejected.

This supports only a narrow pass on the four observed extraction
changes. It does not establish semantic precision for the 2,025
unchanged outputs, validate the nineteen rejects, measure task
correctness, or estimate population false-positive rates. The responses
are clustered within prompts. Four examples do not exhaust their wrapper
classes or malformed-input behavior. The candidate remains undeployed
and is not used to recompute C1 recovery.

\section{Conclusion}\label{conclusion}

C1 establishes an auditable frozen scoring result and three conservative
numerical counterexamples to the idea that eight observed wrong answers
force all future answers to be wrong under this checkpoint, task and
decoder. It also demonstrates why selection on exact-match zeros can
enrich reference faults, ambiguity and representation mismatches. The
unchanged 11/64 score and the 3/64 corroborated witness count answer
different questions and must be reported together.

A next experimental step would require a new prospective design with
independently reviewed references, explicit unit rules, random or fully
enumerated follow-up selection, multiple seeds and a matched rollout
budget. Any claim about training utility would additionally require an
actual training intervention and held-out evaluation. Those experiments
were not performed here. Historical V2 findings remain preserved as
separate evidence.
