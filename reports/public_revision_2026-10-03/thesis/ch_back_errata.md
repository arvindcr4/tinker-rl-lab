# Appendix E. Corrections to Internal Source Documents

The numbers in this thesis are drawn from the project's internal source documents: the unpublished P1–P8 reports and their section files, audit summaries and result files. Some of those documents contain errors — a wrong sign, a test applied to the wrong unit of analysis, an interval that does not describe its own estimate, or a label that does not match the rows it covers. The chapters report only the corrected value and mark it "(corrected; see Table E.1)". This appendix records, for each correction, what the source states, what the thesis uses instead, and the basis for the change, so that a reader who opens the source can reconcile it with the thesis. Caveats about the evidence itself, as opposed to errors in a source document, stay in the chapters. Paths are relative to the repository root; "sections/" is `platform_hybrid/paper/sections/` and "results/" is `platform_hybrid/experiments/results/`.

## E.1 Evidence-scope repairs after the 2 October review

The following corrections were made without new model training or regenerated historical receipts.

- **ZVF definition (§§4.3, 6.3.1, 7.1, 7.3).** Exact ties and sample variance ≤ 10⁻⁶ are now separate metrics. On the 600 stored groups, seeded micro-jitter changes exact ties from 95 to zero, but leaves 95 below the declared threshold. The epsilon sweep and source hashes are recorded in `pcd_vs_zvf_tolerance_analysis.json`.
- **Controller interpretation (§7.3).** Zero rescue after plugging p̂ = 0 or 1 into a Bernoulli model, or resampling eight identical rewards, is estimator-imposed. It does not show latent certainty or impossibility of recovery with independent continuations.
- **Loss provenance (§§1.1, 4.2–4.3, 6.3.1).** Zero-KL claims are row-specific. TRL nonzero-KL configuration evidence, adaptive-KL defaults, managed unknowns and actor source-binding gaps remain visible; zero reward-relative advantage does not imply a zero full optimiser update.
- **PPO and group-size scope (§6.5.5; Chapter 9).** The unsaturated rerun is present in the synthesis. Its five-seed PPO comparison is baseline-specific and test-sensitive (paired-t p = 0.016; exact p = 0.0625), with unequal prompt exposure and near-cap lengths. G8 minus G2 remains inconclusive, without equivalence.
- **E11 uncertainty (§8.2; Appendix D).** The point score stays 129/312. Resampling the 156 paired underlying designs gives a 95% percentile interval [34.3%, 48.7%], replacing the iid-312 Wilson interval. The conditional extracted-only score remains descriptive.
- **E4 mechanism (§8.5; Chapter 9).** Missing stops are a verified defect, but the one corrected-path task changes several conditions together and cannot attribute the full 100-trial zero to stops alone.
- **Source access (Appendix B §B.11; recovery update, 3 October).** Six exact originals covering all five previously missing basenames were recovered from the Mac, including both pending-state snapshots, and preserved with hashes in a separate private package. All remain absent from the frozen checkout. Supplementary read-only checks corroborate stored E8/E10 grades and E14 disposition/denominator reporting; they do not rerun scorers or establish content decontamination. Training raw logs and E4 raw rerun evidence remain unresolved. The E14 denominator and E10/E4 attribution repairs above change no scientific counts or existing thesis denominator arithmetic.
- **Fraud side-study intervals (§F.4).** Sensor-minus-full AUC and accuracy intervals are [−0.0320, −0.0174] and [−0.0160, −0.0109], reversing and reordering the source's opposite-direction intervals.
