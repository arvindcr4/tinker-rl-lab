# Appendix G. C1 Evidence and Case Ledger

Benchmark reference errors, ambiguous questions and equivalent units limit what this diagnostic can establish. Its frozen parser-defined result is **11/64 questions (17.19%; Wilson 95% interval 9.88-28.21%)**. Assistant-assisted reconciliation corroborates **three numeric recovery witnesses on the same 64-question denominator**. This 3/64 count (4.69%) describes the support found under a conservative review rule; it does not replace the frozen endpoint or estimate a population recovery rate. The other eight parser events include three convention-dependent interpretations, four materially ambiguous cases and one match to an incorrect reference answer. [S1-S4]

The study contains no training update. It does not measure useful reinforcement-learning gradients, reasoning improvement, controller superiority, held-out accuracy or general model capability. The prompts are public GSM8K training questions and are fresh only to this campaign. Historical V2 evidence remains separate and unpooled.

**C00 Santana presents.** Gold 8 is (4 second-half birthdays+7 Christmas gifts)-3 March gifts. Both reviews support the quantity. Fresh 12 matches 8 through a false 8-0 argument; fresh 15 and 23 give the correct 11-3 calculation.

**C07 Suzanne bread.** 3 slices per meal × 2 weekend meals × 52 weeks/12 slices per loaf = 26. Sharing one slice does not require knowing the number of daughters. All 8 initial 90/91 claims use seven days per week; every fresh 26 uses the correct weekend schedule.

**C08 Citations.** Other citations most naturally means the combined non-parking count: L+L+2(L+L) = 24 gives 4. All initial 6 claims substitute twice one category. First treats that as material ambiguity; second supports 4. Ordinary-reading support is stronger than the per-category alternative, but disagreement remains and this is not added to the corroborated recovery count.

**C19 Pet owners.** Half of pet owners plus disjoint exhaustive dog/cat ownership gives 100; half of all citizens gives 300 under the same partition. Overlap allows other values. The denominator ambiguity is material even if ordinary classroom partition assumptions are granted.

**C20 Raft capacity.** The ordinary exact additive-space model makes 14 jackets occupy 7 person-spaces; 8 jackets reduce 21 by 4, yielding 17. Arbitrary real packing/safety possibilities do not by themselves constitute a defect. First conservatively withheld support; second accepts the conventional model. Retain that disagreement and conditional event support only in the ledger.

**C29 Fundraiser.** Next 4 days naturally names a single comparison period, making 950 the four-day total and shortfall 475 per student. Initial 0 answers insert per day before multiplying by 4; the text does not say each day. Retain the first alternative-scope objection and second conventional support, not a revised recovery estimate.

**C35 Checkpoints.** Interior route checkpoints at mile 1 and mile 25 enclose 24 miles and 3 intervening gaps, hence 8 miles. The closing 2-mile arc is outside those gaps. All 8 initials give 8; all 4 gold matches 6 divide by 4 and are wrong. Screen 1 has an internally contradictory parenthetical despite its correct final 8.

**C37 Walking distance.** Summed person-distance is 5 × 3 × 6+12+6 = 108; shared outings counted once give 36. The aggregate quantity is genuinely different. Initial 0-6 are correct under person-mile reading; initial 7 incorrectly repeats weekly extras and gets 198. Neither gold-matching response proves the 108 answers wrong.

**C38 Hank reading.** 5 × (30+60)+2 × 2 × (30+60) = 810 minutes. Reference final sentence mislabels subtotals but final 810 is correct. Initial 630/690/1350 claims are wrong; fresh 2 visibly supplies the correct 450+360 calculation.

**C47 Cake and ham.** Cake-day overlap with any ham day during the week has probability 3/5 = 60%. A specified/randomly sampled test day has joint probability 3/25 = 12%. Both reviewer rounds retain sampling-unit ambiguity; all 5 fresh 12 matches use the latter event.

**C50 Reggie marbles.** With starting Reggie count R and losses L,90 = R+10 × (9-2 L), so R = 20 L. His friend's 100 does not set R. Multiple feasible starts/losses exist. Gold's net-loss shortcut is invalid; all 3 fresh 1 matches have defective justifications. There is no uniquely correct loss count.
