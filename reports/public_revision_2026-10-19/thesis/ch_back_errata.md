# Appendix E. Corrections to Internal Source Documents

The numbers in this thesis are drawn from the project's internal source documents: the unpublished P1–P8 reports and their section files, audit summaries and result files. Some of those documents contain errors — a wrong sign, a test applied to the wrong unit of analysis, an interval that does not describe its own estimate, or a label that does not match the rows it covers. The chapters report only the corrected value and mark it "(corrected; see Table E.1)". This appendix records, for each correction, what the source states, what the thesis uses instead, and the basis for the change, so that a reader who opens the source can reconcile it with the thesis. Caveats about the evidence itself, as opposed to errors in a source document, stay in the chapters. Paths are relative to the repository root; "sections/" is `platform_hybrid/paper/sections/` and "results/" is `platform_hybrid/experiments/results/`.

## E.3 Adversarial ESA Documentation Review

The later 11 October review corrected reporting errors in prose, source definitions, figures and selected citations. Earlier PDFs and original results are preserved; the separate ledger records exact edits and hashes. No training or historical scorer was rerun.
