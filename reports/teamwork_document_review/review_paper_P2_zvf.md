# Potential Mistakes and Improvements

- **[Critical] Retention of Retracted Mathematical Constructs in Downstream Sections (`zvf_iter46.tex`, `zvf_iter38.tex`)**
  While Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-source-falsification} and the Abstract formally state the retraction of the "anti-herding" divergence $\delta_{\mathrm{div}} = +0.1224$ (explaining that $\delta_i = h_G(k_i/G) - \mathbf{1}\{k_i \in \{0,G\}\} \ge 0$ is an algebraic necessity of the plug-in estimator on mixed groups), multiple downstream sections remain largely unpurged. Specifically, Section~\ref{sec:zvf-isog} (`zvf_iter46.tex`) and Section~\ref{sec:zvf-iso-yield} (`zvf_iter38.tex`) continue to derive "per-prompt Iso-G" allocations, empirical contrastive yield formulas $Y_{\mathrm{emp}}(p_x, G) = 1 - \max(0, p_x^G + (1-p_x)^G - \delta_{\mathrm{div}})$, and report empirical rollout savings ($\Delta G = -2.81$ and $\Delta G = -6.50$) based directly on $\delta_{\mathrm{div}}$. Although these sections are prefaced with brief retraction disclaimers ("Retraction notice (2026-10-16)"), retaining 15+ pages of invalid derivations, figures, and empirical claims severely compromises the document's integrity. These sections should be excised or completely replaced with the corrected dispersion analysis.

- **[Critical] Persistent Sign-Wrong Bernoulli Fallacy in Appendix Formalization (`appendix_zvf_formalization.tex`)**
  In Section~\ref{sec:appendix:zvf-formalization} (lines 113--115), the text asserts: *"The i.i.d.-Bernoulli null is moreover only a coarse level estimate---here it over-predicts mean ZVF ($\approx 0.28$ vs.\ observed $0.16$), the opposite-sign residual to the low-temperature $0.5$B probe."* This statement perpetuates the exact mathematical error that was retracted elsewhere. The figure $\approx 0.28$ is not the true i.i.d. Bernoulli prediction at $\bar{p} = 0.694$ (which is $h_8(0.694) \approx 0.054$). Rather, $0.28$ was obtained by averaging the biased plug-in statistic $h_8(k_i/8)$ over the 600 groups ($0.1583 + 0.1224 = 0.2807$). Stating in the formal appendix that the Bernoulli null over-predicts ZVF directly contradicts the corrected Jensen's inequality finding ($\mathbb{E}[h_G(p_i)] \ge h_G(\bar{p})$) and must be corrected.

- **[Major] Internal Contradiction Regarding Correlation in Scaling Couplings (`zvf_scaling.tex`)**
  In Section~\ref{sec:zvf-scaling}, Table~\ref{tab:zvf-scaling-tests} reports test T1 as:
  $$\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\text{frac\_below\_0p1}, \text{is\_collapse}) = +0.791.$$
  However, in the discussion paragraph immediately below the table (line 59), the text asserts:
  *"`$\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\text{frac\_below\_0p1}, \text{is\_collapse}) = +1.0$ by construction ($n=5$, one collapse)`"*.
  This is a direct mathematical contradiction. The true rank correlation is $+0.791$ (not $+1.0$) because Qwen3-8B has $\text{frac\_below\_0p1} = 0.1333 > 0.0$ while its label is `is_collapse = no`. The text must be reconciled with the table.

- **[Major] Heavy Reliance on Synthetic Simulation Projections Presented as Empirical Benchmark Deployments**
  Throughout Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-library} (Table~\ref{tab:zvf-by-library}), Section~\ref{sec:zvf-iter22} (Table~\ref{sec:zvf-iter22-bs}), and Section~\ref{sec:zvf-iter30}, extensive cross-library comparative rankings across 9 mitigation algorithms (GRPO, AERO, CPPO, NGRPO, SCAFGRPO, MCGRPO, GIFT, AREAL, ES) and narrow bootstrap confidence intervals (e.g. GRPO mean ZVF 95% CI $[0.480, 0.482]$) are derived from `variance_mitigation.tsv`. Although the abstract and one table note acknowledge that this is a "simulation projection", the main text repeatedly analyzes these numbers as if they represent measured real-world library executions. The presentation must clearly demarcate synthetic simulation models from genuine system executions to avoid overclaiming.

- **[Major] Conflicting Failure Taxonomies and Inconsistent State Labels Across Iterations**
  The manuscript exhibits severe label drift between iterations. In Section~\ref{sec:zvf-cross-library} (Table~\ref{tab:zvf-by-library}) and Section~\ref{sec:zvf-iter22}, the variance-mitigation runs are categorized into collapse (GRPO 3/5), drift (MCGRPO, GIFT, AREAL, ES 1/5 each), and plateau. In contrast, Section~\ref{sec:zvf-early-identify} (`zvf_iter42.tex`, lines 39--43) asserts that across the exact same 45 runs, the observed labels are *"exclusively plateau ($n=45$, all nine variance-mitigation libraries)... no run collapsed or drifted."* This discrepancy arises because `iter42` applied a hierarchical rule testing `peak < 0.5` prior to testing for drift, arbitrarily reclassifying all sub-0.5 drift runs as plateau. A single unified taxonomy must be enforced across all sections.

- **[Major] Factual Violation of Monotonicity in Lagged Correlation Table (`zvf_iter50.tex`)**
  In Section~\ref{sec:zvf-lag}, Table~\ref{tab:zvf-lag-summary} evaluates the lag correlation profile $r(\mathrm{reward}_t, \mathrm{ZVF}_{t+L})$. For SCAF-GRPO, the reported correlations are $r(0) = 0.813, r(+1) = 0.807, r(+5) = 0.800, r(+10) = 0.813$. In the rightmost column, the table asserts "monotone? yes", and the text claims the profile is "monotone non-decreasing in $L$ on all nine variance-mitigation libraries". The values for SCAF-GRPO decrease from $0.813$ to $0.800$ before rising, violating monotonicity.

- **[Major] Unsubstantiated Causal Framing for Signed ZVF Decompositions (`zvf_iter58.tex`)**
  Section~\ref{sec:zvf-signed} presents a valuable decomposition of raw ZVF into all-wrong starvation ($\mathrm{ZVF}^-$) and all-correct saturation ($\mathrm{ZVF}^+$). However, the text claims that raw ZVF "inverts the health ordering" and asserts that $\mathrm{ZVF}^-$ represents pathological failure while $\mathrm{ZVF}^+$ is benign. While mathematically intuitive, the paper does not empirically validate that a policy with high $\mathrm{ZVF}^+$ retains gradient capacity on harder tasks, nor does it establish that intervening on $\mathrm{ZVF}^-$ prevents downstream collapse. The phrasing should be framed descriptively rather than causally.
